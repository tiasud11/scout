This notice also explains your rights under applicable data protection laws including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (“UK GDPR”), the Data Protection Act 2018, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (“EU GDPR”) and equivalent laws where applicable. Controller of your personal data A “controller” is the person or organisation that determines the purposes and means of processing personal data. Unless we tell you otherwise Trackr is the controller of your personal data for the purposes described in this notice. There may be circumstances in which Trackr processes information obtained through Trackr ATS for its own purposes for example to provide, maintain and secure our platform, maintain records, respond to enquiries, prevent misuse or meet legal obligations. In those circumstances, Trackr will be a controller for that processing. Where you apply for a role using Trackr ATS, the employer advertising the role will also be the controller of personal data processed for the purpose of considering and managing your application. Scope This privacy notice applies to the processing of personal data in connection with: Students and candidates: people browsing opportunities, creating Trackr accounts, using Trackr services or applying for roles. Trackr Premium users: people subscribing to our job-alert notification services. CV review and Trackr Academy users: people purchasing or participating in our CV review, training, lecture or seminar services. Employers and business users: representatives of employers and organisations or other recruitment service providers. Suppliers and service providers: individuals working for or representing organisations that provide goods or services to us. Website visitors: anyone browsing our website or interacting with our online content.
