CIBC Capital Markets London – Investment Banking Summer Analyst Programme CIBC is a leading Canadian-based financial institution with a market capitalisation of more than CA$140 billion. Through our three major business units - Retail and Business Banking, Wealth Management and Capital Markets – 50,000 employees provide a full range of financial products and services to 15 million individual, small business, commercial, corporate and institutional clients in Canada, the U.S. and around the world. CIBC Capital Markets is a leading provider of M&A advisory, debt and equity financing, corporate banking, trading, hedging and research to corporate, government and institutional clients around the world. As an Investment Banking Summer Analyst you will have the opportunity to: Participate in an orientation session to learn more about the bank and our Capital Markets business here in the region Work within deal teams on live transactions, gaining experience across different products, industries and clients Assist in idea generation, financial and investment analysis, preparation of pitches, client materials, and sector analysis Work on specific projects across various industries throughout the origination and execution phase to the asset management Attend and contribute to team meetings.
