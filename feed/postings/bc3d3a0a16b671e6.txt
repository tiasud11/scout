Title: 2027 UK Treasury Summer Internship Requisition ID: 278815 Join a purpose driven winning team, committed to results, in an inclusive and high-performing culture. Duration: June – end of August 2027 (10 weeks) Location: London, United Kingdom Scotiabank is a leading bank in the Americas. We are powered by our 97,000 high-performing teammates who make a real difference across the globe as a leading provider of advice, products, digital experiences, and financial services. This is a place where you’ll get to learn and develop your skills, while being recognised for your hard work. At Scotiabank, we’re passionate about bringing our whole selves to work, allowing us to create inclusive work environments for everyone to enjoy. Our Europe operations are located in London and Dublin and are part of our Global Banking & Markets (GBM) business. GBM provides a full range of investment banking, credit and risk management products and services relevant to the financing and strategic development needs of our clients. Our products include debt and equity financing, mergers & acquisitions, corporate banking, institutional equity sales, trading and research, fixed income products, derivatives, energy, foreign exchange, and precious metals. We also cross-sell the full range of wholesale products and services offered by the Scotiabank Group. Programme Overview: Scotiabank offers an exciting opportunity to join the London-based Group Treasury team of a Canadian Bank commencing in June 2027. This 10-week programme is designed for students in their first year of study (or second year of a four year degree) who are eager to learn and have a passion for Treasury. The programme offers experience across multiple financial market products providing the opportunity to gain insight into the daily workings of the following Treasury areas: Liquidity and Balance Sheet Management -Unsecured funding -Secured funding -Liquidity Portfolio -Management of nostro account and daily cash positions using foreign exchange (FX) spot and forwards positions -Interest rate risk -Corporate lending -Regulatory risk and management Marketing -Sourcing funding with external customers -Internal customers in support of sourcing funding What You Can Expect: In support of Treasury activities, work closely with key internal stakeholders, i.e. Capital Markets Sales & Trading and Debt Capital Markets, Market & Liquidity Risk (TFRM) Management, Finance, Global Transactional Banking Sales and Operations to develop a strong understanding of financial markets and products, with the opportunity to apply knowledge to real world situations Shadowing dealers to learn risk management techniques and develop key deal execution skills Completing projects set by the team which includes preparing market commentary, booking and managing the cycle of corporate loans, understanding the balance sheet and it risks, and other ad hoc tasks Understand how the Bank’s risk appetite and risk culture should be considered in day-to-day activities and decisions Complete an intern project assignment and present to leaders within Group Treasury Programme Highlights: Our internship is designed to give you real insight into life at Scotiabank. Alongside your day-to-day responsibilities, you’ll take part in: A weekly speaker series with leaders from across the Bank Peer to peer intern presentations Social and networking events A CSR project that supports one of our charity partners Who We're Looking For: You are a first-year undergraduate student (or second-year on a four-year degree) You are available for a 10-week internship between mid-June to end of August 2027 You have a demonstrated interest in and knowledge of the financial markets You have strong MS office skills, e.g. advanced MS Excel, and can practically apply these to solve problems You have strong interpersonal skills and the ability to work in a collaborative environment You have the ability to overcome challenges presented by a constantly changing work environment You are eligible to work in the United Kingdom How To Apply: Fill out an application via the Scotiabank careers portal and submit your CV when prompted (please note a cover letter is not required) Successful applicants will be contacted regarding next steps, which will be comprised of two interviews with members of the Treasury team Summer Internship applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.
