We’re waving goodbye to the complicated and confusing ways of traditional banking. After starting as a prepaid card, our product offering has grown a lot in the last 10 years in the UK. As well as personal and business bank accounts, we offer joint accounts , accounts for 16-17 year olds , a free kids account and credit cards in the UK, with more exciting things to come beyond. Our UK customers can also save , invest and combine their pensions with us. With our hot coral cards and get-paid-early feature, combined with financial education on social media and our award winning customer service, we have a long history of creating magical moments for our customers! We’re not only about selling products - we want to solve problems and change lives through Monzo. 📍 London | 💰 £60k – £80k base salary | £100k – £135k OTE + Incentive Awards tied to your performance + Benefits Our Monzo Business team ⭐ Monzo Business helps businesses manage their money, and more than a million businesses (1 in 6!) in the UK bank with us. Now, we’re exploring how we can help more established businesses switch to Monzo, across the UK and Europe 🚀 Many of those businesses have more complex needs. They may need help understanding whether Monzo is right for them, moving their banking or getting the most from our products. A great conversation with the right person can make a real difference. We’ve started building a sales team and testing a more personal approach. Now we’re looking for a Sales Manager to lead a team of Account Executives and help us turn what we’re learning into a repeatable, effective sales motion. This is a chance to build something new. You’ll coach your team, help them deliver great results and use what you see every day to make the way we sell better. You’ll work closely with Revenue Operations (RevOps), Product and other teams to improve our processes and customer journeys. You’ll also help build an AI-first sales motion from the start. We want someone who’s curious about AI and automation, actively experiments with new tools and can help the team spend more time on conversations that matter. 🔑 You’ll play a key role by... Leading a team of Account Executives. Manage a pod of 6–8 people and be accountable for its performance, from reaching the right businesses and converting opportunities to customer activation and quality. Translate the wider Business strategy and set clear expectations, understand what’s driving results and act when performance needs to improve. Coaching and developing your team. Spend time listening to calls, reviewing opportunities and giving clear, regular feedback. Help each person build their skills and confidence, recognise great work and address underperformance fairly. Help hire and onboard new team members as we grow. Building the sales motion. Turn what you and your team learn into better ways of working. Spot friction in lead handling, follow-up, handovers and the sales process; test improvements, measure their impact and share what works so we can build a repeatable playbook. Staying close to customers. We expect Sales Managers to also take a few sales calls each week and make a few sales a month. Use what you learn from customers and the sales journey to coach the team, identify product needs and improve the motion. Most of your time will be spent leading the team and improving how we work. Helping shape our Products. Share what you learn from customers with Product to help shape our proposition and priorities, and use what we learn to improve how we sell. Putting AI and automation to work. Experiment with tools, agents and workflows that help the team research prospects, prepare for conversations, follow up or learn from calls. Work with specialist teams to test ideas responsibly, understand their impact and help the team adopt what works. Making good decisions with data. Use pipeline, conversion, activation, productivity and quality data to understand where the team is doing well and where customers or opportunities are getting stuck. Keep the pipeline accurate and work with RevOps on reporting and forecasting. Working as one team. Bring frontline insight to RevOps, Product, Marketing and Operations. Help shape the tools, processes and customer journeys Sales needs, and work together to solve problems that affect your team and customers. Earning customers’ trust. Build a culture that puts customers first. Coach your team to understand their needs, represent Monzo accurately and deliver good outcomes. Raise concerns early and work with Risk, Compliance and Legal when needed. 🤩 We’d love to hear from you if… You’ve managed and developed a sales team. You can share examples of how you’ve coached individuals, given feedback and improved performance, including when conversations were difficult. You understand selling to small and medium-sized businesses in a relatively high-volume environment. You can balance an efficient process with thoughtful, customer-focused conversations. You’re a builder. You’ve spotted a problem in how a team sells, tried a better approach and helped people put it into practice. You’re comfortable making progress when the playbook is still being written. You’ve actively experimented with AI or automation at work and are keen to explore what agents and new workflows can do. You can tell us what you tried, the problem you were solving and what you learned. You’re happy to stay hands-on with customers. You enjoy sales conversations yourself and use what you learn to help others improve. You use data to understand performance. You can look at a funnel or pipeline, ask good questions and turn the answers into practical action. You work well with people in different teams. You can explain what your team and customers need, share evidence and help colleagues find solutions together. You care about doing the right thing for customers. You’re comfortable working within clear standards and learning what it takes to sell in a regulated environment. You might have built your experience in financial services, technology or another business selling to businesses.
