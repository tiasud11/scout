You’ll also help refine our operations strategy and optimise key processes to help us safely scale our products and features to millions of customers. You won't have a team to run day-to-day; instead you'll lead through influence, structure and expertise - bringing order to complex, cross-functional planning cycles, taking non-technical delivery work off engineers' plates, and being accountable for the success metrics in your area. You'll do this across two big strategic bets: going deep in the UK market and expanding our products into Europe, all while we scale to millions more customers. You will be able to leverage our established and award-winning customer service operations whilst also working closely with the Product, Engineering, Partnerships and Risk & Compliance teams to deliver a delightful experience for our users. In time, your remit may also expand to cover other products. Develop and implement operational strategies to enhance the growth of Monzo's Wealth business Drive high-impact initiatives in your area, defining how we implement compliant, scalable processes that are built for efficiency. Navigate ambiguity to bring structure to complex cross-functional planning cycles — including the operational readiness required for EU market expansion and deeper UK propositions. Ensure efficient, compliant, and seamless delivery of operations, serving as the key contact for escalations Drive best practices within the team for all internal operations, both supplier-facing and customer-facing Work with product managers, engineers, and designers to enhance product and customer experiences Monitor key performance and risk indicators to improve customer experience and operational performance, and manage regulatory compliance with Risk & Governance Oversee third-party provider effectiveness and collaborate with the Commercial & Partnerships Manager to optimise partnerships and operations Ad hoc project manage the implementation of new initiatives, such as supplier integrations, where appropriate Identify where digital tools and new technologies can make us more effective, and put them to work.
