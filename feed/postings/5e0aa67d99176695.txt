If you answered “YES,” we’ve got something special for you: Join us as a Quantitative Research/Trading Intern on-site in Bratislava, Slovakia. We support European and UK citizens with accommodation & travel arrangements. You’ll dive into the exciting world of cryptocurrency trading, gain hands-on experience in quantitative analysis, algorithm development, and market research. As a Quant Intern, you’ll have the opportunity to contribute to real projects, learn from experienced quant traders, and gain practical experience to get a head start on your trading career. Working on projects alongside mentors will give you a sense of the variety of problems we solve every day. Throughout the summer, you’ll also participate in simulated interactive mock trading sessions. Apply today to join our Quantitative Research/Trading team. About the Quant Research/Trading team Our trading team digs into fresh trading ideas and tweaks the ones we already have. Most of our trading is fully automated, but we’re not afraid to mix in some gut feeling or out-of-the-box ideas if they seem promising. A solid trader usually rocks a mix of strong technical skills (think stats and basic coding) and a good feel for probabilities and expected values. What will you do in the Quant Research/Trading team We’ve structured the program to be both fun and challenging, with a typical day including: Morning betting games to get the day started. Discussions on trading-related topics to deepen your understanding of market dynamics. Work on practical exercises that mirror actual quant and trading tasks. Time to explore your own ideas and projects, supported by our experienced team. Play various games to enhance your trading intuition. On top of that, you will: Work with Python, Pandas, SQL, and Grafana for data analysis and real-time monitoring. Learn trading fundamentals from experienced quants and traders. Collaborate in teams to tackle challenging open-ended quant problems suitable for all levels, including beginners. Participate in discussions about the problems to sharpen your argumentation skills. Experience manual trading with substantial, yet manageable positions to grasp market dynamics. What we expect from you Love for games or betting. Basic coding skills in Python or similar languages, with a willingness to learn and adapt to new technologies. Strong communication and collaboration skills. Curiosity and enthusiasm for cryptocurrency markets, with a desire to learn and grow in a fast-paced environment. On-site in Slovakia – RELOCATION PROVIDED This internship opportunity is on-site in Bratislava, Slovakia, with relocation provided. Internships are NOT open to remote work. Interview process Online Math test: Low-to-medium complexity problems focused on probability, logic, and mathematical skills. This is a take-home challenge that you can do anytime in your free time. Quant Interview 45min: A set of medium complexity problems to assess your intuition and problem-solving approach. Quant Interview 90min: General discussion about your background and passions, followed by medium-hard complexity problems to assess your intuition and problem-solving approach. On-site Interview: Engage in a set of betting games to evaluate your quantitative reasoning and decision-making skills. You’ll conduct the final rounds on-site in Bratislava, Slovakia. All costs are covered and you’ll get to know the team, city, and our venues. Travel, Accommodation, and Visa Support: We’ll help you cover the costs of joining us on-site. Potential for Full-Time Transition: Exceptional interns may be considered for full-time positions based on their performance and our business needs. Commitment: Full-time commitment for the duration of the seven-week summer 2027 internship. On-site: Participation in our offices in Bratislava, Slovakia, is required to facilitate continuous interaction and learning with the team. We will support accommodation arrangements. Onboarding: You will receive comprehensive training and guidance from our team, with introductory tasks designed to help you quickly grasp quantitative trading concepts and methodologies. Why to choose Bratislava over other locations Low living costs: Bratislava offers a significantly lower cost of living compared to most major European cities (source: Numbeo). Low income tax: Slovakia offers only 25% personal income tax, compared to 55% Austria, 50% Belgium, 49% Netherlands, 48% Portugal, 47% Spain, 45% UK, France, and Germany, 44% Greece, and 40% Ireland according to taxsummaries.pwc.com . Regional hub: Bratislava lies at the intersection of Austria (only 80km from Vienna), Czechia (130km from Brno), and Hungary (200km from Budapest). Safety: Bratislava is one of the safest capitals in Europe, providing peace of mind that is harder to find in larger cities with higher crime rates, such as Paris, London, or Brussels. High quality of life: Bratislava offers a balanced lifestyle with access to nature, excellent public amenities, and shorter commutes, unlike many larger cities where high living costs often compromise quality of life. Booming economy and opportunities: Bratislava is a growing economic hub with a focus on technology, finance, and innovation, attracting international companies and startups. It begins in June and runs through the end of July, taking place fully on-site at our offices. This timeline is designed to give you enough time to work on meaningful projects while still fitting into a summer break. Do I need to have prior trading experience? No, we do not require previous trading experience. Our internship program is designed to teach you the fundamentals of trading and provide hands-on exposure to how markets work. What we value most are strong analytical skills, curiosity, and a willingness to learn. Many of our successful interns come from backgrounds in mathematics, computer science, or engineering, with no direct trading experience before joining. Do you provide relocation assistance? Yes. We provide comprehensive relocation support to make your transition as smooth as possible. This includes covering your travel expenses and arranging fully paid accommodation for the duration of your internship. Our team will also guide you through the logistics of moving, so you can focus on learning and getting the most out of your experience. Do you provide accommodation? Yes. We provide fully covered accommodation for the entire duration of your internship. Housing is arranged by our team so you don’t need to worry about finding a place yourself, allowing you to focus fully on the program. Will I work on real trading projects? Yes. As an intern, you’ll contribute directly to projects that have real impact on our trading strategies and operations. You’ll work alongside experienced traders and engineers, gaining hands-on experience with the same tools and data we use every day. Your work won’t be limited to simulations, you’ll see how your contributions fit into live projects and decision-making. Is this internship remote? No. The internship is fully on-site in Bratislava. We believe that being physically present allows interns to learn faster, collaborate closely with traders and engineers, and experience our team culture firsthand. This setup ensures you gain the most from mentorship and real-time feedback. Can this lead to a full-time offer? Yes. High-performing interns may receive a return offer to join us full-time after graduation. Each year, we extend offers to some of our interns who demonstrate strong skills, curiosity, and cultural fit. The internship is designed to give you a real sense of what a long-term career with us could look like, and many of our current team members started as interns. How do return offers work? Return offers are extended to interns who demonstrate exceptional performance, strong problem-solving ability, and a good cultural fit during the program. Depending on your strengths and interests, you may receive an offer to join one of several paths—such as trading, quantitative research, or DeFi. The area of the offer is determined by the skills you showcase during the internship and where we see the best long-term match. What’s the interview process like? Our interview process is designed to test your problem-solving ability rather than prior experience in trading. We focus on probability, statistics, logical reasoning, and intuition, as well as your ability to think under uncertainty. The process begins with an online assessment. Successful candidates are then invited to two technical rounds covering probability and statistics. The final stage is an on-site session in Bratislava, where you’ll participate in interactive betting games that simulate real decision-making scenarios. For a detailed breakdown, please check our interview process. How long do I need to wait for your response? We aim to get back to all applicants within one week of receiving the initial application.
