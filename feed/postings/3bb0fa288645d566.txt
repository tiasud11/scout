Title: 2027 UK Global Transaction Banking Summer Internship Requisition ID: 278819 Join a purpose driven winning team, committed to results, in an inclusive and high-performing culture. Duration: June – end of August 2027 (10 weeks) Location: London, United Kingdom Scotiabank is a leading bank in the Americas. We are powered by our 97,000 high-performing teammates who make a real difference across the globe as a leading provider of advice, products, digital experiences, and financial services. This is a place where you’ll get to learn and develop your skills, while being recognised for your hard work. At Scotiabank, we’re passionate about bringing our whole selves to work, allowing us to create inclusive work environments for everyone to enjoy. Our Europe operations are located in London and Dublin and are part of our Global Banking & Markets (GBM) business. GBM provides a full range of investment banking, credit and risk management products and services relevant to the financing and strategic development needs of our clients. Our products include debt and equity financing, mergers & acquisitions, corporate banking, institutional equity sales, trading and research, fixed income products, derivatives, energy, foreign exchange, and precious metals. We also cross-sell the full range of wholesale products and services offered by the Scotiabank Group. Programme Overview: Scotiabank offers an exciting opportunity to join the London-based Global Transaction Banking team of a Canadian Bank commencing in June 2027. This 10-week programme is designed for students in their first year of study (or second year of a four-year degree) who are eager to learn, curious about global banking and financial markets, and interested in how banks support the day-to-day financial needs of Corporate and Financial Institution clients. Global Transaction Banking provides solutions across cash management, payments, liquidity, trade finance and working capital. Working alongside relationship managers, product specialists and teams across Scotiabank's global network, you will gain exposure to both Corporate and Financial Institution clients. As an intern, you will gain practical experience in a dynamic and supportive environment, developing your knowledge of transaction banking, analytical skills and understanding of client relationship management. You will gain exposure to both Corporate and Financial Institution clients, providing a broad understanding of how GTB solutions are delivered across different client segments. What You Can Expect: During the programme, you will: Support GTB relationship and product teams in the coverage and servicing of Corporate and Financial Institution clients Develop an understanding of GTB solutions, including cash management, payments, trade finance, working capital, liquidity and deposits, and correspondent banking Support live client opportunities and transactions through research, analysis, preparation of client materials and coordination with internal stakeholders Prepare client presentations, meeting and briefing materials, and support follow-up actions from client discussions Gain exposure to trade finance transactions and develop an understanding of how clients manage cash flows, liquidity and domestic and cross-border payments Support the monitoring and analysis of client portfolios, business opportunities and transaction activity Collaborate with teams across Scotiabank, including Corporate Banking, Financial Institutions, Product, Credit, Risk, Operations and Compliance Develop an understanding of Scotiabank's risk appetite, risk culture and regulatory responsibilities, including KYC and AML/ATF Build commercial awareness by keeping up to date with relevant market and transaction banking developments Complete an intern project assignment and present to leaders within Global Transaction Banking Programme Highlights: Our internship is designed to give you real insight into life at Scotiabank. Alongside your day-to-day responsibilities, you’ll take part in: A weekly speaker series with leaders from across the Bank Peer to peer intern presentations Social and networking events A CSR project that supports one of our charity partners Who We're Looking For: You are a first-year undergraduate student (or second-year on a four-year degree) You are available for a 10-week internship between mid-June to end of August 2027 You possess a strong interest in finance, transaction banking and global financial markets You are able to demonstrate strong commercial awareness, curiosity and a proactive approach to learning You have strong analytical, organisational and problem-solving skills with attention to detail You have strong interpersonal skills and the ability to work in a collaborative environment You are proficient in Excel and PowerPoint You have excellent written and verbal communication skills and the ability to work effectively in a collaborative environment You are eligible to work in the United Kingdom How To Apply: Fill out an application via the Scotiabank careers portal and submit your CV when prompted (please note a cover letter is not required) Successful applicants will be contacted regarding next steps, which will be comprised of two interviews with members of the Global Transaction Banking team Summer Internship applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.
