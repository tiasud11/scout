Begin your career journey in Risk & Compliance: Join M&G’s eight-week Summer Internship Join us on 28 June 2027 for an eight week internship to gain hands-on experience, develop technical and professional skills, and build a strong understanding of Risk & Compliance. Your journey will start with a cohort-wide induction, with focused insight sessions to help you learn more about M&G, network with colleagues, and begin developing your professional skills. You will take part in a structured programme, including: Team Placement: Join one team for eight weeks where you will take on meaningful work, contribute to live projects, and shadow experienced colleagues to gain a solid understanding of Risk and Compliance. Group Project: An opportunity to collaborate with your peers on a live strategic challenge, developing teamwork and problem-solving skills and presenting your recommendations back to the business at the end of the programme. Professional Development: Take part in interactive learning sessions and networking opportunities designed to grow your expertise and build your professional network. Your Support Network: Regular check-ins with the Early Careers Team, your manager, and a buddy will ensure you have all the support you need during the programme. This internship is your first step toward a career in Risk & Compliance. At the end of the programme, you’ll have the opportunity to apply for our Graduate Programme starting in September 2028. What will you be doing? Our Risk & Compliance function plays an important role in helping M&G make good, informed decisions and manage risk effectively. We provide independent oversight and challenge, help the business identify and manage its risks, support a strong control environment and help M&G meet its regulatory obligations. We work in partnership with colleagues across the business to uphold high standards of conduct and deliver good outcomes for our customers. During your internship, you’ll be based in one Risk & Compliance team, giving you the opportunity to develop practical experience and make a meaningful contribution. Depending on your placement, you could be involved in: Supporting the identification and assessment of different types of risk and understanding the controls used to manage them. Contributing to analysis, research and reporting to support Risk & Compliance decision making. Understanding how frameworks, policies and standards help M&G manage risk and meet its regulatory obligations. Supporting business projects and change initiatives, bringing a Risk & Compliance perspective and considering customer outcomes. Using data and technology to identify trends, provide insight and support effective risk management. Attending team meetings and forums, contributing your ideas and having opportunities to present your work to colleagues. Who are we looking for? We’re looking for curious minds and creative problem-solvers who are ready to start their career in our Risk & Compliance teams. If you’re eager to learn, keen to collaborate, and motivated to deliver great outcomes, this is the place for you. Successful candidates will bring: A proactive mindset and eagerness to learn. Analytical thinking and problem-solving skills. Strong attention to detail. Clear communication and collaboration skills. Comfort with technology and digital tools. Ability to build and maintain strong professional relationships across teams and stakeholders We welcome applicants from all degree disciplines and value diverse perspectives and experiences.
