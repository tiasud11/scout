M&G’s strategy is built around three pillars that guide how the business grows and creates value. We are focused on building growth through diversified markets, simplifying and modernising operations, and delivering financial strength through disciplined capital management and sustainable returns. Maintain our financial strength 01 Ensuring our clients can depend on us, while rewarding shareholders. Our Group priorities Continue to shift towards a capital-light model.​ Progress on our target of cumulative Operating Capital Generation (OCG) of £2.7 billion (before new business strain) over 2025-2027.​ Maintain a progressive and sustainable dividend policy. 2025 Group highlights Moved to a progressive dividend policy, and increased Dividend Per Share by 2% to 20.5p for 2025, continuing to deliver attractive returns for shareholders. Generated £928m of operating capital before new business strain in line with our overall target, demonstrating ongoing financial resilience.​ Stable Adjusted Operating Profit year on year but positive underlying momentum. Our strategy Simplify our business 02 Becoming more nimble and efficient in how we work to best serve our customers. Our Group priorities Continue to streamline our business model to enable us to work more effectively across the Group and deliver our growth priorities. Simplify and automate our processes, using technology and AI, to improve efficiency, service and customer experience. Reduce Asset Management Cost-to-Income Ratio to 70%. 2025 Group highlights Completed our Group transformation programme, delivering cost savings of £250 million by the end of 2025 (against our target of £230m by the end of 2025).​ Our transformation programme over the last three years has enabled us to create capacity to invest in growth. Continued to enhance customer journeys, increasing overall satisfaction rates and improving response time. Our strategy Deliver profitable growth 03 Building on our strengths to better anticipate and address our clients’ needs. Our Group priorities Broaden international presence and strengthen our distribution capabilities to enable more customers and advisers to access our solutions.​ Leverage the strength of our business model to develop innovative products and investment solutions to meet evolving customer needs. Grow Adjusted Operating Profit by average >5% annually over 2025-2027.​ 2025 Group highlights Generated £7bn net flows in Asset Management, (56% into private markets), delivered £1.5bn in BPA new business volumes (65% increase on 2024) and had consistent net inflows in the second half of the year. Enhanced our international presence through strategic partnerships with Dai‑ichi Life Holdings and Guotai Haitong Securities in China, and acquisition of P Capital Partners. Continued to broaden our product offering and distribution with 16 new fund launches in Asset Management, integration of PruFund on FNZ technology and the launch of retail fixed term annuity.
