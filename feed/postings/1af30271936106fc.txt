Join us in our Glasgow office for an interactive Spring Insight event designed to give first and second-year university students a unique introduction to Morgan Stanley and the wide range of career opportunities available across our Finance, Risk and Business Services divisions. Throughout the event, you'll hear directly from employees, explore how different teams contribute to the success of the firm, and gain valuable insight into the career pathways available through our internship and graduate programmes. What you can expect Connect with representatives from Finance, Fund Services, Non-Financial Risk and Internal Audit through interactive networking stalls. Learn how these divisions support Morgan Stanley's global business and play a critical role in helping the firm manage risk, serve clients and drive strategic decision-making. Network with professionals at various stages of their careers and gain first-hand insight into life at Morgan Stanley. Develop your understanding of our recruitment process, application tips and the opportunities available through our 2028 programmes. Divisions represented at this event Finance The Finance Division partners with businesses across Morgan Stanley, providing financial oversight, strategic advice and risk management. As the guardian of the firm's books and records, Finance plays a key role in helping the firm make informed decisions and achieve its long-term objectives. Non-Financial Risk The Non-Financial Risk (NFR) organisation brings together Compliance, Global Financial Crimes and Operational Risk teams to provide a comprehensive and independent view of non-financial risks across the firm, helping Morgan Stanley operate with integrity and resilience. Internal Audit Internal Audit provides objective and independent assessments of the firm's governance, risk management and control environment, helping strengthen processes and support sustainable business growth. Fund Services Morgan Stanley Fund Services delivers a comprehensive suite of fund administration solutions to clients around the world. Supported by more than 1,400 professionals across global locations, including Glasgow, the division combines industry expertise with innovative technology to support a diverse range of investment funds.
