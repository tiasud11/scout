Apply here We are an education focussed B-Corp , that provides free, industry-leading training and coaching to graduates, who are then deployed at clients as high performance consultants in technical and non-technical roles. We do this because we have two foundational beliefs: 1. Exceptional talent is missed every day: a lot of people with huge potential struggle to access early-career opportunities and start their careers well. We unashamedly aim high to help our consultants have high trajectory careers. 2. Exceptional training changes outcomes: great education is a game-changer. We invest heavily in training and support to develop the skills, behaviours, and understanding that can amplify the potential of an individual’s career, no matter the degree or technical experience they have. We're proud to say our work has been recognised by the UK data industry as we won both the Best Data Graduate Employer Award and the Best Diversity, Equity and Inclusion (DEI) Initiative Award at the 2024 dataIQ Awards! ‍ What we do Every day we work hard to find people with the potential to succeed. We train them in technical and non-technical functional skills and professional high performance behaviours over 15 weeks to kick-start a successful career, and then support our consultants as they excel with our clients for two years. Our training is gaining a reputation as high-quality and highly supportive. We aim to develop a wide range of skills and the right mindset and approach so that our consultants are more likely to meet any client's demands. Though remote, we maintain engagement through interactive workshops, group projects, multiple communication channels and regular individual coaching. Our low coach-to-learner ratio means questions are answered quickly and time is available to give attention when needed. Our consultants work in a range of industries and functions (mostly typically data, software and consulting), with more than 75% recognised as high performers by our clients. We keep in regular contact with them and continue to provide technical and professional training throughout their deployments. ‍ Why do we focus on tech and data? Not only are these growing industries that will provide our trainees with the opportunity to enjoy long-term career success, they are also exciting industries that are constantly evolving, offering our trainees the chance to have varied careers where they are able to continuously learn and try new things. This is reflected by a 2024 study that found that businesses have recently increased their investment in data analytics by 54%. This is likely due to the ever increasing amount of data that is produced every day. However, this data is often messy and ambiguous, which is why data professionals are in high demand as they have the capabilities to make this data useful and deliver insights that support decision-making at the highest levels. These industries also align to Sigma Labs’ purpose as they are about driving positive change and developing smarter solutions, whether that be making more effective use of precious company data, helping to develop innovative products and apps, or even enhancing customer and public services. But ultimately choosing to focus on these skills/industries allows us to position you at the forefront of a rapidly evolving digital world and provide you with unparalleled access to today's decision-makers and tomorrow's leaders. Our offer isn't an entry level role you're being slotted into, it's a structured pathway into a professional tech career. By the end of our programme, you don't just have a job title, you have a track record. To enable this, our offer is split into two parts: a four month training programme, followed by a two-year deployment as a consultant with one of our brand-name clients. The training programme is designed to equip you with the technical and professional skills needed to excel in our clients’ roles and make meaningful contributions from day one of your career. If you successfully complete the training, we will support you in landing a client role where you will be embedded within their team whilst receiving ongoing coaching, mentoring and support from a dedicated Sigma Labs coach. This ensures you are not thrown in at the deep end as we know starting a career can feel daunting, so your coach will be there to support you through any challenges that might come up throughout your two years with your client. If you continue to grow and perform well during this time, the client may offer you a permanent position once your deployment ends, and this may even come with a promotion which we will support you towards. However, it is entirely up to you what you choose to do after your deployment. Regardless of your choice, we are committed to supporting you with your future career path, and as part of this you will become part of our alumni network which provides you with the opportunity to connect with people who have been through the same journey as you and are building serious careers in tech. We believe this programme is the start of something long-term, not a temporary short-term fix or a work experience programme, and we're committed to making sure it sets you up for whatever journey your career takes you on. The Training Programme Our training programme can be seen as a more practical extension of your academic journey towards becoming a great professional - it’s like a short post-university masters with a supported job search to get you into the working world! But so that you can be absolutely clear on what you are signing up for, our training programme is: Full time - 9am-6pm Monday-Friday. Approximately 15 weeks long - any variation is because we adapt our training to ensure it is relevant to your likely client roles. Fully funded - unlike most university education! Unpaid - except for when we reimburse you for any reasonable travel expenses. Predominantly remote - there are a few exciting in-person days but you will not need to relocate for this portion of the programme. Focused on embedding two practical skill sets - functional (eg: technical) skills and professional high performance. Continuously updated - our coaches are all industry experts and constantly ensure our training is up to date and relevant to the broader industry, as part of this we have recently incorporated training on how to get the most out of AI into our programme. Designed to reduce drop-outs - our programme is challenging by design, but we provide structured support throughout and over 85% of trainees successfully complete the full training programme. ‍ Client Deployment After your training, if you have not already secured a client role, you will enter a transition period that typically lasts about four to eight weeks. During this time, you will continue to solidify your knowledge and prepare for client interviews that we will arrange for you. If you are successful in a client interview and secure a role, you will become a consultant employed by Sigma Labs but will conduct your day-to-day work with the client’s team. You will earn £30,000 in your first year, rising to £38,000 in your second year, and will continue to receive mentoring from Sigma Labs through monthly one-to-one meetings with our expert coaches where you will discuss your development, career progression, and how you're doing. The client role you are deployed into will depend on what’s available when you finish training, and the potential opportunities are varied as our clients span a wide range of industries, roles, and locations across the UK. This is why we require every trainee to be willing to accept any potential client opportunity to ensure we can place you with a client as quickly as possible, and you can find out more about this in the Eligibility tab. To be completely transparent, although the majority do, not everyone who completes the training is successful in landing a client role with us. If this is the case, whilst we continue to set up client interviews for you, we will also support you in finding a job outside of our preferred client list as our ultimate aim is to ensure you get a great role. ‍ Why do we offer training followed by client work? Through our training and deployment model, we are able to upskill individuals and support their progression into roles with companies such as our top-tier client organisations without any financial barriers. However, in order to do this and survive as a business, we need to finance your training (approximately £9,000) and then employ you as a Sigma Labs consultant where you are placed with one of our clients for 2 years, at which point they then provide us with our revenue, allowing us to ensure that our training programme will always be fully funded for our trainees! We aim to be as transparent as possible, and as part of this, all successful offer holders will receive a training contract detailing exactly what we will offer you and the key policies that deter people from taking advantage of our free training with no intention of fully committing to becoming a consultant with us. As such, and because this is a long-term investment for both you and Sigma Labs, we are looking for candidates who are excited about committing to the full ~2.5 year programme, and we commit to supporting you throughout that journey.
