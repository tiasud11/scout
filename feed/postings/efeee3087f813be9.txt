Greenhill Careers siteUK Housing (Mayfair)Greenhill is an independent investment bank focused on M&A, restructuring, and capital advisory services for corporations, financial sponsors, and governments. Founded in 1996 and headquartered in New York, the firm operates globally with offices in North America, Europe, Asia, and Latin America. Greenhill is known for its conflict-free model, avoiding lending or trading activities to offer clients fully independent strategic advice. It has advised on a wide range of high-profile transactions across sectors including financial services, healthcare, infrastructure, and consumer goods. The firm also maintains a strong presence in restructuring advisory, including both creditor and debtor-side mandates. Greenhill’s lean structure and senior-led execution model appeal to clients seeking discretion and deep expertise.
