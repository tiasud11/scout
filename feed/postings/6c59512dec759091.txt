Graduate Management Trainee – Supply Chain (London) London, England, United Kingdom Supply Chain Management Trainee – London, July 2027 Applications close: 20th November Programme start: July 2027 Starting salary: £36,500, rising to £41,500 on completion of the scheme Eligibility: You must be in your final year of study or have graduated within the past two years. Hello, we’re L’Oréal We’re not just building brands; we’re shaping how the world experiences beauty. If you’re curious about how products get from idea to customer and keen to develop your skills, this could be the opportunity for you. A day in the life Based at our UK headquarters in White City, London, you’ll complete three six-month rotations across Demand Planning, Supply Planning and Physical Logistics over an 18-month scheme. You’ll report to a Supply Chain Manager in each rotation and learn how our teams help get products to customers. You could: Help review sales information, market trends and promotions to support sales forecasts. Work with teams involved in product launches to help make sure products are available at the right time. Help monitor stock levels and understand how teams balance product availability with the risk of excess stock. Support distribution activities and work with teams across our logistics network. Take part in projects with teams such as Marketing, Commercial and Customer Care to improve how products move from production to customers. What we’re looking for You don’t need previous Supply Chain experience. We’re looking for potential, curiosity and a willingness to learn. Throughout the recruitment process, we’ll look for examples of how you demonstrate: Ambition: motivation, energy and a desire to grow and succeed. Resilience: the ability to stay composed, learn from setbacks and keep moving forward. Empathy: active listening, respect for different perspectives and a collaborative approach. Judgement: logical thinking and balanced decisions, including in new or complex situations. Learning agility: curiosity, openness to feedback and the ability to adapt and apply what you learn. AI fluency: an openness to using AI tools effectively and responsibly, with an awareness of trustworthy AI principles. You’ll also bring an interest in how products are made and delivered, confidence working with information and numbers, and a practical approach to solving problems. You don’t need to know everything already; you’ll develop your Supply Chain skills during the scheme. Right to work in the UK This scheme leads to a guaranteed operational role upon successful completion. As graduates are retained after the scheme, it’s important that you have the right to work in the UK for the longer term. During the application process, you’ll be asked: We are required by law to verify work authorization for all prospective employees. Please confirm your visa status using the drop down below. I’m a UK or Irish citizen, or hold EU Settled Status, with an indefinite right to work and no sponsorship requirement. I hold EU Pre-Settled Status, which gives me time-limited right to work and may require a status update or future visa. I hold another UK visa that currently gives me the right to work, but may require sponsorship in the future. I do not currently have the right to work in the UK. What’s in it for you?
