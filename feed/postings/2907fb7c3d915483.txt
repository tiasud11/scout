Distinctive outcomes. The behaviours we look for at PwC The PwC Professional. Living our values and playing our part in the delivery of our global strategy. Why behaviours matter at PwC In a rapidly changing world, one thing is becoming increasingly clear; to meet the changing expectations of our clients, our people and society, we must continue to evolve. We need to lead differently, develop new skills and ways of thinking to make this happen. The PwC Professional is designed to help bring this to life, ensuring we’re all living our values and demonstrating the behaviours needed to deliver our global strategy. Focussed on two dimensions, Trusted Leadership and Distinctive Outcomes, the PwC Professional outlines the behaviours that apply to all of us. It recognises that how we deliver is just as important as what we deliver, so that we can be the best at what we do. The behaviours apply across all grades and are key in helping us deliver sustained outcomes. They’re also designed to help our people better understand how our purpose, strategy, and values fit together - where our purpose grounds our existence, our strategy directs our focus and our values (supported by our Code of Conduct) guide our actions. How PwC behaviours shape our people and culture What we look for in early careers candidates Trusted Leadership Distinctive Outcomes How PwC behaviours shape our people and culture It’s about how we inspire, empower and evolve ourselves and each other to demonstrate we’re trusted leaders, and take personal responsibility to champion, build and deliver quality in everything we do to deliver distinctive outcomes. These changes will better develop and equip us with the behaviours we need to reach our full potential. We’ll support each other, embedding these behaviours in our culture so that we can continue building an experience that differentiates us from the rest, for now and for years to come. What we look for in early careers candidates We look for people to join our early careers programmes who have the potential to be great at what they do, and bring our behaviours to life in how they do it. The PwC Professional framework is made up of two core dimensions; Trusted Leadership and Distinctive Outcomes. Trusted Leadership Do you inspire, empower and evolve yourself and others? Inspire - I understand my impact and people want to work with me. Empower - I coach and work side by side with others. Evolve - I grow as a person and guide us into the future. Distinctive Outcomes Do you take personal responsibility to champion, build and deliver quality in everything you do? Champion - I commit to quality, integrity and inclusive behaviour. Build - I apply a business mindset to my work to increase PwC's success. Deliver - I collaborate across PwC to deliver quality and distinctive client experiences. Before applying Before applying for an opportunity at PwC, we suggest you consider each of these behaviours and how you would aspire to bring these to life in your chosen role. You might also be able to think of examples of where you’ve demonstrated these behaviours in your past work experience, or at school, college or university. We encourage you to reflect on these and how they could apply to a career at PwC. During our assessment process, you'll also have the opportunity to go through a series of workplace scenarios. These scenarios are designed to help us understand how you would approach situations you may encounter at PwC, and to help you reflect on whether PwC’s culture and ways of working feels like the kind of environment in which you would thrive. If you’re successful in securing a role with us, you’ll use the PwC Professional behaviours as a guide to underpin your performance in the workplace, as well as setting your expectations of how others will behave. We have a culture of high performance built on exceptional quality, ethical and professional standards. By leading with our purpose, values and behaviours in every interaction, we are inspiring, curious, collaborative and clear. What behaviours does PwC look for in early careers applicants? PwC assesses candidates using the PwC Professional framework, which focuses on Trusted Leadership and Distinctive Outcomes, alongside the behaviours that sit beneath each dimension. These behaviours help us understand not just what you can do, but how you work with others. Are PwC behaviours assessed during the application process? The behaviours are considered throughout the selection process, including assessments and interviews, to understand how you approach challenges, collaborate, and deliver quality outcomes. Do I need work experience to demonstrate PwC behaviours? You can demonstrate these behaviours through examples from education, part-time work, volunteering, or extracurricular activities. What matters is how you reflect on your experiences and what you learned from them. Will the PwC Professional behaviours be used after I join? The PwC Professional framework continues to guide development, performance and expectations once you join, helping you grow throughout your career at PwC. Are the behaviours the same across all early careers programmes? The PwC Professional behaviours apply across all early careers programmes, including work experience, internships, Flying Start and graduate roles, providing a consistent framework for assessment and development.
