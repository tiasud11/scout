Our Insight Programme is your opportunity to experience the fast-paced world of Corporate & Investment Banking first-hand. This three-day, in-person experience is designed for first-year university students (or second year of a four-year degree) who want to explore a future career in Corporate & Investment Banking (CIB). Over three immersive days you’ll: Explore the world of Corporate & Investment Banking and the role it plays in the global economy Develop future-ready skills through practical workshops and business simulations Meet colleagues from across our CIB teams and hear about their career journeys Gain insights into internship opportunities and potential future careers at Standard Chartered Get a fast-track opportunity for our 2028 Summer Internship Programme in London. Programme Dates 6 – 8 April 2027 in person at our London headquarters. What to expect Deep dives into CIB - Learn how we help clients navigate international trade, capital flows and economic growth across more than 50 markets. Meet our business - Gain exposure to teams across Markets, Global Banking, Coverage Banking and Transaction Banking, and learn how they work together to support our clients. Hear from leaders and graduates - Learn from colleagues at different stages of their careers and gain insight into what it's really like to work in CIB. Interactive workshops and simulations – Build commercial awareness, analytical thinking, communication and teamwork skills through practical challenges and real-world scenarios. Job shadowing – Experience the pace and variety of work across Corporate & Investment Banking and explore different career paths. Career coaching - Receive application, CV and interview guidance from our Early Careers team. Skills you’ll build This programme is designed to help you develop the skills that matter for a career in banking: Commercial awareness and curiosity Analytical and problem-solving skills Communication and presentation skills Collaboration and teamwork Adaptability and resilience Confidence to learn, contribute and take ownership. Who can apply? We’re committed to building an inclusive future for banking and welcome applications from students of all backgrounds and identities. We actively encourage applications from groups that are underrepresented in banking, including women and Black Heritage students. Eligibility Any degree discipline First year university students (or second year if on a four-year course) No prior banking or financial services experience required. Who we’re looking for Potential matters more than experience. We value: Curiosity and a desire to learn Strong communication and teamwork skills Motivation, resilience and a willingness to be challenged Interest in business, markets and the global economy A proactive approach to solving problems and developing new skills A commitment to doing the right thing and making a positive impact. Next Steps Perform well during the Spring Insight Programme and you’ll be first in line for our 2028 Summer Internship Programme – your gateway to a graduate role with us. Recruitment Assessments If selected, you’ll receive an invitation to complete an online assessment. Your responses will help us determine your place on the programme. Additional Details The Insight Programme is an unpaid educational workshop; however, travel and accommodation will be reimbursed for students outside of the immediate London area.
