The internship will begin with one week of formal training for all London TD summer analysts that includes orientation and interactive technical training. This training is designed to teach you the building blocks of financial institutions, Excel, and PowerPoint, which will prepare you for the next nine weeks of the internship. Summer Analysts will have the opportunity to engage with bankers at all levels across all teams. Summer Analysts will also be able to participate in social networking events with one-another as well as colleagues across all divisions on an on-going basis to experience our culture and integrate with our Firm.
