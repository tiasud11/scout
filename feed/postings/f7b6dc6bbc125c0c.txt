Program ID 15299 Region Europe, Middle East & Africa Location United Kingdom City Chester Program Firmwide Program type Insight program Entry level Not Applicable Program description 2027 Spring Insight – Operations, Technology & Finance Chester, United Kingdom Discover your future at Bank of America Our newly formatted Spring Insight offers a full day of insight into a specific line of business, giving students the opportunity to learn directly from our people, explore the work we do and build practical career knowledge. Business overview: Our Chester office is home to a diverse range of teams across Finance, Operations and Technology, all playing an important role in supporting the bank and its clients globally. During the insight, you will explore how different teams contribute to the wider organisation, from using technology and data to solve problems, to supporting critical processes and helping the bank operate effectively. You will hear directly from colleagues about the work they do, how teams collaborate, and the different skills and perspectives that contribute to our success. This will give you broad exposure to the variety of work undertaken in Chester, while helping you discover the different career paths and opportunities available across the site. As a Spring Insight participant, you can expect to: Gain focused insight into the selected line of business, its priorities and the career opportunities available Learn about Bank of America’s work, culture and commitment to an inclusive workplace Hear directly from business representatives about their roles, career journeys and day-to-day work Take part in interactive sessions, which may include case studies, simulations or skills-based activities Develop practical professional and commercial skills Network with business representatives, recruiters and fellow students Receive recruitment guidance and tips to help you prepare for future applications and assessments Ask questions and deepen your understanding of the bank and the selected line of business Eligibility Candidates should be pursuing an undergraduate degree at an accredited college or university and expect to graduate in 2028 or 2029. Studying towards any degree subject and on track for a 2:1 or equivalent Available to attend the full event in Chester on 7 April 2027 Meets any local right-to-work, language or programme eligibility requirements stated in the application What we are looking for Strong academic performance together with initiative, self-motivation, teamwork, leadership and problem-solving skills A genuine interest in the selected business area and in financial services or technology, as relevant Curiosity, an inclusive mindset and the confidence to engage in interactive sessions Our recruitment process The application process includes an online assessment. Selected candidates will then be invited to complete a video interview. Candidates may make one application to one EMEA programme per recruitment season.
