Through its branch and subsidiary network, NBC serves clients across North America, Europe, Asia and MENA. NBC’s strategy is to continue developing NBCCM as its European hub for capital markets and financial services.What We’re Looking ForNBCCM’s Mining and Metals Investment Banking team is seeking an Investment Banking Intern to support deal execution and origination activities in London, UK.As an Investment Banking Intern, you will contribute to transaction execution and industry coverage activities while gaining exposure to complex advisory and financing mandates. Your responsibilities will develop throughout the internship as your knowledge and experience grow, providing strong learning opportunities within a specialist, sector-focused franchise.Internship metals sectorSupport senior team members with deal execution, project coordination and client coverage activitiesMaintain financial databases and assist with the preparation of internal and external reportsQualifications and SkillsCurrently pursuing or recently completed an undergraduate or graduate degree in finance, accounting, economics, business, engineering or a related disciplineStrong academic record, with demonstrated analytical and quantitative abilitiesPrevious internship or relevant experience in investment banking, corporate finance, transaction advisory, equity research, valuation methodologies and capital marketsProficiency in Microsoft Excel, PowerPoint and WordExperience with Bloomberg, Capital IQ, FactSet or a similar financial database is an assetStrong attention
