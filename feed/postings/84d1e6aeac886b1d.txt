Our Consulting culture and vision Our culture is something we're proud of. In a world that's constantly changing, we're building a trusted, technology-enabled and outcome-focused consulting business that puts people at the centre of everything we do. As part of our Consulting team, you'll be encouraged to bring your authentic self to work, with the support, opportunities and flexibility to grow, thrive and succeed. You'll build in-demand skills through continuous learning, including AI and technology-enabled development, helping you stay ahead as the world of work evolves. We're committed to creating an inclusive environment where everyone has equal opportunities to develop and progress. We value curiosity, empathy and diverse perspectives because they help us build trusted relationships and deliver value faster for our clients. You'll also be part of a collaborative community, working alongside colleagues from a wide range of specialisms to solve complex challenges for leading organisations. Throughout your career, you'll be supported by dedicated Career and Talent Coaches who will help you develop your strengths, achieve your goals and make a positive impact in an environment where everyone is encouraged to contribute and succeed. How we work Our people’s expertise and skill sets are split across seven areas. Together, we work across a huge breadth of sectors including; Banking and Capital Markets, Insurance & Asset Wealth Management, Government and Public, Industrial and Services, Energy, Utilities and Resources, Retail and Consumer Markets, Technology, Media and Telecommunications and Defence.
