At Vista, we recognize the immense potential of investing in enterprise software—solutions that drive efficiency, resilience and innovation across industries. This white paper, authored by Vista’s Founder, Chairman and CEO Robert F. Smith, outlines why this sector offers compelling opportunities for long-term value creation. Why Enterprise Software? Enterprise software supports mission-critical operations for businesses across sectors—from healthcare and finance to logistics and education. Its integration into daily business functions makes it indispensable, sticky and high-margin, providing a strong case for resilient investment. Key Investment Benefits of Enterprise Software Enterprise software can offer several compelling portfolio benefits for investors, including diversification, stability and resilience.
