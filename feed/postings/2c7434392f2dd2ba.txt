Title: 2027 UK UpLIFTED: Women In Finance Development Programme Requisition ID: 276763 Join a purpose driven winning team, committed to results, in an inclusive and high-performing culture. About Scotiabank Scotiabank is one of the 'Big Five' Canadian banks and is one of the leading foreign banks serving large national and multinational corporations through its Global Banking and Markets (GBM) business line in Europe. Global Banking and Markets provides a full range of credit, risk management and investment banking products and services relevant to the financing and strategic development needs of our clients. Our products include transaction banking (including payments, trade finance and cash management), investment banking, mergers & acquisitions, corporate banking, fixed income and equity underwriting, sales, trading, and research; prime services (prime brokerage and stock lending); foreign exchange sales and trading; commodity derivatives; and collateral management. We also cross-sell the full range of wholesale products and services offered by Scotiabank. About this Programme Explore, develop, and ignite your passion for the world of Finance. Scotiabank’s UpLIFTED Women in Finance Development programme is a selective four-month programme designed for female-identifying, first-year students (or second-years on a four-year degree) who have an interest in finance. Our programme supports and develops women early in their academic career by providing exposure, insights, and opportunities to learn about finance from experienced professionals. UpLIFTED predominantly focuses on upskilling students and increasing knowledge of our Global Capital Markets and Corporate Banking business lines. The development programme will run from the beginning of January 2027 to the end of April 2027. During the programme, participants will be assigned a mentor, have access to targeted workshops to help participants prepare for a career in finance, and have the opportunity to participate in candid discussions about what it means to be a woman in finance. Programme topics may include, but are not limited to: Career Pathways Workshops Leadership Speaker Series Networking and Interview Workshops Technical Skills Workshops Office Visits (location dependent) Participants will also have regular 1-1 sessions with their mentors and Scotiabank career advisors. Programme Eligibility: We are seeking highly motivated individuals who are interested in learning and development opportunities within Global Banking & Markets. To be considered for the UpLIFTED Programme, candidates will need to demonstrate a high level of energy, a keen desire to learn new concepts, and a defined interest in the financial markets. To apply, you must: Be currently enrolled in an undergraduate degree in the UK in any discipline Be a current first-year student on a three-year degree, or second-year student on a four-year degree Self-identify as a woman Have a demonstrated passion for and interest in learning about capital markets/corporate banking and finance Be able to attend regular mentor meetings and programme workshops for the duration of the 4-month programme (Jan - April 2027) Application Process: 1.
