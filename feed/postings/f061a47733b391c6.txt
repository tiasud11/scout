Spring into Deloitte: Finance (Inspiring Women) is for: First year university students enrolled on a three-year undergraduate degree; or First year students enrolled in a four-year undergraduate degree that includes an industrial placement year; or Second year university students enrolled on a four-year degree that does not include an industrial placement year. The programme is particularly suited to students who want to explore a finance-related career within Audit & Assurance or Tax. You’ll learn how our teams help organisations to make sense of financial information, solve complex problems and support client decision-making. You’ll also start to build skills in analysis, problem solving and communication. At Deloitte, we are committed to creating opportunities for all students, for this programme, we particularly encourage applications from women, as part of our wider efforts to improve representation within our industry. What skills and academic qualifications do I need? You’ll need to be on track for a degree classification of 2:1 or above in any discipline. You don’t need to have studied a finance subject; what matters is your interest in understanding how organisations work and your willingness to build new skills. Therefore, you’ll be proactive, curious and keen to learn about finance-related careers in Audit & Assurance or Tax. What will I be doing? Over two days, you'll take part in interactive workshops, and learn about the exciting role that finance professionals play in helping organisations to build trust, manage risks and make informed financial decisions. You’ll explore careers in finance, gaining a better understanding of the work our teams do and the impact they have for clients across a range of industries. Stepping into the role of a Deloitte professional, you'll work in a group to tackle a real-world business challenge and develop a solution, giving you a flavour of the work we do every day across our business. Whether your strengths lie in problem-solving, working with people, analysing information, or generating ideas, there'll be opportunities to play to your strengths and discover where your skills could thrive. At Deloitte, we support equity, equality and inclusion. We know that different perspectives make us stronger, helping us to support our clients, our people and the communities we work with. Our 12 diversity networks bring together colleagues and allies from a wide range of backgrounds, helping everyone feel heard and supported. They include the Black Network, Hindu, Muslim, Sikh, Christian, Jewish, Neurodiversity, Multicultural, Workability, Working Families, Proud at Deloitte, and Gender Balance networks. Through Spring into Deloitte: Finance (Inspiring Women), you’ll have the chance to learn more about our inclusive culture, explore what matters to you, and consider the career path that feels right for you. When and where does Spring into Deloitte: Finance (Inspiring Women) take place? Spring into Deloitte: Finance (Inspiring Women) will take place in Manchester from Tuesday 30 th - Wednesday 31 st March 2027. How could Spring into Deloitte: Finance (Inspiring Women) launch my career? You will leave the programme with plenty of key employability skills which will set you up for success in the next step of your career journey. Spring into Deloitte: Finance (Inspiring Women) can also open doors to future opportunities at the firm, including progression onto our Summer Vacation Scheme or Industrial Placement in either Audit & Assurance or Tax. Please note that once you have chosen a pathway after taking part in the programme, you won’t be able to switch business area or location. Additionally, if you’re on a four-year degree course with a year in industry, you’ll only be eligible to convert to our Industrial Placement programme, not the Summer Vacation Scheme.
