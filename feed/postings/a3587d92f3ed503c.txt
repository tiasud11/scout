Gatekeeping & Process Championing: Act as the "gatekeeper of the possible" during briefing sessions and creative reviews. Traffic Meetings & Stand-ups: Lead daily and weekly traffic status meetings with internal studio teams and project owners to review project progress, monitor deadlines, and clear operational blockers.
