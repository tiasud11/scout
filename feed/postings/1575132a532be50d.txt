Read more Our stories Get in touch Menu Explore LCP Home Experienced hires Life at LCP Our stories Get in touch Find a role Home Find a role Job vacancy Insurance Consulting - Summer Internship 2027 Business area Early Careers Location London Job ID 325062 LCP is an award-winning actuarial and analytics consultancy providing advice on insurance, energy, pensions, investments, employee benefits and health. We fuse human expertise with powerful analytics to shape a more positive future for our clients and people. The Opportunity At LCP the work we do involves assessing the risks associated with uncertain future events. We help clients better understand and quantify these uncertainties, thereby supporting them to mitigate these risks. As a Summer Intern in the Insurance Consulting department specifically, you'll get an insight into the work we do advising general insurance companies on reserving and financial risk management, building models to calculate capital requirements, and helping insurance companies meet the Solvency UK /Solvency II regulatory requirements. You will also have the opportunity to support the development of our analytics tools, using R and Python, including our multi-award-winning reserving platform InsurSight. An internship with us will give you real insight into how we communicate our analysis to our clients in a way that’s simple, clear, and easy to understand. You will have the opportunity to get involved in real client projects, giving you a deep insight into what a career in insurance consulting could look like. What We’re Looking For: · A Level Maths A Grade · Studying for a Degree in any subject and on track to achieve a 2:1 · An interest in insurance and awareness of topical issues · Problem solving, numeracy and analytical skills · Excellent communication skills and evidence of coding ability and presentation skills Why should you choose LCP? · Vibrant Culture: Join an environment brimming with fun, collaboration, and mutual growth · Gain Experience: Build your knowledge with exposure to real projects and work · Network: Opportunity to learn from experienced professionals and build a network · Future Opportunities: Potential for future career opportunities on our Graduate Programme · A Celebration of Diversity: At LCP, every voice matters. We champion a space where all forms of diversity are celebrated, ensuring everyone feels seen, heard, and valued Take a look at our Career stories to find out why people love working at LCP. You would be joining a multi-award winning, fun, collaborative, people first organisation where your personal and professional skills will be developed to make you the best you can be.
