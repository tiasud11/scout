Title: 2027 UK Finance Summer Internship Requisition ID: 278323 Join a purpose driven winning team, committed to results, in an inclusive and high-performing culture. Duration: June – end of August 2027 (10 weeks) Location: London, United Kingdom Scotiabank is a leading bank in the Americas. We are powered by our 97,000 high-performing teammates who make a real difference across the globe as a leading provider of advice, products, digital experiences, and financial services. This is a place where you’ll get to learn and develop your skills, while being recognised for your hard work. At Scotiabank, we’re passionate about bringing our whole selves to work, allowing us to create inclusive work environments for everyone to enjoy. Our Europe operations are located in London and Dublin and are part of our Global Banking & Markets (GBM) business. GBM provides a full range of investment banking, credit and risk management products and services relevant to the financing and strategic development needs of our clients. Our products include debt and equity financing, mergers & acquisitions, corporate banking, institutional equity sales, trading and research, fixed income products, derivatives, energy, foreign exchange, and precious metals. We also cross-sell the full range of wholesale products and services offered by the Scotiabank Group. Programme Overview: Scotiabank offers an exciting opportunity to join the London-based internal Finance team of a Canadian Bank commencing in June 2027. This 10-week programme is designed for students in their first year of study (or second year of a four year degree) who are eager to learn and have a passion for Finance. The programme is rotational and will provide the opportunity to gain a varied experience in the different areas that make up a modern Finance division in an Investment Bank. This internship will allow you to deepen your understanding of how an Investment Bank’s Finance function works and learning what it takes to be a trusted business advisor. What You Can Expect: During the programme, you will: Assist with the preparation of key regulatory reporting, tax and financial reporting returns Assist with Daily Limit Monitoring and investigating differences with the business Provide analysis to support advice to the business on managing capital and liquidity resources Create and prepare payment runs to ensure timely payment of expenses Providing detailed analysis on results and providing insight to business leaders to help make decisions Working on key initiatives which will help to optimise business performance Managing expenses in the business to drive business performance Gain a better understanding of the regulatory environment that Financial Institutions work within Ensuring a flawless controls environment Assist with implementation of efficient processes ensuring continuous improvement of processes & reporting Develop financial business analysis skills working with Projects team to plan and test for computing solutions whilst meeting appropriate requirements of efficiency and robustness Complete an intern project assignment and present it to the wider team Any other ad hoc work efforts assigned by management Programme Highlights: Our internship is designed to give you real insight into life at Scotiabank. Alongside your day-to-day responsibilities, you’ll take part in: A weekly speaker series with leaders from across the Bank Peer to peer intern presentations Social and networking events A CSR project that supports one of our charity partners Who We're Looking For: You are a first-year undergraduate student (or second-year on a four-year degree) You are available for a 10-week internship between mid-June to end of August 2027 You have a demonstrated interest in and knowledge of the financial markets You have a basic understanding of financial statements, accounting and variance analysis You have an analytical mindset with a passion for critical thinking and working collaboratively You have excellent interpersonal skills and looking to work as part of a diverse and inclusive team You are eligible to work in the United Kingdom How To Apply: Fill out an application via the Scotiabank careers portal and submit your CV when prompted (please note a cover letter is not required) Successful applicants will be contacted regarding next steps, which will be comprised of two interviews with members of the Finance team Summer Internship applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.
