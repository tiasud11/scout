Want to explore a career in business while building your commercial skills and professional network?Join the P&G Commercial Internship Week, a 3-day interactive experience designed for first-year university students looking to gain real-world business exposure and discover what a career in Sales at P&G could look like.Based in our Harrogate office, you'll get a behind-the-scenes look at how we partner with major retailers, develop growth strategies for iconic brands, and turn shopper insights into commercial success. What To ExpectOur Commercial Internship Week will give you the opportunity to meet with senior leaders as well as recent graduates so you can get a real sense for what it’s like to work for P&G after 1 year or 20!The Internship Week will take place at our Harrogate Office, Yorkshire, 31st March – 2nd April 2027. We will provide accommodation for two nights and a chance to network with a variety of people around the company through activities in the evenings.We believe socio-economic status should not be a barrier to accessing job opportunities and seek to ensure that we are championing social mobility. As a 'build from within' company, our people start at entry level in Harrogate and progress through the organization.
