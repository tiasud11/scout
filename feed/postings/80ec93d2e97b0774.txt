Featured Sign up for our Talent Community Loading Results No Match Found View All Results Working at PwC PwC is a hugely diverse business bound together by a single purpose. Our purpose Our purpose is to build trust in society and solve important problems. At PwC, we unite expertise and tech so that our clients can outthink, outpace and outperform their competition. That makes us a hugely diverse business, offering all kinds of professional services to all kinds of organisations, all over the world. Our purpose is what unites and underpins all that insight and impact.​ Our greatest opportunity to deliver our purpose is by doing meaningful work that makes a difference to our clients, our colleagues, the communities around us and society as a whole. ​ Our Global firm is made up of hundreds of thousands of talented people who sit across our wider network. We work with clients of every shape and size –from the tech startups disrupting the way we live to the biggest brands, the most successful multinationals and the most significant organisations, which shape the world around us. So the trust we build and the problems we solve do really have a far-reaching impact. And everyone who joins us plays their part in that shared purpose. ​ Our values While PwC people come from all kinds of different backgrounds and cultures, our values are what we have in common. They set the standards for what we expect from ourselves and one another. They hold us accountable to be the best we can be, and to take personal responsibility for our actions. They guide how we work with, and care for, our clients, our communities and each other.​ Act with integrity Make a difference Care Work together Reimagine the possible Act with integrity We expect and deliver the highest quality outcomes, speak up for what’s right, even in difficult situations, and make decisions as if our personal reputation is at stake. Make a difference We stay informed and respond with agility to the ever-changing business environment in which we operate—always looking to create a positive impact on our colleagues, clients and society. Care We aim to understand every individual and what matters to them, recognising each person’s value and contribution, while enabling them to grow in a way that brings out their best. Work together We collaborate, share ideas and integrate a diverse range of perspectives to improve ourselves and others. Reimagine the possible We empower innovation and challenge the status quo by keeping an open mind to the possibilities in every idea. Grow here. Go further. Our People Value Proposition (PVP) ‘Grow here. Go Further' is designed to enhance the way we work, grow and learn together. It creates a distinctive and unified people experience that delivers on our brand promise. It outlines the mutual expectations of working here, and is shaped around the four pillars detailed below, which represent our offering to you and the experience you'll have working here. These are underpinned by our PwC Professional behaviours ( early careers / experienced careers ), which outline the expectations we have of our people in return. At PwC, you lead by example. Our standards for quality and care ​are high – and together we surpass them. We redefine possibilities for our clients and communities. Through diverse starting points ​and collective expertise, we embrace every opportunity to shape ​ the future.​ Here, success means influencing where the world will be. We work with emerging technologies and leading organisations, questioning assumptions about what’s next and empowering our people to be their best. ​ We do more than develop skills — we cultivate potential. Your growth becomes our clients’ advantage. Here, we uncover hidden talents, build lifelong relationships rooted in trust and empathy, and turn challenges into opportunities for innovation.​ Together, we set the future in motion.​ We support you in making a meaningful impact — one that reaches far beyond PwC.​ Shaping tomorrow Committing to growth Bringing your best, every day​ Embracing inclusion, driving impact​ Shaping tomorrow At PwC, we’re dedicated to our purpose of building trust in society and solving important problems. You’ll live our purpose every day and create meaningful impact by tackling today’s complex challenges with a future-focused mindset. You’ll have the opportunity to gain hands-on and diverse experience by partnering closely with our clients and stakeholders. By fostering continuous learning and growth, we ensure that we stay ahead in an ever-evolving landscape. Innovation is at the heart of everything we do, and at PwC you’ll have the opportunity to be at the forefront of this. With access to cutting-edge technology, you’ll push the boundaries of what's possible and create leading solutions that transform industries. We encourage bold thinking and curiosity to ensure that we are able to provide our clients with the solutions, skills, and tools they need to thrive in a competitive environment and stay relevant in the marketplace. Committing to growth Our people grow here and go further. We understand that each individual's career path is unique, and at PwC you’ll have the chance to shape yours. We are a skills-enabled organisation and, in a rapidly changing world, cultivating the right skills through upskilling and reskilling is more vital than ever. You’ll have access to a wide variety of growth opportunities, including formal learning, allowing you to develop the skills you need for the career you want. You’ll take personal accountability for your development and the development of others, with a commitment to lifelong learning and agility, and will be empowered to apply your skills where they have the greatest impact. At PwC, you’ll have the opportunity to collaborate with talented teams and individuals who’ll fuel your development through coaching with clear and constructive feedback at all levels, giving you the guidance and support you need to thrive. You’ll grow through challenging and meaningful work that pushes you to develop transferrable skills and knowledge. We’ll support you to navigate change, and by being curious you’ll find opportunities to grow and thrive in a dynamic environment. Bringing your best, every day​ At PwC, we want you to be able to bring your best, every day. We empower our people and strive to provide a people experience grounded in care for you and your wellbeing. Our innovative and inclusive offices, empowered flexibility, and cutting-edge technology enable you to work in a way that works for you, your teams, and your clients, and support you to maintain a healthy work-life balance. We'll aim to recognise and reward you in a way that is meaningful to you. We strive for excellence in everything we do and you’ll be empowered to take personal responsibility for delivering the highest level of quality to our clients. You’ll develop a critical lens, act with integrity and learn to embrace uncertainty whilst managing risk. You’ll have access to industry leaders and experts who will share their knowledge and insights, empowering you to stretch yourself and reimagine what’s possible. By challenging the status quo, you’ll unlock new opportunities and innovate in ways that drive progress. We’ll support your ambition and curiosity, allowing you to explore new solutions and make a meaningful impact. Embracing inclusion, driving impact​ At PwC, you’ll be recognised for the uniqueness you bring and will have the chance to work with diverse and inclusive teams and leaders, who will support you to unlock your full potential. We want you, and everyone around you, to feel like you belong, feel able to speak up when something’s not right, and be proud to be part of PwC. You’ll be inspired to collaborate inside and outside our flexible, accessible and exciting office spaces, to bring the best of the firm and deliver pre-eminent outcomes for our clients and communities, so you’ll never have to go it alone. You’ll have the opportunity to discover your community with purpose-driven people who are passionate about making a difference. You’ll be curious about the people you work with, ensuring that every voice is heard and valued. You’ll promote a collaborative environment with fair and equitable outcomes, where ideas flourish and where we create a positive lasting impact. Our culture As PwC enters its next era, we've taken the opportunity to consider what culture we need to succeed. We have a culture of high performance built on exceptional quality, ethical and professional standards. There are many critical aspects to that culture. But we’re clear about the culture priorities that we all need to bring to life. And we’re focusing on the elements that will have the biggest impact and benefits, shaping our future as a UK firm. ​ Be inspiring This is about showing up as the leader you aspire to be authentically, every day. It’s about driving a deep sense of pride in our teams to create positive energy (optimism) and drive our commercial success.​ Be curious This is about committing to a growth mindset in everything we do and showing up with a hunger to learn every day. Curiosity drives us to be bold, embrace technology and be more innovative, thinking differently and doing things in new ways. Be collaborative This is about working together to find a better way. When we truly embrace collaboration, we connect the people with the right skills and experience to deliver for our clients, no matter their level, team or role. Be clear This is about creating clarity for our clients and colleagues.
