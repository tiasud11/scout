How to Apply to Ark Teacher Training Start your teaching journey with Ark Teacher Training, where you can earn both Qualified Teacher Status (QTS) and a PGCE in just 12 months. The Application Process There are four simple steps to apply, ensuring a smooth and supportive process every step of the way. Apply now Four Simple Steps There are four simple steps to apply, ensuring a smooth and supportive process every step of the way. Step 1 Step 2 Step 3 Step 4 Submit Your Online Application The first step is to complete your application form through the Department for Education website. Simply select the relevant course that denotes your subject and funding route of interest. Applications for our primary programme will open in the new year; please check back for updates nearer the time. To be eligible for Ark Teacher Training, you must: Hold an undergraduate degree (2:2 or higher). Have a GCSE grade C/4 or above (or equivalent) in English and Maths. If you’re applying to teach primary, you’ll also need a GCSE in Science. Have the right to live and work in the UK for the duration of the programme. Not already hold Qualified Teacher Status (QTS). Previous experience working with young people is not required but is highly valued and can strengthen your application. For more information about the qualifications and qualities we look for, click here . Apply now Complete our Application+ form Once you have submitted your Department for Education application, you’ll be asked to complete our Application+ form, which will be emailed to you directly. Once we receive your application, we’ll assess your eligibility for the programme. This includes reviewing your qualifications and making sure you meet the legal requirements for teaching in the UK. Apply now Attend the Virtual Competency-Based Interview The first-round assessment is a virtual interview where we’ll evaluate your communication skills and your alignment with the core competencies we seek in trainee teachers. In this competency-based interview, we’ll look for the five key traits that are crucial for success in teaching: Commitment to train Strong communication skills Work readiness Self-awareness Resilience This interview also allows us to match you with one of our partner schools based on your preferences and location. Apply now Take Part in the School-Based Assessment If successful at your first-round interview, you’ll be invited to a school-based assessment at one of our schools located across the Ark regions of Birmingham, Hastings, Portsmouth, London or Pontefract , or one of our partner school regions, more info here . During this in-person assessment, you’ll: Tour the school and meet key members of staff. Participate in a short task with students , allowing you to demonstrate your interaction with young people. Have an interview with senior leaders , giving you a chance to discuss your goals and aspirations as a teacher. This process is designed not just to assess you, but also to give you a feel for the school environment, the staff you’ll work with, and the students you’ll teach. It ensures a good fit between you and the school, making your training experience more successful and enjoyable. Apply now Why Choose Ark Teacher Training? Apply now Your application and AI We don’t mind you using AI to help shape your application statement, but we want to hear about your motivations and experiences. We assess candidates based on merit, and therefore want to see an authentic, accurate reflection of your skills/experience. With thousands of applications each year, we quickly spot text that hasn’t been proofread or tailored, and statements lacking original thought are likely to be down-marked. We may reject applications where AI is used inappropriately at any stage of the process. DO use an AI tool to... …better organise and clarify your thoughts for a statement or interview Your draft: “ I have had a positive impact on young people in my current role” AI prompt: “ Help me to think of how to quantify the impact I have had’” …research public information on Ark Teacher Training AI prompt: “ What are the core qualities that Ark Teacher Training values in its trainees, based on the public information about the Ark network?” …support you with adjustments for additional needs; ask our recruiters if you feel this would help DON'T use an AI tool to... …answer questions live in an interview. We want to get to know you (& how you’ll be in front of young people) …create or inflate experiences AI prompt : “Write a personal statement for Ark Teacher Training, based on their core competencies” Result: generic statement with experience you haven’t actually had; probing at interview may result in us questioning your honesty as a potential trainee. …copy and paste answers from AI tools into your application Result: recruiters recognise phrasing and content in AI-generated statements, so we know we aren’t seeing the real ‘you’ or your written communication skills. Result: statement looks generic and does not set you apart for your personal potential on our programme. FAQ What qualifications do I need to apply? To apply for Ark Teacher Training, you’ll need the following: An undergraduate degree in any subject GCSE (or equivalent) in English and maths at grade 4 (C) or above GCSE (or equivalent) in science if you are applying to train as a primary teacher The right to work in the UK for the duration of your training I have a disability. How will Ark Teacher Training support me? We want to make sure that our training programme is open to everybody, so if you have a disability, please let us know on your application so we can accommodate you throughout the process. This will in no way affect your application. I'm not a recent graduate; am I still able to apply? Absolutely! Great schools are made of great teachers, and we look for the best for you, whether you’re a graduate or a career changer. Our trainees have been nurses, soldiers, lawyers and doctors, bringing incredible skills and life experience into the classroom. Do I need work experience? When you decide to apply to Ark Teacher Training, we want you to be sure that teaching is the right career for you. That is why it’s great if you have some experience with young people, even if it’s not in the classroom. We will ask you about this at the interview, so be prepared. Your ATT application…and the use of AI As a trainee teacher, you’ll find that one of the few predictable things about a live classroom environment is its unpredictability! Schools are fast-paced, and we need to know that you can think on your feet and communicate effectively to many different stakeholders at short notice (such as students, colleagues, parents). We also know that technology and AI tools are developing rapidly and in exciting ways. As a trainee with us, you’d be likely to use AI in some way, so we don’t mind you using it to help you formulate your statement in our application form. However, we are interested in hearing about your own personal motivations and experiences, so we suggest you use it as a building block and don’t simply paste search results into the form; with thousands of applications each year, we very quickly spot those that haven’t been proofread or tailored to our programme! If we don’t feel there is sufficient original thought in your statement, we will likely down-mark you for communication, so please think carefully about how you want to come across to our recruiters. What is the Subject Knowledge Assessment? The Subject Knowledge Assessment includes 15 multiple-choice questions aligned with the National Curriculum for your chosen subject. The aim is to assess your subject knowledge and identify areas where you may need additional training or support. This is not a timed test, so you can work through it at your own pace. What competencies do you look for in trainees? We look for trainees with the potential to inspire, motivate, and connect with students. Key competencies include: Strong communication skills A passion for teaching and learning Resilience and adaptability A commitment to Ark’s values and mission Self awareness Learn more about the competencies we assess during the interview process here . What happens after I’m offered a place? Once you’ve been offered a place, you’ll need to: Complete your new starter documentation. Undergo an enhanced DBS check as part of our safeguarding policy. Finalise your placement through the Department for Education’s Apply service. Our recruitment team will guide you through every step. When does the programme start? The Ark Teacher Training programme begins in September with a series of induction days. These include: A tutor induction meeting A school orientation experience A compulsory two-week Summer School in August Can I defer my place? Unfortunately, we cannot defer offers to future years. However, we’d love to help you strengthen your application for when you’re ready to apply again. Talk to one of our team about possible graduate teaching assistant roles in the network. Can I reapply if I’m not accepted? If you’re unsuccessful in getting a place this year, you will need to wait until the next recruitment cycle to reapply. We always welcome candidates who apply the following year; often their application is stronger as they have gained additional experience and taken on our feedback! What is the school-based assessment? The final stage of the application process is your school-based assessment, where you’ll visit one of our schools. This stage includes: A tour of the school to familiarise yourself with the environment A group task with students to demonstrate your ability to connect with young people An interview with senior leaders to discuss your fit with the school and the programme You’ll also deliver a 20-minute lesson on a pre-assigned topic. This is an opportunity to showcase your potential and receive feedback. How can I prepare for the pre-recorded video interview? If requested, the pre-recorded video interview helps us get to know you better. You’ll answer five questions about your skills and experiences. To prepare: Find a quiet, well-lit space for recording. Take time to reflect on your motivations for teaching. Practise articulating how your experiences translate into teaching skills. Remember, this step is designed to help you succeed, so don’t stress! What happens during the virtual first-round assessment? The first-round assessment is a competency-based interview conducted online. You’ll: Participate in a 50-minute interview with our recruitment team. Present a 5-minute teaching-related presentation. This stage allows us to assess your communication skills and teaching potential. What is the Employee Assistance Programme? As part of our commitment to trainee wellbeing, Ark offers access to the Employee Assistance Programme . This service provides: Counselling and emotional support Financial advice General guidance to help you navigate challenges during your training year How do you ensure the right fit between trainees and schools? Matching trainees to schools is a collaborative process based on your preferences, strengths, and location. We aim to place you in a school with a manageable commute and a supportive environment. Why is the application process so thorough? We believe our comprehensive application process ensures the best fit for both you and your school placement. By taking the time to assess eligibility, potential, and alignment with our programme, we set our trainees up for success. Do you have a subcontracting policy?
