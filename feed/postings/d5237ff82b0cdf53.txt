We are dedicated to helping our clients achieve superior results through trusted, independent, and innovative advice on matters of strategic significance to boards of directors, management teams, and shareholders – including mergers and acquisitions, shareholder advisory, restructurings, and capital structure. Evercore also assists clients in raising public and private capital, and delivers equity research, equity sales, and agency trading execution, in addition to providing wealth and investment management services to high-net-worth individuals. Our Intern Programme gives students the opportunity to work on industry-shaping transactions with global clients. Founded in 1995, the Firm is headquartered in New York and maintains offices and affiliate offices in major financial centers in North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia. Job Description Our Private Funds Group (PFG) provides comprehensive global advisory services on capital raising for select sponsors. Our clients include general partners raising capital for private markets investments, including private equity, private debt and infrastructure. PFG advises and executes on all aspects of the fundraising process, including competitive positioning and market assessment, preparation of marketing materials, investor development, and documentation. Our internship is designed for undergraduates in their penultimate year of study, looking to pursue a career in investment banking. Following one week of technical and industry-based training, you will start on desk and quickly become a much-valued team member providing the vital support required in a dynamic firm and industry. Across the programme we aim to provide you with regular support and performance feedback to help aid your progression and experience at Evercore. There is also plenty of opportunity to broaden your network through our speaker series sessions, summer firm-wide events and intern socials for you to attend throughout the programme! We have discovered talented, motivated and professional individuals through our previous summer internship programmes and full-time offers will be awarded to top performers. We recruit on a rolling basis so please apply early! Qualifications Who are we looking for: Academic achievement We look for an exceptional academic record, coupled with diverse interests. Critical mindset Our Interns need to think critically about a range of strategic issues impacting our clients.
