You’ll discover more about the work we do, as well as our diverse teams, and the career opportunities available to you. Our programmes are a perfect opportunity to build your work experience, develop new skills, grow your network and gain learning that will last you a lifetime. You’ll get an insider’s view into one of the world’s leading professional services firms - the projects we work on, the industries we work in and the meaningful and positive difference we make for our clients, people and the communities we serve. Who's it for? We are committed to creating an inclusive environment where all colleagues thrive and reach their full potential, whatever their identity or background. To achieve this, we have set long-term targets to 2030, one of which is to increase our Black Heritage representation at partner level to 5% by 2030. We recognise that individuals of Black Heritage may face additional barriers. That's why we have launched the Black Heritage Talent Insight Programme. The Black Heritage Talent Insight Programme is aimed at students of Black Heritage who are seeking insight into a career in Audit or Tax and are in their penultimate, final year of study or have already graduated. By Black Heritage we mean students who might identify as Black, African, Caribbean, Black British, other Black or Mixed Black ethnic groups. If this doesn’t apply to you, we encourage you to review our alternative online work experience programme . We want everyone to be able to perform at their best, therefore if there are any adjustments you feel would benefit you during the recruitment process, you can provide information within the relevant section of your application. We will also ask you to confirm if any adjustments are needed before each stage of the processes. To find out more information please see support for graduates. The programme will be offered to students who are in their final year of university or recently graduated. This programme has been purposefully designed as a step towards a possible Graduate offer if you’re successful on the programme. This offer would only be for the same location and business area as that you completed your Talent Insight Programme. As such, please ensure you have a commitment to the location and business area that you apply to.
