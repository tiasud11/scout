Take on a role where your ideas, expertise and ambition can make a real difference, helping shape a secure and evolving energy future. As a Risk Graduate, you'll help provide the analysis, reporting and controls that enable SEFE to make informed commercial decisions in a complex and fast-moving market environment. You'll work with experienced colleagues across Risk, Trading, Compliance and Middle Office functions to understand how risk is identified, measured, monitored and managed across the business. This is an excellent opportunity for graduates who enjoy analytical thinking, problem-solving and understanding how businesses balance commercial opportunity with effective risk management. You'll gain broad business exposure while developing specialist knowledge across multiple risk disciplines. WHAT WILL YOU DO Produce quantitative risk reporting and analysis used by stakeholders across Risk Management and Front Office teams to support commercial decision-making. Analyse data, market information and business activities to identify key risks, trends and areas requiring further investigation. Develop an understanding of market, credit and operational risk frameworks and how they are applied within a global energy trading business. Create and maintain documentation including risk policies, methodologies, procedures and incident reporting materials. Present findings, insights and recommendations to stakeholders across Risk and other business functions. Contribute to projects that enhance risk management processes, controls and reporting capabilities. Build strong relationships with colleagues across Risk, Trading, Compliance, Audit and other business areas, developing a broad understanding of SEFE's operations. Take ownership of your professional development, building both technical expertise and commercial understanding throughout the programme. WHAT WILL YOU BRING We're looking for curious and analytical graduates who enjoy working with data, solving problems and understanding how businesses manage risk in complex commercial environments. You'll ideally have: A Bachelor's or Master's degree (completed or due to be completed before the programme starts) in Finance, Economics, Mathematics, Engineering, Physics, Business, Data Science or another numerate discipline. Strong analytical thinking and problem-solving skills, with the ability to evaluate information, identify key issues and draw logical, well-reasoned conclusions. Strong organisational skills, with the ability to manage priorities, meet deadlines and adapt to changing business needs and circumstances. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the confidence to explain findings and recommendations clearly to a range of audiences. The ability to build strong working relationships and collaborate effectively with colleagues across different teams and functions. Proficiency in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel, with an interest in developing further analytical and technical skills. Resilience, adaptability and a commitment to continuous learning and professional development in a dynamic business environment. An interest in financial markets, energy trading, risk management or corporate governance. Previous internships, placements or work experience in financial services, energy, consulting or another analytical environment would be advantageous but are not essential. Applications are open until 16 December 2026. After the application deadline, no further submissions will be accepted. ABOUT US SEFE is an international energy company anchored in Europe, delivering energy solutions that ensure reliable and affordable supply. Our activities span the entire energy value chain – from origination and trading to sales, transport and storage. With decades of trading expertise and a growing LNG portfolio, SEFE is one of Europe’s leading suppliers to industrial customers, providing more than 200 TWh of gas and power each year. We supply over 50,000 clients, from small businesses to municipalities and multinational organisations. By investing in clean energies, we support our customers on their decarbonisation journey and contribute to the energy transition. SEFE employs over 2,000 people worldwide and is owned by the Federal Government of Germany. Securing energy – now and for the future.
