Placement and duration The Morgan Stanley Summer Analyst Programme in London runs for 10-weeks and offers you the opportunity to join a world class equity boutique including firm training and networking as well as on the desk training. Training Program London-based training begins in June with one week of intensive classroom training, including an introduction to Morgan Stanley, followed by team specific training. Throughout the internship, you will be exposed to business speakers and practical sessions as you continue to become familiar with the asset management industry. Roles and Responsibilities Opportunities exist within the following teams across MSIM Real Assets: Real Estate Investing – Capital Markets: Morgan Stanley Real Estate Investing (REI) is Morgan Stanley's integrated global real estate practice. We are the world's leading global real estate investment manager with over $60 billion in real estate assets under management across core, value-added and opportunistic investment vehicles. REI acquires properties, operating companies, and real estate backed departments on a global scale on behalf of our institutional investors. Position will be in MSREI’s London capital markets team which focusses on raising, structuring and executing financing solutions for all European investments. Private Structured Credit – European Real Estate Debt: The European Real Assets Private Credit Team is part of Morgan Stanley’s Investment Management Real Assets capabilities which manages over $75 billion of investable capital globally across real estate and infrastructure equity and credit strategies. The European Real Assets Private Credit team manages approximately $4 billion of investable capital and focuses on investments in European real estate debt across investment grade and value-add strategies.
