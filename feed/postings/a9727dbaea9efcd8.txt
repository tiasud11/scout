You can apply for one role only. Role: Investment Internship Programme Location: London Who are St. James's Place? At St. James's Place, we believe in investing in potential. Our Early Careers opportunities provide a platform for students and graduates to launch a meaningful career in financial services. Whether you're joining us through a graduate scheme, internship or apprenticeship, you'll be part of a collaborative environment where learning and development are central. We are dedicated to creating a diverse and inclusive environment where you are supported in your development and encouraged to thrive from the very beginning of your journey with us. The Investment Internship Programme: Our six-week paid internship is designed for students in their penultimate year of study, offering a valuable opportunity to gain first-hand experience within the Investment division in one of the UK's largest wealth management organisations. Throughout the internship programme, you'll be part of an inclusive and supportive team, contributing to day-to-day responsibilities while also working on ad-hoc projects across the Investment division. This will give you a well-rounded combination of practical experience, continuous learning and advancement of your technical knowledge. This is a chance to build your professional network, develop essential workplace skills, and explore what a future career in Investments at St. James's Place could look like. The internship provides both structure and flexibility, giving you the opportunity to learn, contribute, and grow in a dynamic setting and upon completion of the internship you have the opportunity of being considered for a position on our Investment Graduate Programme the following year once you complete your studies. Key responsibilities: ​​During the programme you will rotate across core teams and your responsibilities during these rotations will include: ​ Investment Management & CIO Office ​Collaborating with colleagues across the different teams, including Investment Research, Portfolios and the CIO Office which comprises the investment specialists and product specialists. ​Conducting data analysis using Power BI, Excel, Bloomberg, Financial Express, etc to deliver insights to the rest of the team. ​Summarising investment discussions, to internal teams. ​Talking to our financial advisers about our investment approach, asset allocation views, manager research decisions and associated topics. ​ ​ Investment Product ​Support the review and analysis of existing investment products, ensuring they remain competitive, relevant and aligned with target market needs, and recommend modifications and enhancements as appropriate. ​Assist in the preparation of product documentation, including business cases, prospectuses, fact sheets, and marketing collateral as required. ​Help coordinate product launches and changes (namely, fund launches, fund manager replacements, reallocations and IMA updates). ​Gain an understanding of how fund manager fee structures are set and support the team in shaping these decisions. ​ ​ ​ What we're looking for: ​​You must be In your penultimate year at university studying towards a 2:1 in Finance, Economics or Mathematics ​Minimum grade B at A-level mathematics ​Interest in investments, financial markets and the wealth management market. ​Clear and confident communication, both written and verbal ​Proficiency in MS Office (Excel and PowerPoint); experience with Python, Power BI or similar tools is an advantage. ​Highly organised, collaborative, and committed to producing high-quality work. Special Requirements: ​​You will be based in our office in the City of London and will be expected to be in the office for a minimum of 3 days per week and more when business needs require it. From time to time you may be required to travel to other SJP offices across the UK, particularly Cirencester in Gloucestershire where some of the wider Investment Directorate are based.​ To be eligible for this vacancy, all candidates must have the right to work in the UK.
