Shape the Future Through Insight At Capital Group, our mission is to improve people’s lives through successful investing. Our Capital Associates Program (CAP) invites exceptional early-career talent who combine deep curiosity, analytical rigor, collaborative leadership, and independent thinking to help us explore some of the world’s most complex and consequential investment questions.CAP is not a typical internship – it’s a highly selective, immersive experience designed for students who want to think deeply, challenge assumptions, and learn about Capital’s unique investment process. A Truly Differentiated Experience As a CAP Summer Associate, you will investigate complex investment questions that require original insight, careful judgment, and the ability to synthesize information across industries, markets and disciplines.The emphasis is on rigorous research: examining industries, assessing long-term trends, asking thoughtful questions, and developing well-reasoned perspectives when answers are rarely obvious. You’ll share your own research approach, explore ideas in depth, and produce thoughtful analyses that contribute to Capital Group’s long-term investment thinking.CAP Associates have recently researched and presented recommendations/insights on:Data Centers in SpaceValuation Frameworks for Bitcoin and GoldEmerging Market Debt in North Macedonia, Albania and MontenegroThe Lifecycle of U.S. Apparel and Footwear BrandsGrowth of Indian Quick-Service Restaurant IndustryQuantum Computing Supply Chain and Implications for CMOS ManufacturingJapan Satellite CompaniesPotential Applications of Hydrogen in Decarbonizing MobilityEnergy Storage and Global LithiumThese aren’t hypothetical exercises. CAP Associates explore real-world investment questions and contribute differentiated insights to our investment dialogue.How You’ll Contribute and Develop Ownership of Substantive Research: Lead an end-to-end investment research project that rewards curiosity, intellectual discipline, and strong writing. You’ll frame the question, conduct deep analysis, and present clear, well-supported conclusions to senior investors. Development Through Practice: Strengthen your analytical reasoning, research process, and communication skills through hands-on work, mentorship, and targeted development sessions. Learn Alongside Experienced Investors: Work closely with analysts and portfolio managers to understand how rigorous inquiry, qualitative and quantitative analyses, and independent thinking contribute to investment decisions.Join A Global Community of Thinkers: Join a small, high-calibre cohort of Summer Associates across London, Los Angeles, and Singapore united by intellectual curiosity and an interest in investing.Who Should Apply? We welcome applications from undergraduate and master’s students (non-MBA) graduating between December 2027 and July 2028. When we hire for CAP, we look for raw talent and potential. Ideal candidates demonstrate: Analytical, Fast Learning: Strong quantitative problem-solvers who quickly absorb new ideas, challenge assumptions, and turn complexity into clear, actionable insights. Academic Excellence: Demonstrated record of exceptional academic achievement. Double majors, minors, and interdisciplinary study are valued. Adaptable High Performance: Thrive in fast-paced, ambiguous environments; receptive to feedback and able to balance rigorous academics with leadership, work, or side projects. Collaborative Leadership: Proven ability to lead, influence, and contribute meaningfully within teams across academic, professional, or extracurricular settings. Investing Curiosity: Demonstrated interest in investing through student organizations, competitions, coursework, or independent learning. Unique Perspective: Bring distinctive life experiences or passions—athletics, music, creative pursuits, global exposure, or deep expertise in a specific area.
