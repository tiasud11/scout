Around the world, FTI Consulting seasoned litigation consultants provide clear, reliable and objective advice — in matters ranging from discovery and investigation to expert witness testimony and damages quantification — to courts and tribunals, parties to disputes and their legal counsel. Our trusted, senior experts have proven track records in international arbitration and dispute resolution consulting. We support our global clients in dealing with disputes of all kinds, including claims in international public law, and complex commercial and regulatory disputes that require industry-specific knowledge. Our goal is to provide independent, authoritative expert witness testimony and advice. We bring insight to all phases of a case and offer unique perspectives to a case’s strengths and weaknesses. Whatever your specific situation requires, our dispute advisory services help determine whether a position is defendable, test theories, evaluate strategies, quantify damages, and anticipate and critique opposing arguments. We simplify the matter with in-depth analyses, expert witness testimony and persuasive visual presentations. Clients benefit from our best-in-class expert witnesses and a top-notch litigation consulting team. We provide input to courts, tribunals and regulators that help them to determine solutions. Our deep and extensive bench of industry experts, former regulators, and consulting experts provides the foundation for our litigation and expert witness services — across a broad range of issues including industry standards and customary business practices, accounting, liability, valuation , economic damages, fact finding and forensic review and analysis.
