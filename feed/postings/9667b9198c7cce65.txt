Commercial roles here go far beyond a standard sales pitch. You will operate in a true O+O (Offline + Online) environment, connecting physical shelves with digital carts across everything from mass market to luxury boutiques and social commerce. The expectations are high: you will negotiate directly with major retailers, take early ownership of your strategy, and manage a real P&L (Profit and Loss). If you want to build sharp omnichannel skills and see your decisions directly impact how the world buys our iconic brands, this is where you do it. Skills you'll sharpen here Business opportunities identification P&L management Sustainable business model design Negotiation Activation excellence Omnichannel experience design As well as these, L'Oréal employees are upksilled in AI fluency, data fluency, and critical thinking. How we do business? You won’t be boxed into a single channel. You’ll navigate a massive Offline plus Online (O+O) ecosystem, from mass retail and pharmacies to luxury boutiques and e-commerce, working alongside Marketing to connect physical shelves to digital carts.. Expect early ownership and a steep learning curve. You won’t sit on the sidelines. You will manage real budgets, drive live negotiations, and deliver immediate business impact. To back you up, our Commerce University will arm you with the exact omnichannel skills needed to thrive here. Graduate Management Trainee – Commercial (London) London, England, United Kingdom Commercial Management Trainee – London Applications close: 20th November Programme start: Monday 3rd January 2028 Starting salary: £36,500, rising to £41,500 on completion of the scheme Eligibility: You must be in your final year of study or have graduated within the past two years. Hello, we’re L’Oréal We’re not just building brands; we’re shaping how the world experiences beauty. If you’re curious about how businesses grow and keen to develop your skills, this could be the opportunity for you. A day in the life Based at our UK headquarters in White City, London, you’ll complete three six-month rotations across Key Accounts, E-Commerce and Field Sales over an 18-month programme. You’ll learn from different teams and see how we work with retail and digital partners to grow our brands. You could: Help prepare sales forecasts, track promotions and report on commercial performance. Support customer meetings and product range reviews by preparing presentations. Review sales across retail and online channels to help identify opportunities for growth. Help coordinate field sales activity and in-store campaigns. Work with Commercial, Supply Chain and Marketing teams to help ensure products are available for customers. What we’re looking for You don’t need previous commercial experience. We’re looking for potential, curiosity and a willingness to learn. Throughout the recruitment process, we’ll look for examples of how you demonstrate: Ambition: motivation, energy and a desire to grow and succeed. Resilience: the ability to stay composed, learn from setbacks and keep moving forward. Empathy: active listening, respect for different perspectives and a collaborative approach. Judgement: logical thinking and balanced decisions, including in new or complex situations. Learning agility: curiosity, openness to feedback and the ability to adapt and apply what you learn. AI fluency: an openness to using AI tools effectively and responsibly, with an awareness of trustworthy AI principles. You’ll also bring an interest in how businesses and brands grow, clear communication skills, and confidence working with information and numbers, or a willingness to develop these skills during the programme. Right to work in the UK This programme leads to a guaranteed operational role upon successful completion. As graduates are retained after the programme, it’s important that you have the right to work in the UK for the longer term. During the application process, you’ll be asked: We are required by law to verify work authorization for all prospective employees. Please confirm your visa status using the drop down below. I’m a UK or Irish citizen, or hold Settled Status, with an indefinite right to work and no sponsorship requirement. I hold Pre-Settled Status, which gives me time-limited right to work and may require a status update or future visa. I hold another UK visa that currently gives me the right to work, but may require sponsorship in the future. I do not currently have the right to work in the UK. What’s in it for you? Three rotations to explore different areas of Commercial. Structured mentoring and opportunities to learn on the job. Real responsibility and the chance to contribute from day one. An environment where people from different backgrounds and perspectives are welcomed and can thrive. The opportunity to contribute to work that supports our brands’ societal and environmental commitments. Who we are You’ll join an international, diverse team where fresh perspectives are welcomed. L’Oréal creates the beauty that moves the world across four divisions: Luxe, Consumer Products, Dermatological Beauty and Professional Products. Our Commercial teams help our brands grow across retail, e-commerce and salon partners. We’re committed to an inclusive recruitment process and to treating candidates fairly.
