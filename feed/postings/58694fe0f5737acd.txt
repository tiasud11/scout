As part of the client-facing arm of Corporate & Investment Banking (CIB), you’ll work alongside experienced bankers to help deliver integrated banking solutions, build client relationships and support strategic growth opportunities across markets. With our unique international footprint, you’ll gain exposure to cross-border business, global clients and real-world banking challenges. You’ll work across teams, products and geographies while developing the commercial, analytical and relationship-building skills that underpin a successful career in banking. Join us if you’re curious about how businesses grow, how financial markets operate and how trusted client relationships are built. About the Internship Programme – how you’ll learn and grow Join a skills-first organisation where your potential matters more than your background. Over 10 weeks, you’ll work on real business challenges, build future-ready skills, and gain insight into how we support clients, facilitate trade and finance growth across our markets. Working alongside colleagues across our global network, you’ll develop the skills, confidence and connections to accelerate your career. For many interns, this is the first step towards a graduate role with us. The programme will support your development through: • A 10-week summer internship • A structured induction and orientation • Exposure to our products, clients and international network • Real projects and meaningful responsibilities from day one • Technical, professional and behavioural skills development • Support from managers, mentors and buddies • Opportunities to learn from senior leaders and industry experts • Community and volunteering initiatives. Where you could work You will be placed into one of the following Coverage Banking teams, working alongside experienced bankers to support client relationships, business development activities and transaction execution across our global franchise. Corporate Coverage: Support relationship teams managing multinational corporations, large local corporates and mid-market clients. You’ll gain exposure to financing solutions, transaction banking, capital markets activities and strategic client engagement while learning how long-term client relationships are developed and managed. Financial Institutions Coverage : Work with teams covering banks, insurance companies, asset managers, sovereign entities and other financial institutions. You’ll gain insight into how financial institutions access financing, manage risk and utilise the Bank’s global capabilities to support their businesses. Mergers & Acquisitions (M&A) : Gain exposure to landmark transactions including acquisitions, divestments, mergers and strategic partnerships. You’ll support research, analysis and deal execution activities while learning how clients navigate transformational business decisions. Bring the skills - we’ll help you build your career We welcome students from all degree disciplines and backgrounds, recognising that diverse perspectives drive innovation. We’re looking for people who can: • Take ownership and show initiative. • Think critically and solve problems using logic and sound judgement. • Learn quickly, stay curious and seek opportunities to grow. • Build strong relationships and collaborate effectively with others. • Adapt and perform in a fast-paced environment. • Demonstrate commercial curiosity and an interest in clients, industries and global markets. • Communicate confidently and work effectively across different cultures and backgrounds. Eligibility To be eligible for this internship, you should be a penultimate year student, able to intern in June 2027, and start full time in July 2028. You should have the permanent legal right to work in the country you’re applying to. You can only submit one application within a six-month period, so take time to review our opportunities and apply for the role that best fits your interests, skills and career goals. Key Dates • Assessment Centres: October 2026 onwards • Start Date: June 2027 About Corporate & Investment Banking (CIB) We are a super-connector for many of the world’s largest companies across more than 50 markets, playing a central role in global trade, capital flows and economic growth. We work with governments, financial institutions, and some of the world’s most ambitious corporates to help them expand, invest, and navigate uncertainty across increasingly complex and fast-changing markets. What makes us distinctive is our combination of deep local expertise with an extensive and unique global network. We support clients across risk management, financing, transaction services, advisory, and sustainable finance, helping move capital to where it’s needed most and supporting the real economy across our footprint.
