We’re looking for curious adventurers ready to find their party, fueled by imagination and drive to build what’s never been built before. At Hasbro and Wizards of the Coast, you’ll collaborate with passionate teams to reimagine our iconic brands and create experiences that spark joy, connection, and community through the magic of play. This is your chance to shape legendary play that lasts a lifetime. Help fans discover—and rediscover—the brands they love. Bring your curiosity, ideas and point of view to teams creating products, games and experiences enjoyed by fans around the world. One application. Many ways to play. Apply once to this general application for consideration for anticipated internship opportunities in London, UK (Capper St. & Stockley Park). This is not one specific vacancy. Projects are confirmed on a rolling basis, and one application puts you in consideration across multiple opportunities with participating teams and brands. You do not need to submit separate applications for each team or brand. If your profile aligns with a specific internship project, a recruiter may contact you directly with details and next steps. Applying does not guarantee an interview or placement. About Hasbro Hasbro creates joy and community through the magic of play. Across Hasbro, Wizards of the Coast and our partner network, teams bring iconic brands and new ideas to life through games, toys, entertainment and experiences. Teams and areas you may support Depending on confirmed projects and business needs, your internship could align to one or more areas such as: Account management and customer business planning Retail and e-commerce sales Sales operations and analytics Category and shelf strategy Trade planning and promotions Business development and new channels Examples of what you may work on Depending on your placement, you may: Analyze sell-in, sell-through, inventory and retail data to spot trends and opportunities. Support account plans, line reviews, sell-in presentations or promotional calendars for key customers. Build reports, dashboards or recaps that make sales performance and next steps clear. Research retailers, channels, competitors or shopper behaviour and summarize recommendations. Partner with Marketing, Brand, Finance, Supply Chain and customer teams to bring plans to market. Help improve a sales process, forecast or customer experience. What you bring You are currently enrolled in a university degree program in Business, Sales, Marketing, Economics, Analytics, Retail Management or a related degree program. Commercial curiosity and interest in how products reach retailers, partners and fans. Analytical thinking and comfort turning data into a clear recommendation. Clear communication, relationship-building and presentation skills. Familiarity with spreadsheets, presentation tools or customer relationship management systems is helpful. Work samples: No portfolio is required. Relevant analysis, case competitions, student organizations or coursework may be highlighted. Find your party: your global intern community Level up together. All interns join one global cohort connected through shared, structured onboarding and weekly learning and development. Undergraduate and graduate students are welcome to apply. We generally prioritize students entering the final year of their degree after the internship. Students at other eligible stages, including those with approximately two academic years remaining, are also encouraged to apply. Eligibility is based on program stage, not age. You can commit to the full program schedule and work model for the assigned location. You meet the work-authorization requirements stated below. Listed majors are examples. Related programs, coursework and transferable projects are welcome; previous internship experience is not required. If this work excites you but you do not match every preferred skill, we still encourage you to apply. We value potential, curiosity, willingness to learn and different paths to the same capability. Program details Program dates and length: June - August (10–12 WEEKS) Schedule: Full-time, 37.5 hours per week Work model: Most internships follow a hybrid schedule with three days per week in the office. Select roles may require five days per week onsite. The project-specific schedule will be shared before interviews. Locations: Capper St. and Stockley Park Opportunities may support Hasbro or Wizards of the Coast teams, depending on confirmed projects and business needs. Application deadline: APPLICATIONS WILL BE REVIEWED ON A ROLLING BASIS from November to March, as needed. Pay and benefits This is a paid internship. Hourly rates vary by degree level and year in school. A separate good-faith range is provided for each worksite. Relocation: Assistance is available to candidates who meet the applicable distance and other eligibility criteria. What happens after you apply Our Early Careers team reviews applications as internship projects are confirmed. If your profile aligns with a project, a recruiter will contact you with the specific team, project, location, work model, dates and pay. Selected candidates will interview with the hiring team. Timing may vary by project, so please keep your contact information and availability current. If another function more closely matches your skills and interests, we may consider you for it with your permission. You do not need to submit a duplicate application. Work authorization Applicants must be legally authorized to work in the United Kingdom. The Company is unable to provide immigration sponsorship for this position.
