Our benefits fall into three main categories: As soon as you join PwC, you’ll have access to a suite of free and discounted goods and services, which are available all year round*. In addition to these, you’ll also have access to a number of core benefits, funded by PwC, many of which you can enhance and add dependants to** And you’ll have the option throughout your PwC career to choose optional benefits through salary exchange*** Our benefits offering is an important part of your total reward package. Our benefits package is focused around five core areas: health and wellbeing; financial; lifestyle; protection against the unexpected and recognising you. *Benefits Plus benefits. **Core benefits. ***Optional salary sacrifice benefits through salary exchange (subject to contract type). We want to ensure that they are supported at every stage and feel confident balancing their work and home lives. We therefore provide enhanced parental pay to eligible parents. No matter your role, grade or location, the support we provide to working parents is available to all PwC employees from day one. Maternity and Adoption Leave, including in surrogacy arrangements Paternity Leave Shared Parental Leave Premature Births and Neonatal Care Leave Unpaid Parental Leave Miscarriage and Baby Loss Fertility leave Additional support Maternity and Adoption Leave, including in surrogacy arrangements Up to 22 weeks of full pay (known as PwC “payroll cash*”), inclusive of any statutory pay. Followed by up to 17 weeks of statutory pay, subject to eligibility. *Payroll cash is your base pay plus your benefits allowance, minus the benefits price for the benefits you’ve requested. Paternity Leave Up to four weeks of leave at full pay (called PwC “payroll cash*”). *Payroll cash is your base pay plus your benefits allowance, minus the benefits price for the benefits you’ve requested. Shared Parental Leave Up to 20 weeks of full pay (known as PwC “payroll cash*”), inclusive of any statutory shared parental pay. Entitlement to full pay is unaffected by the level of pay the employee’s partner may receive from their employer. Following the period of full pay, employees may be entitled to statutory shared parental pay for any remaining leave, depending on how the leave is shared with their partner or spouse. When combined with maternity, paternity or adoption leave, all eligible new parents can access up to 22 weeks of leave at full pay, depending on how they share their leave. *Payroll cash is your base pay plus your benefits allowance, minus the benefits price for the benefits you’ve requested. Premature Births and Neonatal Care Leave Up to 12 weeks at full pay (PwC “payroll cash*”) where babies arrive early or require neonatal care. This is in addition to other parental leave and pay, such as maternity and paternity. We waive the statutory requirement for 26 weeks’ service. *Payroll cash is your base pay plus your benefits allowance, minus the benefits price for the benefits you’ve requested. Unpaid Parental Leave Up to 18 weeks of unpaid leave per child before their 18th birthday, using a maximum of four weeks in any year. Miscarriage and Baby Loss We provide two weeks of leave at full pay (PwC “payroll cash*”) to anyone who experiences a miscarriage before the end of week 24 of their pregnancy (before any statutory entitlement to leave applies). Spouses and partners are also entitled to this leave. In the event of a baby loss after 24 weeks of pregnancy, we waive the statutory 26-week service requirement for parental bereavement leave and provide two weeks of leave at full pay. *Payroll cash is your base pay plus your benefits allowance, minus the benefits price for the benefits you’ve requested. Fertility leave Up to eight days of paid fertility leave to any employee undergoing fertility treatment. Up to two days of paid leave for employees supporting their partner or spouse through fertility treatment. Additional support We provide a wide range of additional support, including: A dedicated parents and carers hub, with information, resources, and parenting stories from our people. Internal networks for parents to connect, and share experiences and support. Free, specialist coaching to help employees prepare for parental leave, return to work and progress their career beyond. 20 funded back-up care sessions per year.
