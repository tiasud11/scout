A remote internship, also known as a virtual internship or online internship, is completed entirely online from anywhere in the world. With no physical office or place of work, you can conduct your internship from your home, a co-working space, a coffee shop, or anywhere else that has a stable WiFi connection and an environment suitable for you to complete your tasks. How does a remote internship work? Your remote internship will be conducted entirely online - from your onboarding and daily tasks and projects, right through to your program completion. You will use a variety of platforms and tools for internal and external communication and project management, along with software exclusive to your industry. You can expect your meetings to be via video calls rather than around a table, and there is a higher level of trust and independence required. This online working environment will give you the technical skills and initiative required to boost your CV and employability. Learn about the program process > Are remote internships worth it? Yes. Virtual internships can help students and graduates gain professional experience, build industry-specific skills, and strengthen their CV or resume. They also provide valuable exposure to remote working environments, which have become increasingly common across many industries. According to our alumni outcomes survey, 100% reported positive career outcomes and considered their program investment worthwhile. Do employers value remote internships? Yes. Employers generally value relevant work experience and transferable skills, regardless of whether the internship was completed remotely or in person. Completion of a virtual internship can demonstrate strong initiative, communication, adaptability, and professional experience which are all sought-after skills. Can a remote internship lead to a job? A remote internship can help you build professional skills, expand your network, and gain experience that may improve your future employment prospects. While a job offer is never guaranteed, our interns use their experience to strengthen future applications and career opportunities. Based on our alumni outcomes survey, 82% of surveyed alumni secured a first full-time job aligned with their international internship career field , while 100% reported positive career outcomes and considered their program investment worthwhile. What industries offer remote internships? Remote internships are available across a wide range of industries, including business, marketing, finance, engineering, human resources, nonprofit organizations, and many more. What skills can I develop through a virtual internship? A virtual internship can help you develop both industry-specific knowledge and transferable professional skills that are valued by employers across a wide range of sectors. Virtual internships also help participants build essential workplace competencies such as communication, teamwork, problem-solving, time management, adaptability, and professional etiquette. Because you'll be working remotely with colleagues and supervisors, you'll also gain experience using digital workplace tools and developing the remote working skills that are increasingly important in today's global workforce.
