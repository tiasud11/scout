Morgan Stanley Investment Management (MSIM), together with its investment advisory affiliates, has about $2 trillion in assets under management or supervision as of June 30, 2026. With more than 1,300 investment professionals worldwide, and 59 offices in 26 countries, MSIM is able to provide in-depth local knowledge and expertise while channelling the strength of our global presence and resources. Morgan Stanley Investment Management strives to provide outstanding long-term investment performance, outstanding service, and a comprehensive suite of investment management solutions to a diverse client base, which includes governments, institutions, corporations, and individuals worldwide. The Office of the COO supports the leadership team responsible for the day-to-day operations of Investment Management. Working closely with the Chief of Staff team and the MSIM Management team led by the EMEA MSIM Head, analysts help drive strategic and regulatory initiatives, enhance organizational effectiveness, and support senior management priorities. Roles in this team offer broad exposure to business management and operational leadership, while providing a unique opportunity to gain insight into the inner workings of a large Investment Management organization and network with investment and non-investment teams across MSIM. Placement and duration The Morgan Stanley Summer Analyst Programme in London runs for 10-weeks and offers you the opportunity to join a world class equity boutique including firm training and networking as well as on the desk training. Training Program London-based training begins in June with one week of intensive classroom training, including an introduction to Morgan Stanley, followed by team specific training. Throughout the internship, you will be exposed to business speakers and practical sessions as you continue to become familiar with the asset management industry. Roles and Responsibilities Supporting with preparing presentations for various Boards and Committees, as well as regulator meetings and industry forums Supporting the team with project management tasks including development of project plans, updating project reporting / trackers, etc. Working closely with the team on gathering, documenting and mapping business requirements Gathering critical information from meetings with various stakeholders and producing meeting minutes, actions trackers, risk logs etc. Mobilising ad-hoc short term / tactical projects Helping to analyse and track financials and budgets Support the Office Manager on delegated responsibilities and provide cover when OOO Assist with onboarding all new starters for all London offices and ad-hoc projects and events including space allocation, office /desk moves and social events Qualifications/Skills/Requirements: You are pursuing an undergraduate or master’s degree, minimum 2.1 with an expected graduation date between December 2027 and July 2028 You demonstrate a keen interest in the financial markets You have excellent communication skills You have an excellent academic background and fluency in English You are a strong team player; ability to collaborate with colleagues across MSIM and the broader organization You are a self-starter with the ability to work independently You are driven, highly motivated and result-focused You have strong organizational skills with the ability to manage time efficiently and effectively You are detail oriented and have the ability to multi-task and work efficiently within tight time constraints Application Process and Deadline We recruit on a rolling basis so application is encouraged as soon as possible.
