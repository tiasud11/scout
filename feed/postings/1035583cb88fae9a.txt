You'll manage timelines, budgets and internal and external stakeholders to deliver successful outcomes. 🌍 Supporting our global property strategy – Turning our longer term property ambitions into practical plans, ensuring our spaces meet the needs of our people and support Monzo's continued growth. 💪 Leading and developing a brilliant team – Managing six team members across all locations, setting clear priorities, supporting their development and building a scalable, high-performing function. 🤝 Collaborating across Monzo – Building strong relationships with Finance, Legal, TechOps and senior leadership to align priorities, influence decisions and deliver improvements. 💰 Managing budgets and suppliers – Overseeing property and facilities budgets, managing supplier relationships and ensuring we're delivering value for money across our portfolio. 🦺 Keeping our spaces safe and sustainable – Ensuring our workplaces meet health and safety requirements, managing compliance and working with the ESG team to deliver our Scope 1 and 2 environmental commitments. ✨ Continuously improving how we work – Using data and employee feedback to improve our spaces, streamline processes and build scalable ways of working.
