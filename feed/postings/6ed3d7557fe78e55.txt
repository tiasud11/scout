Salary: £27,898 per year Job Type: Placement (10 Months+) Job Roles: Consumer Products/FMCG Marketing PR and Communications Locations: Kingston upon Thames Job Description Our Unilever Industrial Placement (IP) Programme is designed for students who are completing a year in industry as part of their degree studies. In the UK, our IP programme is a 12-month development scheme, for which we recruit top-performing undergraduate talent, with fresh ideas who are eager to gain exposure to real-world challenges. All new joiners are given real responsibility from day one and have an opportunity to make a meaningful impact on the business. Alongside this, they receive one-to-one mentoring and support to aid their development and help them achieve their goals. These programmes provide a variety of experiences, enabling individuals to learn, grow, develop personally and professionally, build confidence and gain a comprehensive understanding of the business. As we receive a high volume of applications, please make sure to read the job descriptions on our website carefully and apply for the ONE function you are most interested in. Only your first application will be considered The Role A placement in Communications & Corporate Affairs puts you at the heart of helping one of the world's leading businesses build trust, strengthen reputation and create the conditions for growth. Our function brings together Communications, Corporate Affairs and Sustainability to influence the issues that matter most to Unilever, our brands and the stakeholders we serve. Applying to this role will place you within our Communications and/or Corporate Affairs teams. If you are interested in Sustainability, please note that there is a separate Sustainability role advertised. The work we do is focused on protecting and strengthening Unilever's reputation, helping the business navigate an increasingly complex external environment and ensuring our strategy, brands and leaders are visible, trusted and understood. From strategic storytelling and executive communications to reputation management, media relations, policy engagement and stakeholder advocacy, you'll work on real business challenges while developing skills in communications, influence, research, content creation, digital channels, campaigning and strategic thinking. This is a unique opportunity to gain exposure to some of the most important conversations shaping business, society and sustainability today while working alongside experienced communications and corporate affairs professionals. Where Could Your Placement Take You? One of the unique aspects of this placement is the breadth of opportunities available across Communications & Corporate Affairs. You could be placed within our Corporate Centre, helping shape Unilever's global reputation, public policy positions, media engagement, leadership communications, content strategy or internal communication programmes. You may also work alongside teams supporting areas such as Sustainability, Legal, Finance, Human Resources, Supply Chain or Digital & Technology. Alternatively, you could be embedded within one of Unilever's Business Groups, helping shape communications and corporate affairs activity closer to our brands, markets and consumers across Beauty & Wellbeing, Personal Care or Home Care. This means no two placements are the same. Whether you're developing executive communications, supporting reputation and policy programmes, creating content, managing stakeholder engagement, analysing emerging issues or helping deliver major campaigns, you'll gain first-hand experience of how communications, reputation and corporate affairs help drive business performance, growth and trust. What Could Your Day-To-Day Role Include? Supporting communications campaigns that build understanding, trust and engagement among internal and external stakeholders Creating content for digital, social media, web and internal communications channels Supporting media relations activity, thought leadership programmes and executive visibility initiatives Monitoring emerging issues, reputation trends and policy developments that could impact Unilever Conducting research and analysis to inform business decisions and stakeholder engagement strategies Supporting advocacy initiatives and engagement with political, industry and societal stakeholders Helping coordinate major campaigns, business moments, events and leadership communications Supporting the development of AI-enabled communications, digital engagement and content strategies Measuring communications effectiveness through data, insights and stakeholder feedback What Do We Look For? We're looking for curious, proactive and collaborative individuals who are excited by the power of communications, reputation and influence. You'll enjoy working with people, asking questions, solving problems and turning complex information into clear and engaging communications. You'll be comfortable managing multiple priorities, adapting to changing circumstances and bringing fresh thinking to challenges. With a strong performance culture, challenging goals and continuous feedback, we'll help you achieve your potential in an inclusive environment. You'll gain exposure to real business challenges where your energy, ideas and perspective can make a genuine impact. No specific degree subject is required, although studies in areas such as Communications, Journalism, Public Relations, Media, Politics, International Relations, Marketing and Business. Skills required include: Strong written and verbal communication skills Ability to build relationships and work effectively with a wide range of stakeholders Curiosity about business, society, public affairs and current affairs Strong organisational skills and attention to detail Ability to analyse information and identify key insights Confidence working both independently and as part of a team Adaptability and willingness to learn in a fast-paced environment Taking initiative and proactively identifying opportunities and solutions Microsoft 365 skills (PowerPoint, Excel, Word, Outlook and Teams) Please note that at Unilever we provide extensive development opportunities. While these skills are helpful at the start of the placement, you'll continue to develop them throughout your 12-month programme. Programme Details Location: Hybrid working with a minimum of 3 days required weekly at our London Blackfriars or Kingston office. Applications open: Monday 28 September 2026 Applications close: This is a rolling deadline, so we encourage you to apply as soon as possible to avoid disappointment in case applications close early. Start date: June 2027 Industrial Placement (IP) Application Process Please note: you will only progress to the next stage if you are successful in the previous stage. Stage 1 – Online Application & IP Simulation Complete an online application form before completing our interactive IP simulation, designed to give you insight into our organisation and the programme you’re applying for. This will prepare you for a series of situational judgement questions based on realistic business scenarios. Stage 2 – Online Assessments. Complete a numerical reasoning assessment, an online test that measures your ability to interpret and draw conclusions from numerical data and verbal Reasoning assessment an online test that measures your ability to understand and evaluate written information. Stage 3 – Digital Interview Complete a video interview. Please note, we do not use AI as a screening tool in our recruitment process therefore, your digital interview submission will be reviewed by a real person Stage 4 – Pre-Screening Interview Attend a virtual pre-screening interview with a member of our recruitment team. Stage 5 – In-Person Discovery Centre Attend an in-person discovery centre alongside fellow candidates. This will give you the opportunity to visit a Unilever office, meet and interview with business leaders, and work on function-related business challenges. What Can Unilever Offer You? Recruitment Fraud Cyber criminals advertise fake job adverts with prestigious employers as a way of stealing information or even defrauding individuals out of money. In the most sophisticated cases, they will set up fake websites, which have a similar address to companies like Unilever. They even conduct fake telephone interviews and then offer candidates a role with the proviso they pay a fee for background checks or to cover work visa costs. These types of attacks are becoming more common as more people are looking for employment in the economic climate. How is Unilever tackling this? Many of Unilever’s recruitment sites publish a warning to candidates about recruitment fraud. The Cyber Security team also proactively scan for signs of people setting up fake Unilever sites and act to close them down. What can I do? If you become aware of potential recruitment fraud, spot fake Unilever recruitment adverts or fake LinkedIn profiles, report them via Una Live Chat. Unilever does not accept responsibility or liability for any candidates who are financially impacted by recruitment fraud. Your vigilance is key!
