Job Description Why consider joining our PA Futures Programme: Women In Consulting? Experience a 3-day immersive introduction to the world of consulting, designed specifically for women in their first year at university (or second year of a four-year course). Gain insight into the consulting industry, develop core consulting skills, and work on a real-world business challenge. Network with PA consultants, graduates, and leaders, and learn about career pathways in consulting. Fast-track your future: all attendees will be shortlisted for our Summer Internship or Industrial Placement assessment event following the programme. What you can expect Interactive workshops on problem-solving, structured thinking, and storytelling. Panel events and roundtable sessions with consultants from across PA. A team case study project, culminating in presentations to a panel of consultants. Sessions on workplace etiquette, presentation skills, and career development. Support from mentors and the opportunity to build your professional network. Assessment process Application form Online Assessments Shortlisting Qualifications This opportunity is open to women in their first year of their degree Qualification: Undergraduate degree — we welcome first year applicants from all disciplines and have no specific grade or subject requirements for this role. Genuine interest in consulting and PA — we’re looking for individuals who are curious about the world of consulting and excited to explore what a career at PA could look like. Personal motivation and self-awareness — we value hearing about your experiences, interests, and what drives you. Whether it’s a challenge you’ve overcome, a hobby you love, or a cause you care about, we want to understand what makes you, you. Professional and reliable — show strong workplace etiquette by being punctual, dependable, and respectful of business expectations and office conduct. The Offer A unique 3-day insight programme in London in April 2027. A fast-track opportunity for future internships or placements. Access to PA’s networks, mentors, and resources.
