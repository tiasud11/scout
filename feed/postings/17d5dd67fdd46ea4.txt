Job Description We are looking for a strategically-minded Lead Analyst to join our Marketing Analytics team. This role is centered on owning reporting and deepening our understanding of what actually drives marketing growth, while helping the wider team pursue the opportunities and gaps that matter most. You'll focus on surfacing strategic opportunities from our Marketing Financials process and making them accessible for action across our analytics and marketing context. By using and streamlining emerging technologies and AI in the team, you will play an important role in the technical engine and processes that turn complex data into central resources, insights and clear prioritisation. In this role, you'll spot the horizontal opportunities that don't surface within any single Marketing Analytics pillar, build the business case for the ones that matter, and hand them to the right team to execute. You won't own every initiative, but you'll be the one who sees the whole board and helps decide where the leverage is. You'll also shape how we operate as a wider team, defining and improving how we collaborate and prioritise. You have the business acumen to know what makes a data product genuinely useful to marketing stakeholders at a strategic level and a real interest in enabling other analytics teams to pursue the opportunities you find. You'll keep data quality solid across a broad horizontal scope, building a clear, reflective view of past growth drivers while laying the foundation for future strategy, ideation, and understanding across the wider analytics team. Your Mission Your mission is to turn complex marketing data into a scalable engine for insights, growth driver explanation and strategic execution. You will own the technical set-up behind how we explain marketing performance, developing the automated tools (such as Streamlit apps, AI/agentic automation, and reporting tools) that make the drivers of our growth clear and easy to act on. You will further enhance productivity and prioritisation within the wider Marketing Analytics team leveraging these same technologies into effective and streamlined processes such as semantic layers, impact calculations and AI-skill alignment across teams. Rather than just reporting the financials, you'll focus on the why to pinpoint the highest-value opportunities within our Marketing Financials process, working closely with the wider marketing, analytics, product, and regional teams to carry those opportunities through to execution and improvement. You'll also use your cross-team overview to strengthen how the wider Marketing Analytics team works, so that we prioritise, manage, and deliver initiatives effectively.
