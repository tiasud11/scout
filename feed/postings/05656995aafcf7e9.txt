We are seeking leaders with recent MBA or technically focused Master’s degrees to join us in innovating cutting edge solutions. What to expect during your program You will have the opportunity to own and drive results, transforming the customer experience via immediate placement into high-impact teams. You will own key strategic initiatives focused on AI, automation, advanced analytics, digital intimacy and product personalization while collaborating with leaders and experts in Digital, Retail Distribution, Consumer Products, and Marketing to deliver agile solutions. You will gain high-visibility exposure and a strong leader network through active executive sponsorship, and ongoing support throughout the program experience. Your development will be accelerated through hands-on experience, professional domain training and mentorship from senior leaders in technical subject matter areas like Data & Analytics, AI, Technology, and more. You will be a part of an inclusive culture committed to operating at the highest ethical standards. Program requirements Graduate degree in related field Recent (within the last 2 years) MBA graduate with technically focused undergraduate degree (i.e., STEM) OR Recent technically focused Master’s graduate (i.e., Analytics, Innovation, Digital area, etc.) with business undergraduate degree 3–5 years of relevant professional experience Demonstrated leadership experience, a history of delivering strong results, and high aspirations and motivation for career progression to senior leadership roles Application deadlines Full-time roles are posted in the fall of each year.
