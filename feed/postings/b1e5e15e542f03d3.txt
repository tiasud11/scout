We’re great believers in powerful, real-time analytics and empowerment of the wider business. Join a team where your ideas can rapidly transform into reality, leveraging our cutting-edge technology and agile ways of working. Our credit analysts and managers are fully embedded into a product squad alongside Product Managers, Engineers, Designers, Marketers, Customer Researchers, Data Scientists etc. This enables us to solve customer problems in a fast paced and innovative way. Your day-to-day: Own Portfolio Monitoring & Forecasting: Build and oversee robust performance frameworks to monitor and forecast both risk and revenue metrics, ensuring our rapidly growing portfolio scales sustainably. Drive Contractual Pricing Strategies: Architect and optimise an ongoing risk-based pricing program that balances commercial viability and fair customer outcomes. Fuel Engagement & Retention Loops: Design and deploy sophisticated credit strategies for lifecycle events, including product upgrades, engagement incentives, and balance transfer propositions that deepen customer loyalty. Execute Data-Driven Loss Mitigation: Develop proactive risk management frameworks—such as credit limit decrease strategies—to safely mitigate losses and support financially vulnerable customers. Lead and Nurture Talent: Manage, coach, and scale a high-performing team of 3 credit analysts, fostering a culture of technical excellence, continuous learning, and strong commercial focus. Cross-Functional Squad Collaboration: Act as the embedded credit risk expert within a multi-disciplinary squad, working side-by-side with Product, Engineering, Design, Marketing, Customer Research, and Risk & Control. Cross-functional Leadership: Build deep collaborative ties with wider credit functions and manage relationships with 2nd Line Credit Risk, Legal, and Compliance, while translating complex data into clear narratives for executive-level stakeholders.
