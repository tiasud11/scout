Job Description As a KYC Operations, you will play a critical role in ensuring that Wise adheres to regulatory and compliance requirements. You will begin with a training program designed to teach you the complexities of Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD). Upon successful completion of your training and accreditation, you will be responsible for analysing customer activities, behaviour patterns, and ensuring that our KYC records are kept up to date according to our internal standards. This role is a great opportunity for fresh graduates who are interested in starting a career and deepening their knowledge in the KYC domain.
