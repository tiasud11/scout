Salary: £27,898 per year Job Type: Placement (10 Months+) Job Roles: Information Technology Locations: Wirral Kingston upon Thames Job Description Our Unilever Industrial Placement (IP) Programme is designed for students who are completing a year in industry as part of their degree studies. In the UK, our IP programme is a 12-month development scheme, for which we recruit top-performing undergraduate talent, with fresh ideas who are eager to gain exposure to real-world challenges. All new joiners are given real responsibility from day one and have an opportunity to make a meaningful impact on the business. Alongside this, they receive one-to-one mentoring and support to aid their development and help them achieve their goals. These programmes provide a variety of experiences, enabling individuals to learn, grow, develop personally and professionally, build confidence and gain a comprehensive understanding of the business. A career at Unilever offers the opportunity to work at global scale while helping shape the future of some of the world's most recognised brands. Our people are at the heart of everything we do, bringing curiosity, creativity and passion to solve real business challenges and make a meaningful impact every day. *As we receive a high volume of applications, please make sure to read the job descriptions on our website carefully and apply for the ONE function you are most interested in. Only your first application will be considered* THE ROLE Technology is now an integrated part of all our lives – employees and consumers alike – and as a result it’s at the heart of everything we do at Unilever, opening new possibilities and propelling us into the future. Technology touches everything from the sourcing of sustainable ingredients, to manufacturing and distribution of products to customers, to how we market and track the performance of our brands in the digital world. Our people have the opportunity to help build a bright future for our world, making a big impact every day, on a local level, across Europe and the globe. We have a proven tradition of developing our purpose-driven leaders and encouraging innovative thinking. Some of our positions you could be allocated to include: Developing cloud products that enable rapid delivery of IT systems, joining a team that delivers and scales cloud technologies across the organisation, and creating flexible cloud hosting solutions that keep services running 24/7. A data analyst within our Data and Analytics team, helping to deliver insights, analytics, and recommendations to teams around the world. Support in implementing AI to help ensure Unilever’s campaigns are leading edge Play a critical role in driving the plan for landing capabilities for consumer engagement, innovation, and marketing performance, including expanding the use of AI and automation, partnering closely with the Marketing leaders and Products to drive user centricity. What do we look for? Our Technology focused Industrial Placements are specially designed to give you the experience you’re hungry for – before you even graduate! You will have a passion for working with the latest technology innovations along with some impressive strategic thinking, and business awareness. With a strong performance culture, challenging goals, and continuous feedback, we will support you to achieve your potential in an inclusive environment. You will thrive by gaining exposure to leading edge Cloud technology and how technology systems solve real business challenges where your energy and fresh thinking will have an impact. First-hand experience- it’s the fastest way to learn! Students must have completed the penultimate year of their undergraduate degree by the time they start their placement and be returning to study after their placement. Our Technology Industrial Placements are a chance to showcase your thinking and skills, with total support from a line manager and a buddy. Skills required include: Programming languages – Python etc. Cloud Hosting Cyber Security Data Ethics Artificial Intelligence Microsoft Office skills (Excel, Outlook, Word, PowerPoint) Please note, that at Unilever, we have development programmes, the skills above are skills that would be good to have at the beginning however you will develop them over the course of the 12 months. Programme details Location: Hybrid working with a minimum of 3 days required weekly either at our Kingston office or Port Sunlight (Wirral) – depending on where you are based. Applications open: Monday 28 September 2026 Applications close: This is a rolling deadline, so we encourage you to apply as soon as possible to avoid disappointment in case applications close early. Start date: June 2027 Industrial Placement (IP) Application Process Please note: you will only progress to the next stage if you are successful in the previous stage. Stage 1 – Online Application & IP Simulation Complete an online application form before completing our interactive IP simulation, designed to give you insight into our organisation and the programme you’re applying for. This will prepare you for a series of situational judgement questions based on realistic business scenarios. Stage 2 – Online Assessments. Complete a numerical reasoning assessment, an online test that measures your ability to interpret and draw conclusions from numerical data and verbal Reasoning assessment an online test that measures your ability to understand and evaluate written information. Stage 3 – Digital Interview Complete a video interview. *Please note, we do not use AI as a screening tool in our recruitment process therefore, your digital interview submission will be reviewed by a real person* Stage 4 – Pre-Screening Interview Attend a virtual pre-screening interview with a member of our recruitment team. Stage 5 – In-Person Discovery Centre Attend an in-person discovery centre alongside fellow candidates. This will give you the opportunity to visit a Unilever office, meet and interview with business leaders, and work on function-related business challenges. What Can Unilever offer YOU? A competitive Salary of £27,898.00 End of Placement Bonus Subsidised Gym Memberships - availability varies by site Discounted Staff Shop Opportunity to work directly with our renowned and exciting brands in a flexible and hybrid working environment. 28 days (pro rata) of holiday inclusive of bank holidays. Once you have completed 12 worked weeks, under the Agency Workers Regulation your holiday entitlement will increase to 33 days inclusive of bank holidays, pro rata for the term of this placement. Opportunity to join one of our Future Careers Development Teams such as Outreach, Digi, Community & Wellbeing and many more! By joining a team, you can grow business-critical skills, develop Unilever Behaviours, and make a meaningful contribution to Future Careers. A dedicated buddy to help you onboard and learn about the business. An experienced Line Manager to develop your knowledge and understanding across the year. Connections with the Graduates (UFLPs) who can share helpful tips on how to be successful at Unilever. Recruitment Fraud Cybercriminals advertise fake job adverts with prestigious employers as a way of stealing information or even defrauding individuals out of money. In the most sophisticated cases, they will set up fake websites, which have a similar address to companies like Unilever. They even conduct fake telephone interviews and then offer candidates a role with the proviso they pay a fee for background checks or to cover work visa costs. These types of attacks are becoming more common as more people are looking for employment in the economic climate. How is Unilever tackling this? Many of Unilever’s recruitment sites publish a warning to candidates about recruitment fraud. The Cyber Security team also proactively scan for signs of people setting up fake Unilever sites and acts to close them down. What can I do? If you become aware of potential recruitment fraud, spot fake Unilever recruitment adverts or fake LinkedIn profiles, report them via Una Live Chat. Unilever does not accept responsibility or liability for any candidates who are financially impacted by recruitment fraud. Your vigilance is key!
