About the business area Corporate and Institutional Banking helps some of the world’s largest companies grow and succeed by offering investment and financial solutions. Working across markets, sectors and borders, you’ll help deliver the insight, connectivity and support that enable clients to navigate change and unlock new opportunities Who you are You’re curious, analytical and motivated to learn in a fast-paced environment. You’ll be a strong fit if you enjoy working with numbers, spotting patterns, communicating clearly and contributing to a team. You’ll need analytical thinking, attention to detail, resilience, strong written and verbal communication, teamwork and the confidence to take ownership of your work. An interest in transactions, financial statements, research, modelling and client materials will help you succeed. You don’t need to come from a finance background or arrive already fluent in investment banking — if you bring curiosity, drive and a willingness to learn, we’ll help you build the technical knowledge. What matters is your potential, your judgement and how you work with others under pressure. What you'll do You’ll join a specialist team and work alongside experienced colleagues on live transactions, products or sectors. Depending on your placement, you’ll support teams in Strategic Advisory, Corporate Finance or Financing Solutions, and collaborate with banking teams across different markets to help deliver for corporates, private equity firms and financial institutions. You could research market conditions, analyse company performance, review financial statements, support credit analysis and help prepare client materials such as pitchbooks, investment memos and presentations. You’ll also build your technical skills through financial modelling and valuation work, while learning how deals move from idea to execution. It’s real exposure to live work, with support from people who do it every day. What you'll learn You’ll build your investment banking foundation through regulated training where required, a global graduate induction and tailored technical development for your role. You’ll learn how deals are structured, how financial statements and valuation techniques are used in practice, and how different financing solutions support clients. Depending on location, you may also work towards industry qualifications such as FCA or FINRA exams, alongside residential technical and behavioural training led by external experts. Just as importantly, you’ll strengthen your professional skills—communication, judgement, teamwork and attention to detail—in a fast-moving environment. By the end of the programme, you’ll be better equipped to navigate complex financial situations and contribute with confidence. Where you'll work You’ll work in Corporate and Institutional Banking, within a specialist investment banking team focused on sectors, products or transactions. Depending on your placement, that could be Strategic Advisory, Corporate Finance or Financing Solutions. It’s a high-performing, team-based environment where analysts and bankers work closely together and where collaboration across global offices is part of everyday life. You can expect a professional office setting, a steep learning curve and a culture that values precision, teamwork and shared delivery.
