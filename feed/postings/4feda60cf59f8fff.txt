Explaining Monzo Business's proposition, product capabilities and application process clearly, helping customers understand their next step without providing financial, tax, legal or regulatory advice. Qualifying and prioritising opportunities, handling objections and following up consistently to help customers progress through their journey. Helping shape our Spain sales playbook, including messaging, qualification, objection handling, scripts and customer journeys. Help create and refine Spanish-language scripts, FAQs, objection handling and sales processes. Deliver outstanding service at every touchpoint: via phone, video, email, text or digital channels.
