Closed for applications < Back < Back [+] Read More Frequently asked questions What does 'entry level' mean? Entry level roles are aimed at school and college leavers, career changers, and those looking to enter the workforce or retrain in a new area. These programmes provide a combination of practical experience, structured training and development opportunities, and may include professional qualifications to help you build a successful career. Do I need a degree to apply for entry level opportunities at PwC? No, our entry level programmes are designed for those without a university degree, focusing on potential, motivation and transferable skills rather than academic background. Are PwC’s entry level programmes open to career changers? Yes. Entry level opportunities are open to people of all ages, including those considering a career change, as long as they meet the programme’s eligibility criteria. What’s the difference between entry level roles and Flying Start? The main difference is how you combine work and study. Flying Start Degree Programme Flying Start programmes also combine university study with paid placements at PwC, giving you practical experience alongside your degree. These programmes do not follow the apprenticeship framework. We offer Flying Start Accounting degrees in partnership with several universities across the UK. Entry-Level Programmes Entry-level programmes are full-time roles at PwC and do not involve studying for a university degree. Instead, you'll work as a PwC employee from day one while completing a professional qualification or apprenticeship alongside your role. PwC funds all training and qualification costs, and you'll receive a full-time salary throughout the programme. How does PwC support diversity and access at entry level? Programmes like New World. New Skills are designed to widen access to professional careers by providing insight and experience to students from under‑represented backgrounds.
