These programmes could be right for you if: You are comfortable and enjoy working with numbers and technology. You build strong relationships and thrive in a team. You are curious to learn the financial management behind every type of organisation. What is Audit, Accounting, Tax and Finance? If you start your career in Audit, Accounting, Actuarial, Tax or Finance, you’ll gain a unique perspective on some of the world’s most influential, most innovative, and most dynamic organisations. Every organisation’s ability to thrive and survive depends on its ability to finance its operations, manage budgets, mitigate risk, comply with regulations, and pay taxes, bills and wages. So whether it’s a start-up business, a multinational corporation, a city council or a charity, every client we work with is measured on its financial performance. That performance data, and the insight you gain from it, tells the story of how an organisation is delivering on its goals. It drives strategies, impacts reputations and shapes the future. By helping our clients to collect, manage and interpret that data accurately, we provide the clarity and expertise to operate effectively, act responsibly and make the right decisions. We help organisations to navigate a rapidly changing world of risk, regulation, legislation, and technological transformation. We help them to become more agile, efficient and profitable. We increase their resilience, sustainability and security. By doing so, we contribute to the transparency and trust that underpins the global economy. At PwC, you’ll get an incredible education in the fundamentals of business. Developing your understanding of finance and technology, you’ll work with clients to solve complex problems that have a real-world impact. And most of our people gain a professional qualification. What you'll do Analytical and investigative work Whether you are auditing financial statements, modelling statistics, reviewing regulations or identifying tax implications, you’ll be using technology to investigate all details of data, systems or processes and turning that analysis into insight. Build client and stakeholder relationships We can’t do our work in a silo. So a key part of your role will involve building relationships and engaging with clients, regulators, shareholders and stakeholders, at all levels. Most of all, you’ll be learning from a network of PwC colleagues, working in close-knit, multi-skilled teams. Provide insight, challenge and recommendations Much of your work will involve interpreting rules, regulations or data and putting them into real-life context for your clients. You’ll challenge clients and make recommendations, always backed up by facts, helping them to make smart, evidence-based decisions. Actuarial Services Audit and Assurance​ Risk and Compliance ​ Tax Services​ Finance​ On our Actuarial Services programme, you’ll learn how to analyse and manage financial risks for clients through statistical modelling and data analysis. Actuaries provide valuable insights and recommendations to help businesses make informed decisions and mitigate potential risks. So, you’ll turn complex data into clear findings, presenting it to clients in simple, easy‑to‑understand language. Discover the breadth of work delivered across our actuarial business. You’ll study with the Institute and Faculty of Actuaries (IFoA), learning how to use complex financial and statistical theories to weigh up the risks and estimate the odds of what the future will bring. Once you’ve completed the exams, you’ll then be fully qualified and a Fellow of the IFoA. If Actuarial is your preference, you’ll need to put this as your first choice when completing your application in order to be considered for a role within this business area. Institute & Faculty of Actuaries (IFoA) Actuaries are problem solvers and strategic thinkers, who use their mathematical skills to help measure the probability and risk of future events. They use these skills to predict the financial impact of these events on a business and their clients. When you join us, we'll support you through your studies to become a qualified Actuary. The qualification is prestigious, and it won't be easy. It'll take dedication and commitment from you to succeed, including studying in your own time. We'll support you with first class tuition from our chosen provider and access to the tools you'll need to succeed. You'll work with a Study Mentor to agree the path and pace you'll take through the qualification. They'll guide you on how to approach your studies and were in the same position as you are not long ago. You can learn more about the syllabus and structure on the Institute & Faculty of Actuaries' website. On our Audit and Assurance programmes, you’ll get a backstage pass to some of the most interesting and dynamic organisations in the world. Audit at PwC is fast-moving, stimulating and full of opportunity, few careers offer such accelerated responsibility and client exposure. Collaboration is fundamental to everything we do, so you’ll learn from supportive colleagues, senior leaders, clients and experts across our global network. The people you work with and grow alongside makes Audit at PwC truly special. To do our work, we need to understand how a client’s business functions, so our people get an incredible grounding in all kinds of industries and sectors. You’ll work directly with clients, experiencing everything from the warehouse to the boardroom. You’ll see how CEOs and CFOs work, and gain a unique perspective on how different organisations do business and deliver value. When an organisation makes financial, sustainability and performance statements, we are trusted by its board, its shareholders and stakeholders, government regulators and, crucially, the markets, to ensure that the data is fair and accurate. Our teams verify the credibility and reliability of all kinds of data, creating transparency and trust by bringing independent rigour to the information that underpins the economy. We’re redefining the future of Audit and embedding the latest technology into every aspect of our work. You’ll work with cutting-edge digital tools and AI, while helping clients ensure they have the systems, internal controls, governance policies and risk management processes in place to deliver information accurately and effectively. Every day brings new opportunities to stretch yourself, take ownership and enhance your expertise, opening up a whole range of potential career paths.
