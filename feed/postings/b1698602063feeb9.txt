Higherin is the next-level careers platform with you at the heart. Supporting students to get higher in their careers, and employers hiring exceptional talent. Jump To: What's Higherin all about? Meet Higherin Academy Last Updated: 20/08/2026 We still remember those days frantically trying to figure out what we actually wanted to do with our career. Sound familiar? Back then, finding decent work experience felt impossible. The few job opportunities out there were either unpaid, irrelevant, or seemed to go to people who "knew someone." We were just three university students who got frustrated enough to do something about it. That's why Higherin exists. We're not just trying to match you with jobs (though we do love doing that). We're here to make sure you actually feel ready to smash those applications, nail those interviews, and walk into the careers world knowing you belong there. Every feature we build, every employer we partner with, every piece of content we create, it all comes back to one simple mission: helping you get to where you want to be, with the confidence to thrive when you get there. Here's to your next chapter! Ali, Ollie & Chris Higherin Founders What's Higherin all about? A GUIDED CAREER COMPANION Higherin is more than your standard jobs website. Our platform learns, grows and evolves with you as you embark on your career journey aged 14 through to 24+. With apprenticeships , internships , placements and graduate jobs all in one place. YOUR CAREER, PERSONALISED Your intuitive dashboard learns your specific interests and experiences, matching you with only relevant job roles. So the more you use it, the more personalised it becomes (kinda like your TikTok FYP). Search for, apply, and track all your jobs in one handy place - so no more doom scrolling and missed deadlines. COMMUNITY AT THE HEART We’ve always championed student voices, and host thousands of unfiltered job reviews and insider content created by people like you who have been there and done it. So you gain a behind-the-scenes look into what work experience at an employer is actually like before you apply. EQUAL OPPORTUNITIES We’re passionate about ensuring career opportunities are available equally to all students, regardless of your background. Higherin only partners with employers who offer proper, paid opportunities. And we listen to you to continuously improve and create positive change in the early careers world. Only The Best We partner with hundreds of top employers in the UK to bring you the most rewarding work experience schemes, proven by our annual rankings.
