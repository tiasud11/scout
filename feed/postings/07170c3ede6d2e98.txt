Investor Login Back All Insights White Paper Demystifying Generative AI: Key Insights for Investors February 2, 2025 4 MIN Artificial Intelligence Enterprise Software Key Generative AI Insights for Investors Generative AI (Gen AI) is more than a technological leap – it’s a structural transformation reshaping how businesses operate and grow. This white paper explores how Gen AI is redefining enterprise software, enhancing productivity and presenting compelling opportunities for long-term investment. What Is Generative AI? Gen AI refers to artificial intelligence systems capable of creating new content—text, images, code and more. Unlike traditional predictive AI, Gen AI models can generate original outputs, enabling next-gen collaboration between humans and machines. Key Drivers of Generative AI Development: Breakthroughs in deep learning Exponential increases in computing power Availability of vast training datasets Why Software Companies Can Benefit Gen AI’s third wave of value creation is expected to benefit enterprise software companies with: Unique access to proprietary, non-public datasets Installed solutions and established customer base Ownership of the end-user relationship Capacity to quantify and deliver ROI through AI integration Download the white paper for deeper Generative AI insights.
