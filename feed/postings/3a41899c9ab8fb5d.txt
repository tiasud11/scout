Featured Sign up for our Talent Community Loading Results No Match Found View All Results Recruitment agencies At PwC, we source candidates directly and we'll use a specified list of preferred suppliers to help support us in sourcing candidates for particular roles, if they are invited to do so. We don't accept any speculative CVs from recruitment agencies or suppliers who are not part of our preferred supplier list. We only pay agency fees where we have a signed agreement in place and that agency has been previously contacted and directed by a member of our recruitment team. We do not pay agency fees when speculative and unsolicited CVs are submitted to PwC or any of our employees other than via our careers website and through our recruitment process. If this is not adhered to, PwC reserve the right to contact these candidates directly and have discussions with them without paying any agency fees. Submission of any unsolicited CVs or proposals to PwC will be deemed evidence of full and unlimited acceptance of our agency policy.
