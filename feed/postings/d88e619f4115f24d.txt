Take on a role where your ideas, expertise and ambition can make a real difference, helping shape a secure and evolving energy future. As a Software Engineering Graduate, you'll be part of a collaborative engineering team delivering solutions that support traders, analysts and risk specialists across the business. You'll gain hands-on experience across software development, testing, cloud technologies and DevOps, while learning from experienced professionals who are invested in your growth. From developing new features to solving real-world technical challenges, you'll help create secure, scalable and maintainable applications while building the skills needed for a successful career in technology. WHAT WILL YOU DO Design, develop and test software features for business-critical Trading IT applications. Write automated tests that help ensure reliable, high-quality software releases. Contribute to modern CI/CD pipelines and agile delivery practices. Collaborate with software engineers, architects, business analysts and QA specialists to deliver innovative technology solutions. Participate in code reviews, receiving and providing feedback to continuously improve engineering standards. Learn and apply secure software development practices across all stages of the development lifecycle. Use industry-leading tools, including AI-assisted development technologies such as GitHub Copilot, while maintaining responsibility for code quality and security. Investigate and resolve technical issues, helping improve system performance, resilience and user experience. Build your understanding of energy trading, risk management and the commercial operations that our technology enables. WHAT WILL YOU BRING We're looking for curious, ambitious graduates who enjoy solving problems and are excited by technology's ability to create real business impact. You'll ideally have: A Master’s or Bachelor’s degree, completed or due to be completed before the programme starts, in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Mathematics, Physics, Engineering or another numerate discipline. Experience writing software through university projects, internships, placements, personal projects, hackathons or open-source contributions. Knowledge of at least one programming language such as Python, C# or Java. An understanding of software engineering fundamentals including version control, automated testing and agile development practices. An interest in cloud technologies, data platforms and modern engineering approaches. Strong analytical thinking and the ability to break complex problems into manageable solutions. The confidence to explain technical concepts to different audiences and work collaboratively within a team environment. A growth mindset, a willingness to learn and a genuine interest in developing your technical expertise. Applications are open until 16 December 2026. After the application deadline, no further submissions will be accepted. ABOUT US SEFE is an international energy company anchored in Europe, delivering energy solutions that ensure reliable and affordable supply. Our activities span the entire energy value chain – from origination and trading to sales, transport and storage. With decades of trading expertise and a growing LNG portfolio, SEFE is one of Europe’s leading suppliers to industrial customers, providing more than 200 TWh of gas and power each year. We supply over 50,000 clients, from small businesses to municipalities and multinational organisations. By investing in clean energies, we support our customers on their decarbonisation journey and contribute to the energy transition. SEFE employs over 2,000 people worldwide and is owned by the Federal Government of Germany. Securing energy – now and for the future.
