Key Responsibilities Set Product Marketing Strategy for a set of products. You'll define a one-year Product Marketing strategy for a major area of Business Banking, connecting customer insight, market opportunity, and commercial priorities into a clear direction for the product area, rather than one individual product, with measurable business outcomes. Develop differentiated positioning & messaging: You'll create compelling positioning, messaging, and value propositions that differentiate Monzo in the market and resonate with different customer segments. You'll build messaging frameworks that Product, Sales, Integrated Marketing, and broader marketing teams use consistently across launches and customer journeys. Drive competitive intelligence and market insights : You’ll build and maintain the market view for your product area. You'll become the recognised authority on your customer segment and competitive landscape, building a coherent view of the market from customer research, commercial data, competitor intelligence, and Sales insight. Your recommendations will shape product strategy, positioning, go-to-market, and commercial priorities across the Business Banking collective. Shape product strategy across markets. You'll partner closely with Product, Research, Design, and Data to identify customer problems, market opportunities, and competitive gaps that influence roadmap priorities. Your customer and market insight will shape what we build, not just how we bring it to market. Set the go-to-market strategy for priority products and markets . You'll define what audiences we prioritise, why they would choose Monzo, what needs to be true for them to switch and stay, and what success looks like - giving Product, Sales, Marketing, and local market teams a shared strategic direction for how the product enters or scales in market. Own product representation across the customer journey. You'll define the core proposition and value messages that should carry through key customer and sales touchpoints, so customers consistently understand why the product matters. You'll partner with teams across Product, Sales, Marketing, CRM, and local markets to make sure the customer experience reflects the agreed positioning. Enable localisation: You'll work with local market teams to determine what should remain globally consistent and what should adapt locally, ensuring positioning, customer journeys, and launches resonate in each market while strengthening the overall Monzo Business Banking proposition.
