As a Transfer Pricing Manager within the Group Tax squad, you will report directly to the International Tax & Transfer Pricing Lead, taking end-to-end ownership of Monzo’s transfer pricing operations, policies, and documentation. This role is ideal for a qualified accountant or tax advisor looking to make a significant impact in a fast-paced fintech environment. We welcome candidates making their first move out of practice, as well as those with previous in-house experience. 🔑You’ll play a key role by... Transfer Pricing Documentation: Leading the preparation, maintenance, and ongoing updates of Master Files, Local Files, and Country-by-Country Reporting (CbCR) across all operating jurisdictions. TP Policy Design & Implementation: Assisting in designing, implementing, and updating intercompany pricing policies to support business growth, new product launches, and international expansion. Intercompany Agreements & Operational TP: Collaborating with Legal, Finance, and Treasury to ensure intercompany agreements match economic realities and operational transfer pricing calculations/adjustments are executed accurately. Expansion & Cross-Functional Projects: Partnering closely with the International Tax Manager and International Tax & Transfer Pricing Lead on key group projects, European expansion initiatives, and cross-border structuring. Audit & Defense: Serving as a key point of contact for tax authority enquiries and audits relating to transfer pricing matters globally. Cross-Functional Advisory: Providing clear, pragmatic transfer pricing advice to internal stakeholders across Business, Finance, and Product teams. 🤩 We’d love to hear from you if… You hold a professional qualification: Qualified accountant (ACA/ACCA) and/or tax advisor (CTA/ADIT or equivalent). You have a strong technical background in transfer pricing. Candidates making their first move directly from professional practice (Big 4 / Top 10 / specialist TP firms) are strongly encouraged to apply, as are those with existing in-house experience. You are Proactive, have a hands-on mindset with the ability to operate autonomously and translate complex transfer pricing concepts for non-tax stakeholders. 🙌 What’s in it for you 💰 £77,300 - £95,000➕ Incentive Awards tied to your performance. 📍This role can be based in our London office, but we're open to distributed working within the UK (with ad hoc meetings in London) ⏰ We offer flexible working hours and trust you to work enough hours to do your job well, and at times that suit you and your team. 📚 £1,000 learning budget each year to use on books, training courses and conferences. 🏡 We will set you up to work from home; all employees are given Macbooks and for fully remote workers we will provide extra support for your work-from-home setup. ➕ Plus lots more!
