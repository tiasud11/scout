Take on a role where your analytical thinking, curiosity and ambition can make a real difference, helping shape a secure and evolving energy future. As a Finance Graduate, you'll rotate across a broad range of finance activities, gaining valuable experience in accounting operations, financial reporting, governance, business analysis and finance transformation projects. You'll develop a strong understanding of how finance supports business performance while building practical experience in financial processes, controls, systems and stakeholder management. This is an excellent opportunity for graduates who enjoy understanding business challenges, working with data and collaborating with others to support better commercial decision-making. You'll gain exposure to both operational and strategic finance activities while developing analytical, technical and project management skills within a leading European energy business. WHAT WILL YOU DO Gain hands-on experience across Accounting Operations, including Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, General Ledger and Master Data Management activities, helping to maintain accurate financial records and efficient financial processes. Contribute to month-end and year-end close activities, including journal preparation, balance sheet reconciliations and financial data reviews to ensure accurate and timely reporting. Analyse financial and operational data, preparing reports and management information that support business performance monitoring and decision-making. Assist with documenting business processes, gathering requirements and identifying opportunities to improve efficiency, controls and ways of working. Contribute to Finance Systems and Transformation initiatives, supporting project activities, testing, training and change management programmes. Participate in finance projects and cross-functional initiatives, helping to coordinate activities, monitor progress and support successful delivery of project objectives. Assist with maintaining finance policies, procedures and internal controls, helping to strengthen governance, compliance and risk management frameworks. Support internal and external audit activities by preparing information, tracking actions and collaborating with stakeholders to address findings and recommendations. Work closely with colleagues across Finance, Treasury, Procurement, IT and Audit teams, building an understanding of how different business functions work together. Take ownership of your professional development, applying new knowledge and contributing fresh ideas to support continuous improvement across the Finance function. WHAT WILL YOU BRING We're looking for curious, commercially-aware graduates who are eager to build a career in finance within a dynamic international business. You'll enjoy analysing information, solving problems and using data to support decision-making, while bringing a proactive approach to learning and development. You'll ideally have: A Bachelor's degree (completed or due to be completed before the programme starts), preferably in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Mathematics, Business, Data Analytics or another numerate discipline. A strong academic background and a genuine interest in finance, business performance and commercial operations. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to interpret information, identify issues and recommend effective solutions. Excellent organisational and planning skills, with the ability to manage priorities and meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment. Strong communication and relationship-building skills, enabling effective collaboration with colleagues and stakeholders across the business. Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, particularly Excel, Word and PowerPoint, together with an interest in finance systems, process improvement and business transformation initiatives. Curiosity about emerging technologies, including Artificial Intelligence, and an interest in data analysis, modelling and tools such as Python to improve financial processes and business outcomes. Adaptability, resilience and a commitment to continuous learning. Experience gained through internships, placements, part-time work, university projects or extracurricular activities related to finance, business or project work would be advantageous but is not essential. ABOUT US SEFE is an international energy company anchored in Europe, delivering energy solutions that ensure reliable and affordable supply. Our activities span the entire energy value chain – from origination and trading to sales, transport and storage. With decades of trading expertise and a growing LNG portfolio, SEFE is one of Europe’s leading suppliers to industrial customers, providing more than 200 TWh of gas and power each year. We supply over 50,000 clients, from small businesses to municipalities and multinational organisations. By investing in clean energies, we support our customers on their decarbonisation journey and contribute to the energy transition. SEFE employs over 2,000 people worldwide and is owned by the Federal Government of Germany. Securing energy – now and for the future.
