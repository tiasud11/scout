Begin your career journey in Asset Management Private Markets: Join M&G’s eight-week Summer Internship Join us on 28 June 2027 for an eight week internship to gain hands-on experience, develop technical and professional skills, and build a strong understanding of Private Markets within Asset Management. Your journey will start with a cohort-wide induction, with focused insight sessions to help you learn more about M&G, network with colleagues, and begin developing your professional skills. You will take part in a structured programme, including: Team Placement: Join one team for eight weeks where you will take on meaningful work, contribute to live projects, and shadow experienced colleagues to gain a solid understanding of Private Markets. Group Project: An opportunity to collaborate with your peers on a live strategic challenge, developing teamwork and problem solving skills and presenting your recommendations back to the business at the end of the programme. Professional Development: Take part in interactive learning sessions and networking opportunities designed to grow your expertise and build your professional network. Your Support Network: Regular check-ins with the Early Careers Team, your manager, and a buddy will ensure you have all the support you need during the programme. This internship is your first step toward a career in Private Markets. At the end of the programme, you’ll have the opportunity to apply for our Graduate Programme starting in September 2028. What will I be doing? M&G's Private Market’s area focuses on investments that are not publicly traded, including structured finance, private equity, real estate, infrastructure, and private debt. The strategy involves direct investments, co-investments, and partnerships with other investors to access unique opportunities that can provide attractive returns and risk-adjusted performance. This also includes Impact strategies across our range such as the Catalyst initiative that targets impactful investments to drive positive change and sustainable growth by seeking out opportunities that align with broader societal and environmental goals, often focusing on sectors such as climate, health and tackling inequality. Interns at M&G make valuable contributions to the teams they work in, in this team you will:Learn about our business by working alongside experienced professionals across various business functions, and investment desks covering multiple asset classes. Where required, support projects and have the opportunity to meet and shadow people from across the investment business.
