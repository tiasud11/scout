We offer a fantastic opportunity to gain experience within a global investment bank, working on real projects whilst developing a strong professional network within a bank that prides itself on its inclusive, cooperative and friendly company culture. We offer the best of both worlds – the feeling of a boutique work environment within a global organisation. Our steady growth in EMEA gives our people at all levels rewarding degrees of responsibility and a richer work experience than a small firm or an established giant could offer alone. The Global Transaction Banking Department (GTBD) works closely with Mizuho’s Corporate and Investment Banking division to provide transaction banking products and solutions to corporates, financial institutions and government-related enterprises across the UK, Continental Europe, the Middle East and Africa. What will I be doing? Over 10 weeks, you’ll gain hands-on experience within the Global Transaction Banking Department, working alongside experienced professionals and gaining insight into how Mizuho supports its clients globally. You’ll have the opportunity to: Conduct company, industry and market research Assist with assessing clients’ transaction banking requirements Support the preparation of client presentations, reports and marketing materials Contribute to the structuring and execution of transactions Support wider business activities, strategic projects and process improvement initiatives Throughout the programme, you’ll gain exposure to different transaction banking products and solutions, developing your understanding of how Mizuho supports clients with their cash management, liquidity and trade finance requirements. What can we offer you? Real-World Experience: Work on real business activities and gain first-hand experience within a global investment bank. Personalised Guidance & Mentorship: One-on-one guidance from experienced market professionals. Comprehensive Training: Technical training and tailored soft skills sessions designed to help you thrive in a professional environment. Buddy System: A directed peer mentor to support you throughout your internship Career Progression: A direct route to our graduate programme Highly competitive salary: in line with salaries offered by other London Corporate Investment Banks. What are we looking for? You should: In your penultimate year of study, with an expected graduation in 2028 (please ensure this is clearly stated on your CV and updated on your GRB profile). Available to complete the 10-week internship starting in June 2027. Be available for full-time employment from September 2028. Have a genuine interest in financial services. Unfortunately, sponsorship cannot be offered for this role We are an equal opportunity employer and are committed to supporting equality and diversity and seek to create a workplace that is fully inclusive. What’s the Application Process? Apply with your CV – no cover letter required, but please ensure your CV is tailored to this internship and that your graduation year is correct on both your CV and GRB profile. Online assessment & video interview – a chance to share your motivations, skills, and personality. Assessment Centre (London – first week of December) – meet our team, experience our culture, and see why Mizuho is the right place for you. Applications may close early, so please apply as soon as possible. If you are having any issues with your application please email [email protected] .
