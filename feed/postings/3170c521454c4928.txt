A 'cookie' is a packet of information placed on a user's computer by a website for record keeping purposes. Cookies are generally used on Macquarie Group sites to access online services, manage advertising, monitor traffic and monitor your use of our website. We may also use cookies to provide optional features, improve our website and support our marketing. Where relevant, our third-party providers will help us make use of these optional cookies. You can choose which types of optional cookies you wish to accept.
