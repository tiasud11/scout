Application trackers are live, public databases of available job opportunities for students. Our trackers are updated throughout the day whenever an application opens, serving as a comprehensive list of relevant internships. How are trackers different to job boards? Traditional job boards are slow to update, and only share opportunities that are actively posted by employers. Our application trackers automatically detect job openings, and share them immediately in a more concise and practical spreadsheet format. How are the trackers updated? We receive our signals from countless sources, ranging from tracking algorithms to word-of-mouth. All job updates go through a manual layer of review to ensure our trackers remain concise, curated and relevant for our users. When do the trackers reset for the next cycle? Most of our trackers reset between May and July to begin tracking opportunities for the following year. US Finance Summer Analyst applications run exceptionally early, hence our US Finance tracker resets in September for opportunities 2 years later. Do you plan to release more trackers? Yes! We are always considering new geographies and industries to develop new trackers for. When selecting new markets, we prioritise the most competitive industries and always recruit expert ex-applicants to advise on or manage the trackers. How do I report an inaccuracy on a tracker? You may notice new opportunities that have not been shared on the trackers, or applications that were shared but have since closed. If you would like to report incomplete information, you may refer to the form at the bottom of each tracker or contact us at info@the-trackr.com. Do you plan to release a mobile app?
