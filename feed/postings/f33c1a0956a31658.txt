Right for you if you're in Year 11/12 in England and Wales, Year 12/13 in Northern Ireland or S4/6 Scotland You'll gain Application skills, business acumen and an understanding of PwC Location Virtual Our Virtual Insight Programme is an experience that builds your skills, explores your career interests and immerses you into the exciting world of PwC. The programme is designed for students who are at still at school or college. So whether you’re thinking about doing an apprenticeship, planning for university or ready to step into working life, it’s the perfect way to start thinking about plans for the future. It’s also a great introduction to one of the world’s leading professional services firms. Our Virtual Insight Programme is currently being reimagined. Further details will be announced soon.
