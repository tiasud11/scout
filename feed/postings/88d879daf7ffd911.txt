Shell Graduate ProgrammeShell Assessed InternshipWebsiteLinkedInAbout Shell Today, Shell is a vital contributor to the UK, supporting energy security, jobs and economic value. We provide energy for homes, vehicles, machinery and factories. Our history here dates back over 125 years. A UK-headquartered global energy leader and multinational, we are active across the country's energy system, with highly talented staff in UK and global roles driving value in the UK and across the Group's global footprint of over 70 countries. Our strength in the UK lies in our unique integrated footprint: our retail network that serves millions of customers and our facilities that help us supply around 20 per cent of the UK's gas, to Shell's global trading hub in the UK, that help us manage renewable power solutions for companies such as Google. We are working to strongly position Shell for the energy transition by evolving our businesses, in areas such as electric-vehicle charging and through Adura, our Shell Equinor incorporated joint venture in the North Sea. We are confident this will provide value for Shell and ensure we continue to play a central role in UK energy today and through the energy transition.
