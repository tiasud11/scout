Spring program dates: TBC About Jefferies Jefferies is a leading global, full-service investment banking and capital markets firm that provides advisory, sales and trading, research and wealth and asset management services. With more than 47 offices in 21 countries around the world, we offer insights and expertise to investors, companies and governments. Our talented professionals provide a full range of advisory and underwriting services through creative and idea-driven M&A, leveraged finance, equity & equity-linked financing and restructuring & recapitalization solutions. Our Equity Research Spring Insight Programme Our Equity Research Spring Insight is a programme designed for students interested in pursuing a career in banking. Through our structured programme, workshops, social event and mentoring programme, you will gain valuable industry experience and learn what it takes to build a long and successful career in Research, as well as a unique look into our core businesses. Key Programme Highlights Professional Skills Training In depth Business Area Overviews, Presentations and Group Exercises Interactive Case Studies Mentoring and guidance from colleagues of all levels Firm-wide networking events Consideration for a 2028 Summer Internship offer based on demonstrated performance and potential. Programme Details Our programme is aimed at students in their first year of study (or second year of a four-year degree), with an anticipated graduation date of Spring/Summer 2029 .
