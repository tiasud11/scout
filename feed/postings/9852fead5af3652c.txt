Report this job BMO Capital Markets is a leading, full-service financial services provider. Part of our core business is offering investment and corporate banking services to clients around the world. At BMO you will use your talents to make a real impact – on a team where you will learn, grow and be recognized for the great work that you do. The Investment Banking Summer Analyst Program in London offers an opportunity to work on high profile transactions as part of BMO’s leading global metals & mining franchise across a range of products (buy-side and sell-side M&A, ECM, and leveraged finance). You will have direct exposure to live transactions, exposure to senior professionals and our diverse client base, and receive focused training and mentoring to support your continued career development. BMO Capital Markets is the number one metals & mining investment banking franchise globally by deal value and volume and has been voted the world’s best metals & mining investment bank every year for over a decade. Our Summer Analyst Program does not require experience in the metals & mining sector and we welcome applicants from diverse backgrounds, but we look forward to the opportunity to develop your curiosity in a sector that is global, macro-driven, and fundamental to a green future. Program details: Our London-based Summer Analyst Program is a ten-week program, providing deep and diverse exposure to our full range of investment banking products across our current suite of advisory and financing mandates, as a fully-integrated member of our team in support of our clients. Following a formal orientation, the program will be an on-the-job experience, including the opportunity to network across various business groups, getting to know BMO Capital Markets, and participating in training and learning seminars. Developing your knowledge: A key focus of the program is to develop your skills and knowledge over a ten-week period. Successful Summer Analysts will be considered for the full-time BMO Capital Markets Investment and Corporate Banking Analyst Program, which commences in July 2027. Responsibilities: Directly supporting our team across its current suite of live advisory and financing mandates Company & industry research and analysis A detailed introduction to financial modelling Drafting and finalising advisory presentations to deliver on our clients’ needs Working and supporting the team with ad hoc requests Attend team meetings, workshops and other development components of the program Requirements: We look for: Excellent academic records Excellent attention to detail Proven analytical skills and numerical skills Committed and results-driven individuals A demonstrated interest in finance A proven team player; demonstrates leadership, collaboration, communication **All candidates must be authorized to work in the UK and not require sponsorship BMO is an Equal Opportunities Employer.
