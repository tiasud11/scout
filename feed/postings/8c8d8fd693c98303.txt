Customers need a reason to accept it. Insight Product Growth Everything changes. Knowing what matters changes everything. AI is reshaping every industry. For the businesses we back, it is the opportunity of a lifetime: to rethink their products and business models around how their customers now work. Doing this well means reading the right signals and acting quickly and decisively. But change this fast creates noise. Our role is to cut through it with clarity. That is the idea at the heart of our new brand: wayfinding. It draws on the expert navigators who, through skill, experience and knowledge passed down through generations, can plot a course in any conditions and move forward with confidence. That idea now guides everything we do, from how we speak to how we look. Hg brand launch 1:03 A brand that evolves Our brand has always evolved with the firm and the world around it. Today Hg manages over $110bn in assets and has an in-house team of 150 AI specialists working alongside our companies. That scale and capability mean our brand now speaks to a wider audience than ever, from founders and institutional investors to private wealth clients and the engineers joining Hg Catalyst. We wanted every one of them to understand what Hg has become, through one brand system that holds it all together. So we rebuilt it, from the ground up. Built on three pillars Our brand reaches a room before we do, so it has to reflect something real. Across 25 years, three qualities define who we are: Grounded. Practical and hands-on, a culture that runs through Hg’s history and still shapes how we work with every business we back. Knowledgeable. Deep, specific technology expertise across investment and operations, built and refined over decades. Dynamic. Always moving forward, from AI transformation across our portfolio to the continuing evolution of our investment strategy and how we run the firm. One system, across everything Wayfinding now shapes every part of how Hg shows up. Language. A clearer, more direct way of speaking about Hg, now running through our website and communications. Identity. A redesigned pointer mark and wordmark, set within a visual language of topographic lines and texture. Motion and photography. New principles for how Hg moves on screen, and imagery that brings sharp focus out of a grainy backdrop, so every piece feels part of the same journey. Media kit One family. Hg Catalyst, Hg Wealth, HgT and The Hg Foundation now sit within a single brand architecture, each distinct but clearly part of Hg. This is just the beginning. “Hg has always moved quickly to stay at the apex of where technology meets work. Our new brand reflects another step change in our scale and capabilities, in a sector that is changing fast. It is built for the founders we back, the people we hire and the investors we serve.” - Steven Batchelor and Jean-Baptiste Brian, Co-CEOs “We wanted a brand that works instantly for every audience we serve and reflects what is already true about Hg. Wayfinding gives us one system that carries our language, identity, and energy across everything we do.” - Tom Eckersley, Head of Marketing and External Affairs A new direction - Q&A Why now? Because we continue to evolve and the market continues to change. As AI transforms software, how a firm is seen shapes how it competes for deals, management talent and investor capital. This reflects a strategic evolution, not a cosmetic one. What’s changed? A new brand system, covering our positioning, language, visual identity, motion and photography, alongside a new website. And unlike last time, our logo has changed too. What is Wayfinding? The idea that organises our brand. It reflects how we work: using deep knowledge and experience to set a clear course in a complex, fast-moving environment, and helping the businesses we back do the same. Tell me about the new look? At its centre is the wayfinder pointer, built into the g of our logo. It works on its own, as part of the logo and in motion. Our wordmark moves to a lowercase h for a cleaner, more contemporary form. Textures drawn from shifting landscapes and a candid photography style bring the system to life. Originally ‘Hg’ was the symbol of Mercury (a nod to our legacy spinning out of Mercury Asset Management). This is something that has lost relevance in our brand today so we're losing it and moving forward. Who is it for? Everyone we work with, from the founders and management teams we partner with and the investors we serve, to the engineers and specialists who join us.
