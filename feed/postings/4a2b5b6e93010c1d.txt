Begin your career journey in Asset Management Client Group & Product: Join M&G’s eight-week Summer Internship Join us on 28 June 2027 for an eight week internship to gain hands-on experience, develop technical and professional skills, and build a strong understanding of Client Group & Product within Asset Management. Your journey will start with a cohort-wide induction, with focused insight sessions to help you learn more about M&G, network with colleagues, and begin developing your professional skills. You will take part in a structured programme, including: Team Placement: Join one team for eight weeks where you will take on meaningful work, contribute to live projects, and shadow experienced colleagues to gain a solid understanding of Client Group & Product. Group Project: An opportunity to collaborate with your peers on a live strategic challenge, developing teamwork and problem solving skills and presenting your recommendations back to the business at the end of the programme. Professional Development: Take part in interactive learning sessions and networking opportunities designed to grow your expertise and build your professional network. Your Support Network: Regular check-ins with the Early Careers Team, your manager, and a buddy will ensure you have all the support you need during the programme. This internship is your first step toward a career in Client Group & Product. At the end of the programme, you’ll have the opportunity to apply for our Graduate Programme starting in September 2028. What will I be doing? As part of our global Client Group and Product functions, you’ll gain hands-on experience across one of our teams: Sales, Client Group Strategy and Operations, and Product teams. Interns at M&G make valuable contributions to the teams they work in, and could be involved in: Sales: You’ll work on projects that explore new and evolving sales channels, conduct research into under-penetrated markets, and provide business development support to sales directors. You’ll also assist in bringing new products to market, learn how the sales process works across different channels. Client Group Strategy and Operations: you’ll get to see the workings of the Client Group function on a day to day basis, as well as gaining exposure to key industry trends and developments. Your role in this team will likely include distribution strategy projects (e.g., research and analysis to inform recommendations on strategic topics such as geographical expansion and targeting specific sales channels) and working with the sales management team to analyse sales and client trends. Additionally, you may assist in developing tools to streamline internal processes and deliver enhanced insights, and helping the Client Group adopt AI to improve productivity. Product: You will assist in designing new funds, focusing on both structural and technical aspects, as well as conducting competitor research. Your role may also involve product management work, such as modifying liquidity terms, incentive fee structures, and legal documentation. This is a unique opportunity to understand how we connect investment capabilities with client needs, develop innovative products, and shape strategic growth What are we looking for? Analytical thinking and problem-solving skills. Strong attention to detail. Clear communication and collaboration skills. Comfort with technology and digital tools. Ability to build and maintain strong professional relationships across teams and stakeholders We welcome applicants from all degree disciplines and value diverse perspectives and experiences.
