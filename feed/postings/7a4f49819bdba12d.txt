Department/Function: Sector Coverage Team: Transport and Logistics Internship Period: 6 months - January - June 2027 About ING Bank We began operating in Singapore in 1987 where we host the regional Asia Pacific headquarters. With over 300 financial experts, we are the largest wholesale banking branch in Asia. ING stands out in the Asia Pacific region because we go a step further for our customers. We look at things from the client’s perspective which allows us to construct tailor-made solutions to fit the needs of every company we serve. This mentality underpins all our client relations and has produced various prestigious awards. Our internship programme offers more than just on-the-job experience. Through structured onboarding, development initiatives, cross-functional networking opportunities, project showcases and an intern alumni community, you'll gain valuable exposure, build meaningful connections and develop skills for your future career. What will you be doing? An opportunity to be a part of the Transport and Logistics sector team financing the Shipping, Aviation and Container industries Assist with portfolio emissions analysis, reporting and reconciliation of data with source for shipping portfolio Participate in structuring and assessment of sustainability linked performance targets and KPI’s Assist with development of sector specific policies and procedures Monitor internal metrics and KPI’s related to the sector’s exposure Understand and assess application of climate scenarios and decarbonization trajectories relevant to the sub sectors Co-ordinate with global teams for internal and external sustainability related requirements Assist with (ad hoc) reporting tasks, covenant tracking, admin forms, banking procedures and reporting procedures Assist coverage team with credit analysis and reviews, financial analysis, pitch decks, client meetings and market updates; Participate in internal analytics and data management product development and update What qualities and skills are we looking for? Affinity for accounting, data analysis and modelling Good familiarity with Excel including advanced functions and working with multiple complex data sets Knowledge of Power BI is an advantage; Finance, sustainability or business studies with a keen interest to develop a career in the financial services sector; An effective team player with good interpersonal and communication skills capable of working with global teams; Detail oriented, systematic and strong analytical skills to optimize repetitive data process; High level of self-motivation and professionalism with ability to multi-task ; Ability to work independently Good command of spoken and written English.
