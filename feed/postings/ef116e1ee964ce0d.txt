Graduate Management Trainee – Marketing (London) London, England, United Kingdom Marketing Management Trainee – London 2028 Applications close: 20th November Programme start dates: July 2027 or January 2028 Starting salary: £36,500, rising to £41,500 on completion of the scheme Eligibility: You must be in your final year of study or have graduated within the past two years. Hello, we’re L’Oréal We’re not just building brands; we’re shaping how the world experiences beauty. If you’re curious about consumers, brands and the ideas that bring them together, this could be the opportunity for you. A day in the life Based at our UK headquarters in White City, London, you’ll complete three six-month rotations across different brands and Marketing teams over an 18-month scheme. You’ll report to a Marketing Manager in each rotation, gaining experience across different parts of the business and learning how our brands connect with consumers. You could: Help coordinate product launches, working with agencies and teams on creative materials and in-store displays. Support campaign plans with Sales and retail teams, including promotional activity and customer presentations. Review information about brands, competitors and consumers to help teams understand what is working and where opportunities may lie. Support campaigns across social media, e-commerce and influencer channels, helping teams track plans and budgets. Work with Marketing, Supply Chain, Regulatory and Sales teams to help keep product launches on track. What we’re looking for You don’t need previous Marketing experience. We’re looking for potential, curiosity and a willingness to learn. Throughout the recruitment process, we’ll look for examples of how you demonstrate: Ambition: motivation, energy and a desire to grow and succeed. Resilience: the ability to stay composed, learn from setbacks and keep moving forward. Empathy: active listening, respect for different perspectives and a collaborative approach. Judgement: logical thinking and balanced decisions, including in new or complex situations. Learning agility: curiosity, openness to feedback and the ability to adapt and apply what you learn. AI fluency: an openness to using AI tools effectively and responsibly, with an awareness of trustworthy AI principles. You’ll also bring an interest in consumers, brands and culture, clear communication skills, and an organised approach to working with different people and priorities. You’ll have the opportunity to develop your Marketing skills throughout the scheme. Right to work in the UK This scheme leads to a guaranteed operational role upon successful completion. As graduates are retained after the scheme, it’s important that you have the right to work in the UK for the longer term. During the application process, you’ll be asked: We are required by law to verify work authorization for all prospective employees. Please confirm your visa status using the drop down below. I’m a UK or Irish citizen, or hold EU Settled Status, with an indefinite right to work and no sponsorship requirement. I hold EU Pre-Settled Status, which gives me time-limited right to work and may require a status update or future visa. I hold another UK visa that currently gives me the right to work, but may require sponsorship in the future. I do not currently have the right to work in the UK. What’s in it for you?
