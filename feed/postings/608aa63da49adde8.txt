We offer private banking and wealth management services to high net worth clients in the UK including banking, lending, and investments designed to meet their diverse and complex needs. We achieve this by offering specialist expertise backed by over 300 years of exceptional service. We’re a trusted partner, adviser, and friend to our clients. Our strategy is underpinned by making sure we’re set up for success by supporting colleagues to learn new skills essential for future growth, and our internship programme helps us do just that. Your internship journey Our Coutts Relationship Management summer internship has been designed to give you a taste of what it’s like to work with us, along with valuable experience to help guide your career choices. Over the course of this eight-week programme, you can look forward to: Accelerating your relationship management capability Seeing first-hand how we work together to deliver outstanding service for our clients Learning about our culture, values, and goals Attending training workshops and getting involved with our day-to-day work Exploring your capability by taking part in a business improvement project Gaining a unique understanding of our products and services Understanding the diverse needs of our high-net-worth clients Developing your ability to identify business development opportunities and digital solutions At the end of the placement, we’ll assess you on what you’ve learned. Complete this successfully and you could be offered a place on our Coutts Relationship Management graduate programme, where you can carry the skills you’ve learnt forward and take the next step in your career. How you’ll benefit We’ll reward you with a starting salary of £30,330 pro rata. Plus, you can expect to: Learn from industry experts and immediately apply your learnings Enrich your learning with access to world-leading research and insights Test and learn new approaches in a safe, inclusive environment Start building a global professional network Grow your confidence, build your resilience, and elevate your presence Experience first-hand what it’s like working in a purpose-driven organisation Entry requirements To be eligible for our Coutts Relationship Management internship you’ll need: To be in your penultimate year of university To be on course to achieving a 2:1 in your degree A genuine desire to work with and support our customers To be a team player Communication skills Interpersonal skills To be change-ready A customer-first approach Ready to apply? The application window for this programme will be open until we receive enough applications to fill our cohort. When that happens, we’ll close the programme to new applications. It’s important to take the time to make sure you’re making the right decision about the programme you want to apply for (you can only apply for one programme) but please complete your application as soon as you can to make sure you don’t miss out!
