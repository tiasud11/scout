We help them to take care of their day-to-day finances and manage, protect and grow their wealth. Asset Management invests on behalf of HSBC’s worldwide customer base of retail and private clients, intermediaries, corporates and institutions. It connects our clients with investment opportunities around the world through an international network of offices. Who you are You think critically, learn quickly and are motivated by understanding how investment decisions are made. You’re curious about markets, economics and the forces that shape portfolio performance, and you enjoy turning research and analysis into useful insight. You could be a strong fit if you’re entrepreneurial, proactive and comfortable taking ownership of your development while working closely with others. You communicate clearly, build relationships well and stay thoughtful when working through complex problems. An interest in develops and manages investment management products and services for clients around the world, from individual retail customers to major multinational corporate clients, sovereign wealth funds and financial institution clients. You don’t need to arrive with every answer, but you should bring intellectual curiosity, sound judgement and a genuine interest in helping deliver strong outcomes for clients. What you'll do You’ll gain experience across Asset Management, with exposure to both investment specialist and alternatives-focused work. You could support teams analysing economic developments, market trends and investment opportunities, helping turn research into insight for internal stakeholders and clients. Depending on your rotation or team, you may contribute to work linked to stock selection, portfolio construction, ongoing portfolio management or specialist capability areas such as private markets, real estate or private debt. You’ll predominantly work with investment teams, while also gaining broader exposure through projects or job shadowing in areas such as sales and operations to understand the wider business. Along the way, you’ll help prepare presentations, support analysis and build a practical understanding of how investment ideas are developed and delivered in a client-focused environment. What you'll learn You’ll build deep technical and commercial understanding through structured development, hands-on experience and support from your team. From day one, you’ll have a dedicated line manager and home team to help you build confidence, strengthen your expertise and feel connected to the business. You’ll deepen your understanding of investment processes, financial markets, portfolio construction and the role of alternatives within broader client solutions. You’ll also further develop practical skills such as financial modelling, research, analysis and communicating investment ideas clearly. Support from mentors, programme alumni and colleagues across the business will help you grow your network and navigate your development. By the end of the programme, you’ll have a stronger foundation in asset management, broader business understanding and the skills to continue building your career in investments. Where you'll work You’ll work in Asset Management within International Wealth and Premier Banking. It’s a collaborative environment where investment teams, specialist capability areas and business partners work together to support clients across a range of markets and asset classes. You’ll be part of a professional office culture that values insight, initiative and continuous learning, with exposure to both the technical side of investing and the wider business activities that help deliver investment solutions to clients. Expect to work closely with experienced colleagues in an international setting shaped by market developments, client needs and long-term growth opportunities. Eligibility Criteria To be eligible, you must: Be available to work full time for 10 weeks commencing June 2027 Have obtained, or expect to achieve, a minimum GPA of 4.0/5.0 or 3.2/4.0 (or overseas equivalent, as shown on your official university transcript/degree certificate) Be fluent in English, knowledge of Mandarin/Cantonese would be an advantage Expected to graduate between November 2027 and July 2028 We will consider candidates that require visa sponsorship for this role We only accept one application per recruitment cycle so please take time to explore our opportunities before applying as we may not be able to transfer your application to other vacancies.
