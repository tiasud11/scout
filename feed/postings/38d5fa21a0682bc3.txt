transactions, from origination through to completion Building and maintaining financial and valuation models (DCF, comparable companies, precedent transactions) Conducting industry and company research to support live mandates and business development Assisting with due diligence, data rooms, and transaction documentation Taking on increasing responsibility across live deals as the programme progresses, with structured on-the-job training and mentoring throughout Applicants must have Graduating in 2027, with a 2.1 degree or above, in a relevant subject (finance, economics, engineering, or a related discipline) Strong numerical and analytical skills, with genuine commercial awareness of the clean energy / energy transition sector Excellent attention to detail, strong business English writing and presentation skills The ability to manage multiple work-streams concurrently, under pressure and to deadline Self-discipline and high motivation, with a clear, demonstrable drive to build a long-term career in the sector Prior internship or work experience in investment banking, corporate finance, or a closely related financial services field A solid understanding of capital raising and M&A Advanced PowerPoint & Word skills for preparing investment presentations Advanced Excel skills: three-statement financial modelling, charting, and company valuation (DCF / comparable company analysis) Preferred (not required) Demonstrated ability to use AI tools to improve the speed or quality of analytical work Direct experience working on live corporate finance or M&A transactions (e.g. via a prior internship) Strong awareness of capital raising and/or M&A deal cycles and processes Familiarity with Bloomberg / CapitalIQ / FactSet Graduate Careers How to Apply Please send your CV, together with short answers to the three questions below, to careers@longspur.com . Please use the subject line: “[Name Surname] – 2027 Summer Internship Application” for the Summer Intern role “[Name Surname] – 2027 Graduate Analyst Application” for the Graduate Analyst role Alongside your CV, please answer the following in the body of your email. Please answer the questions in reverse order, with a maximum of 150 words per answer. Why are you interested in a career in corporate finance / investment banking, and specifically in the clean energy sector? Describe a time you had to manage several competing priorities or a tight deadline. How did you approach it? What relevant experience, coursework, or projects (academic or otherwise) have prepared you for this role? Applications reviewed on a rolling basis until 31 October 2026. Interviews will take place in November and December 2026, with offers made in January 2027. Both positions are expected to start in June 2027.
