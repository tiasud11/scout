About the programme You will be dedicated to a specific team and work on an AI-related project relevant to that team's focus, alongside broader involvement in the team's day-to-day activities. Previous projects have included “AI and automation for renewable energy infrastructure” , “Machine learning for equity management” , “Equity enhanced indexing investment simulation” and “Predicting capital market events” . The balance between project work and wider team involvement varies by business area. A dedicated manager will guide your work, and a buddy will support you throughout the programme. You will be introduced to the organisation through a lecture series featuring senior professionals, as well as social events and team activities where you can connect with fellow interns and colleagues across the fund. At the end of the programme, you will present your work to the wider organisation and the leader group. Where you will be based You can be based in Oslo or London. If you are open and eligible for both locations, please state that in your application. Applicants must have an approved right to work in the country where the internship is based, from the start date and for the full duration of the internship. If you hold a student visa or permit in the country where the internship is based, please ensure that they allow full-time work for the entire internship period. We do not sponsor or support applications for visas or work permits for this programme, and we do not provide accommodation or relocation support. Are you our next intern? We are looking for summer interns with a strong interest in AI and technology who are curious about how these tools can be applied. We welcome candidates from diverse backgrounds. These include data science, AI, finance, computer science, mathematics, statistics and engineering. We value diverse perspectives and encourage applications from candidates with different life experiences and backgrounds. We look for people who: Form their own views and take genuine ownership of their learning Enjoy working through complex problems and making connections Stay calm when things are unclear and treat uncertainty as part of the process Can focus on details and still keep the bigger picture in mind Work well in a team and bring energy and care to how they collaborate You do not need to be a tech expert, but we will require a minimum of AI skills. Curiosity, analytical thinking and a willingness to learn are just as important. Whether you have built things, worked with data, or simply found yourself asking “how does this work, and could it be done better?”, we would like to hear from you. Eligibility The programme is open to students at bachelor’s, master’s, MBA and PhD level. You must be currently enrolled in, or formally accepted to, a degree programme, and have no more than two years of full-time work experience. Internships and part-time work during studies do not count towards this limit. There is no minimum year-of-study requirement, though students in later years of their degree tend to be more competitive. How to apply? Applications close on Sunday October 18th 2026 at 23:59 CET.
