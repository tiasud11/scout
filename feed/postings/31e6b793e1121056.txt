Kernel ATS ← View all vacanciesCaledonia InvestmentsCaledonia Investments 2027 Summer Internship - Caledonia Investments - Summer Internship 2027Internship Overview This year our Sea Lions Programme will run between Monday 21st June 2027 to Friday 16th July 2027 and its purpose is to provide four weeks of structured inclusive learning to individuals who wish to explore the potential to develop a career in financial services. The programme will introduce students to Caledonia and equip them with skills and knowledge for future employment opportunities. Unlike traditional internship programmes, ours is designed as a ‘mini business school’ using financial and staff resources for which Caledonia asks for nothing in return, other than enthusiastic engagement and with the aim of creating new friends for the future. As part of our four week programme you can expect: An introduction to Caledonia and each of its core investment pools (public markets, private markets and fund investing). Extensive personal development and team skills learning. Tour of the City of London and visits to investee companies. A team project culminating in a presentation to a “dragons’ den” style panel at the end of the four weeks. Provision of a Caledonia mentor to provide help and advice through the course and potentially beyond. You will be invited to meet with your mentor in advance of the programme and to pre-programme drinks where you will have the opportunity to meet other Caledonia staff and interns from previous years. You will be paid to attend the course with the rate set at £127.50 per day. Travel expenses to the assessment centre will also be paid on request. Whilst permanent positions are unavailable at the end of the programme, demand for the course remains very high so it is greatly appreciated that those invited to attend and accept, do attend to avoid the waste of a valuable place. What we look for: We are seeking applications from higher education students interested in exploring a career in financial services. There are no pre-requisites for year of study, course, or previous qualification grades. All candidates are required to have the right to paid work in the UK and hold a UK bank account. Timeline: The application process will open on Monday 19th October 2026 A CV and covering letter to be submitted by Friday 6th November 2026 (please don't use AI to write these as we will be screening for this using a tool). Successful candidates at this stage will be invited to an interview with Dartmouth Partners Successful candidates will be selected to attend an assessment day on Tuesday 15th December 2026. This will comprise of group exercises, followed by interviews with members of Caledonia staff.
