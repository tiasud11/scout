It’s where big-picture thinking meets real business impact, helping create the foundations that let millions of customers and colleagues stay connected. Finance helps keep HSBC running smoothly by turning complex numbers into clear insight. From reporting and forecasting to tax, treasury and investor updates, this is where data helps shape smart decisions across the bank. Who you are You’re curious, analytical and interested in the story behind the numbers. You enjoy solving problems, spotting patterns and turning information into ideas that can make a real difference. You don’t need to know every finance concept from day one — bring your curiosity, motivation and problem-solving mindset, and we’ll help you build the rest. You could be a great fit if you like learning, thinking logically and working through complex challenges from different angles. You build strong relationships, communicate clearly and know how to adapt your style for different audiences. You’re also reflective and open to feedback, because growth matters to you. If you want to understand how finance supports big decisions and helps shape the future of a global business, this could be a strong match. What you'll do You’ll work with Finance teams on analysis that supports important business decisions. You could review complex financial data, help identify opportunities to improve reporting through digital or AI tools, and turn trends into clear recommendations for stakeholders. You may support work across balance sheet management, regulatory reporting, tax or transformation projects, depending on your team. You’ll work with colleagues across Finance and the wider bank, helping connect data, insight and decision-making. It’s a practical introduction to how finance analysis supports strategy, performance and change. The internship also includes structured assessments aligned to securing a graduate role, giving you chances to demonstrate how you think, collaborate and deliver, through tasks such as project work, presentations, interviews or skills-based evaluations. What you'll learn Over 10 weeks, you’ll join a finance team and build a clearer understanding of how business analysis supports decision-making at HSBC. Through technical training, skills coaching and hands-on work, you’ll strengthen your knowledge of financial concepts, reporting and market trends, and learn how they influence major business decisions. You’ll also build confidence in turning data into insight and communicating your thinking clearly in a professional environment. The internship is designed to help you connect analytical work to real business outcomes, so the learning feels practical from the start. By the end, you’ll have a stronger foundation in finance-focused analysis and a clearer sense of where this path could take you. Where you'll work You’ll work in Group Infrastructure, within Finance teams that support decision-making across HSBC. It’s a professional environment where finance specialists partner with colleagues across the bank on reporting, balance sheet management, tax and transformation priorities. You’ll be part of a collaborative office culture that values insight, accuracy and strong stakeholder relationships. Expect a setting close to the centre of the bank, where finance teams help shape strategic direction through partnership and analysis.
