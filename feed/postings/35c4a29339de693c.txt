We’re looking for a Staff Technical Program Manager to lead complex company wide programmes driven by the Operations collective. You’ll shape how we work across the collective, influence domain and collective leaders across Monzo, connect technical delivery to operational outcomes and make sure change works for the people who use it every day. What you’ll do Lead programmes that cut through all operational domains at Monzo You’ll lead high-impact programmes from strategy through to measurable outcomes. These may cover AI-powered customer support, tools that help our Customer Operations teams, or the platforms and processes behind millions of operational decisions. Build strong partnerships at every level You’ll work with a large and varied group of partners. This could include frontline Operations teams, subject matter experts, product and engineering teams, risk and compliance partners, senior leaders and executives. Drive change, not just delivery You’ll help the whole collective adopt new ways of working. You’ll understand how changes affect people, processes and technology, then build that into the programme from the start. Key Responsibilities: Lead end-to-end planning and execution of major product or platform programs that cross multiple teams and markets Manage cross-collective programmes: Define programme roadmaps and milestones, set priorities, manage trade-offs, identify and manage the critical path and risks, ensure alignment with the business goals and ambition Collaborate and communicate with multiple stakeholders, both technical and non-technical across the business to ensure alignment and speed of delivery Manage dependencies, timeline and the sequencing of delivery Drive effective decision making to remove blockers Ensure operational effectiveness across your collective/area You should apply if: You have 5+ years of experience managing complex technology programmes or products from inception to launch You’re an exceptional communicator who can create clarity for technical and non-technical audiences You are comfortable working in a global business with a presence in multiple markets and want to be part of scaling this further You can work effectively with a diverse range of people and working styles to get stuff done, and are able to thoughtfully and constructively challenge and influence the people you work with You can make complex work easy to understand without losing the important detail You understand how functionality comes together (across products and platforms) to deliver value for users, and can proactively identify and mitigate delivery risks, and can propose and drive a technical solution You are comfortable personally diving deep into the data to make good decisions in complex situations where there’s often no “right answer” You can influence across teams even when you don’t manage them directly and can influence senior leaders, resolve disagreement and build alignment without relying on authority. You’ve driven organisation-wide change and can show how you supported adoption, not only delivery You don’t need to match every point. The interview process: Recruiter Call (30 mins) You'll meet our Engineering Leadership Recruiter to discuss your experience and learn more about Monzo. Loop Stage (3 hours) The Loop stage consists of 3 x 60 min interviews that take place over 1-2 days (depending on your availability). All things going well, you'll have completed the hiring process 🚀 Our average process takes around 3-4 weeks but we will always work around your availability.
