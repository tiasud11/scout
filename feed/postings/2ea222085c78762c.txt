We've made two changes, effective immediately: The Value Creation Team is now known as Hg Catalyst The AI team, including our AI product incubator, previously known as Hg Catalyst, becomes Catalyst Labs Why we've made the change The new Hg Catalyst brings together AI-first teams across Cyber, Engineering, Finance, Growth, Product, Talent, Tech Partnerships and Transformation. Lasting impact in a portfolio company needs more than AI tools. It comes from pairing our functional experts, who know what good looks like in each function, with AI builders and our Transformation experts to re-imagine processes around AI. These teams work as one, embedded inside portfolio companies in a forward-deployed model alongside business leaders. The same is true of AI products. Creating value from them depends on getting pricing and packaging, go-to-market and customer adoption right, so Catalyst Labs works with our Growth team from the outset. The Catalyst name reflects the team's role in catalysing change through AI across every function. Catalyst Labs Our focus on product innovation remains unchanged. We'll keep investing in Catalyst Labs with the same ambition as at launch: giving portfolio companies access to some of the best AI talent and helping them build real AI products faster.
