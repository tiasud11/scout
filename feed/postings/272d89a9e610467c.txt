About the business area Corporate and Institutional Banking helps some of the world’s largest companies grow and succeed by offering investment and financial solutions. Working across markets, sectors and borders, you’ll help deliver the insight, connectivity and support that enable clients to navigate change and unlock new opportunities Global Payment Solutions (GPS) is a multi-award winning team who helps clients make payments across borders, across currencies and regulations, quickly and efficiently. Who you are You’re someone who enjoys solving problems, building relationships and understanding how money moves around the world. You don’t need to arrive fluent in transaction banking — bring your curiosity, initiative and willingness to learn, and we’ll help with the rest. You could be a great fit if you work well with others, communicate clearly and stay interested when things get technical. You like connecting ideas, using data to support decisions and finding smarter ways to improve experiences for clients. You’re motivated, accountable and comfortable taking ownership of your work. Just as importantly, you enjoy learning from people around you and developing your own style as you grow. If you’re interested in payments, technology and helping clients navigate a fast-changing global landscape, you’ll feel at home here. What you'll do You’ll work in a specialist team and gain exposure across areas such as Sales, Product Management, Client Management, Business Management or Chief Control Office. You could help solve client problems by supporting work on payments, cards or liquidity solutions, preparing dashboards and presentations, and contributing to materials used in client-facing conversations. You may also support work linked to compliance and regulation, and explore how technologies like AI, APIs, Cloud and automation are improving services. You’ll work with colleagues across product, control and client teams, and help HSBC stay competitive in areas such as real-time payments and virtual accounts. It’s practical work with a clear connection to client outcomes. What you'll learn You’ll begin with HSBC’s Global Graduate Induction, then build your knowledge through tailored training focused on Global Payment Solutions. You’ll develop a practical understanding of core products including liquidity and investment solutions, payments and cards, and learn how these help clients manage cash, move money and improve efficiency. Alongside technical learning, you’ll strengthen your communication, problem-solving and commercial skills in a business shaped by regulation, technology and innovation. The programme is designed to help you understand not just what the products are, but why they matter to clients. By the end, you’ll have a strong foundation in global payments and the confidence to keep growing in a specialist transaction banking career. Where you'll work You’ll work in Global Payment Solutions, where teams across sales, product, client management and business management come together to support clients’ payment and liquidity needs. It’s a collaborative environment within Corporate and Institutional Banking, shaped by global commerce, regulation and innovation. You’ll be part of a professional office culture where people work across functions and markets to improve client outcomes. Expect a team setting that combines relationship-building, product expertise and a strong focus on how banking and technology connect.
