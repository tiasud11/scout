Job Application for QT Connect 2027 - EU/UK at Five Rings LLC - Careers Back to jobs New QT Connect 2027 - EU/UK New York Apply About Five Rings Five Rings is a proprietary trading firm founded with a vision of combining strategy, innovation and technology to succeed in today’s global markets. With offices in New York, Boca Raton, London and Amsterdam, Five Rings trades in various domestic and international markets, both established and esoteric. Our team constantly seeks new opportunities, analyzes their risks and rewards, and creates strategies and tools to capitalize on them. We have an open culture and encourage the flow of knowledge and ideas between all areas of the firm. QT Connect 2027 - Quantitative Trading Discovery Program Over the course of two days, participants will have the opportunity to learn about quantitative trading. Students will interact and network directly with our Traders and participate in market making games. Additionally, you will learn about the culture of Five Rings and what goes into being a successful trader. Where: New York City - Five Rings HQ When: April 19th-20th, 2027 Application Deadline: January 18th, 2027 About You Graduating in December 2028, or spring/summer 2029 Attending a university in the EU/UK Interested in pursuing a career in quantitative trading Intending to major in a STEM-related field Eager to learn more about life at Five Rings No prior financial experience necessary Travel and housing accommodations will be provided for all students. Since longer international travel is required, students should be available from Friday, April 16th to Tuesday, April 20th.
