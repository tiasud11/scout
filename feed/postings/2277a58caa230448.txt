As External Partnerships & Engagement Manager, you will strategically manage a portfolio of VAIB partnerships that bring vital specialist expertise and enhanced support to Monzo's most vulnerable customers. You will proactively identify new partnership opportunities while leveraging data and customer feedback to continuously monitor and maximize the value of your existing relationships. Ultimately, you will collaborate with cross-functional teams to translate this external insight and lived experience into tangible, practical improvements across our products, services, and customer journeys. Manage Partnership Lifecycles: Own the end-to-end portfolio of VAIB external partnerships, from identifying strategic opportunities to onboarding, ongoing relationship management, and appropriate exits. Drive Data-Led Impact: Continuously evaluate partnership performance using data and operational feedback, clearly reporting successes and confidently acting to strengthen or stop underperforming relationships. Oversee Governance and Risk: Partner with Supplier Operations to conduct robust due diligence, ensuring all external relationships strictly adhere to Monzo’s risk, governance, and regulatory expectations. Translate Insights into Action: Leverage specialist partner knowledge to identify opportunities and drive tangible improvements across vulnerable customer journeys, products, and support strategies. Lead Lived-Experience Research: Own the Signposting Standard and collaborate with the Research team to capture lived experiences, turning best-practice insights into clear cross-functional recommendations. Deliver Community Engagement: Plan external awareness campaigns, host expert listening sessions, and collaborate with Public Policy and Communications to amplify Monzo's impact and support media activity. 🤩 We’d love to hear from you if… You have a deep understanding of VAIB principles and the barriers vulnerable customers face, driven by a strong commitment to improving customer outcomes through meaningful external collaboration. You have proven experience managing external relationships, going beyond relationship-building to set clear objectives, build trust, and hold both internal and external stakeholders accountable for delivering measurable benefits. You have experience evaluating partnership effectiveness using data and customer feedback, and are highly skilled at translating external expertise and lived experiences into practical, actionable recommendations for teams across Monzo. You have a strong grasp of due diligence and risk management, ensuring all external partnerships are supported by rigorous governance and robust, accurate documentation. You are a highly organised problem-solver capable of managing multiple competing priorities, confidently navigating challenging stakeholder conversations, and proactively course-correcting when partnerships fail to deliver their intended value.
