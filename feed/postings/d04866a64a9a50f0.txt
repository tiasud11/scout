Spring into Deloitte: Advisory is for: First year university students enrolled on a three-year undergraduate degree; or First year students enrolled in a four-year undergraduate degree that includes an industrial placement year; or Second year university students enrolled on a four-year degree that does not include an industrial placement year. This programme is particularly suited to students who enjoy problem-solving and are interested in helping organisations tackle complex challenges. You’ll learn about different areas of our business, including Consulting and Strategy, Risk & Transactions Advisory, and discover how our teams help organisations make better decisions, manage risk and improve performance. You’ll also start to build skills in analysis, strategic thinking and commercial insight. Spring into Deloitte welcomes people from all backgrounds. We particularly encourage applications from women and those from Black heritage backgrounds as part of our wider efforts to improve representation within our industry. We respect differences (whether seen or hidden, physical, monetary or cultural) and our inclusive workspaces aim to support individuals to build a fulfilling career and seek the opportunities they deserve. Whether you’re a champion for diversity, the first in your family to go to university or just curious about what a career at our firm could offer you - Spring into Deloitte is for you. What skills and academic qualifications do I need? You’ll need to be on track for a degree classification of 2:1 or above in any discipline. This programme welcomes students who bring curiosity, enthusiasm and a fresh perspective. We’re looking for people that enjoy collaborating with others, problem solving and feel motivated to learn about a career in Consulting or Strategy, Risk & Transactions Advisory. Whatever your background, Spring into Deloitte: Advisory is an opportunity to discover your strengths, expand your network and explore the possibilities available to you. What will I be doing? Over two days, you'll take part in interactive workshops, hear from Deloitte professionals, and learn about the exciting projects our teams deliver for clients. You’ll gain insight into Consulting and Strategy, Risk and Transactions Advisory, exploring how these teams help organisations achieve their strategic goals, manage risk and improve performance. Stepping into the role of a Deloitte professional, you'll work in a group to tackle a real-world business challenge and develop a solution, giving you a flavour of the work we do every day across our business. Whether your strengths lie in problem-solving, working with people, analysing information, or generating ideas, there'll be opportunities to play to your strengths and discover where your skills could thrive. At Deloitte, we support equity, equality and inclusion. We know that different perspectives make us stronger, helping us to support our clients, our people and the communities we work with. Our 12 diversity networks bring together colleagues and allies from a wide range of backgrounds, helping everyone feel heard and supported. They include the Black Network, Hindu, Muslim, Sikh, Christian, Jewish, Neurodiversity, Multicultural, Workability, Working Families, LGBTQ+ PROUD, and Gender Balance networks. Through Spring into Deloitte: Advisory, you’ll have the chance to learn more about our inclusive culture, explore what matters to you, and consider the career path that feels right for you. When and where does Spring into Deloitte: Advisory take place? Spring into Deloitte: Advisory takes place in London from Thursday 1 st – Friday 2 nd April 2027. How could Spring into Deloitte: Advisory launch my career? You will leave the programme with plenty of key employability skills which will set you up for success in the next step of your career journey. Spring into Deloitte: Advisory can also open doors to future opportunities at the firm, including progression onto our Summer Vacation Scheme or Industrial Placement in either Audit & Assurance or Tax. Please note that once you have chosen a pathway after taking part in the programme, you won’t be able to switch business area or location. Additionally, if you’re on a four-year degree course with a year in industry, you’ll only be eligible to convert to our Industrial Placement programme, not the Summer Vacation Scheme.
