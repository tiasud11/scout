We have a well-established tone of voice, and people here really care about making sure all our writing is consistently good and works hard for customers. It’ll be your job to keep that quality up through the writing you do. Our never-ending reason to exist is to improve the overall quality of writing at Monzo. That means a culture where care for language is the norm. Everyone, in every role, should understand how important it is to write in a clear, empathetic way, and they should all have the skills to do it. The outcome of that will be world-leading writing for customers. They’ll trust us because our writing is clear, effective and works hard for them. You’ll get together with the other writers pretty regularly to review and showcase work, collect feedback and insights from one another and generally just raise the bar for words at Monzo. And as we hinted at already, you’ll live and breathe our tone of voice, which is propped up by 3 guiding principles: straightforward kindness, everyday magic and warm wit. You’ll be covering our Borrowing Writer while they’re on caregiver leave This is a 12-month fixed term contract covering for our Writer who’s based in the Borrowing collective. That means you’ll be focused on supporting teams working on things like loans, credit cards, overdrafts and other borrowing and credit related projects. You’ll work closely with cross-functional squads of engineers, designers, marketers, researchers and so on to make it an amazing experience for existing customers, and bring new people on board. A typical day could see you collaborating with a Product Designer on some in-app screens collaborating with a Product Marketer on positioning and building messaging toolkits for use across web, growth marketing or CRM sitting in on user research sessions led by the Research team writing error messages with input from engineers editing legal wording alongside our in-house lawyers Your home will be in Writing Alongside the work you do in Borrowing, you’ll contribute to the Writing discipline. We’re always working on new resources, training and processes to make writing easier for everyone at Monzo. Writing is part of the wider Marketing team but this role isn’t only about marketing.
