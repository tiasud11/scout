Real impact During your internship, you’ll take on meaningful work within Consumer, one of the Group’s largest business areas. You’ll use customer insight, data and digital technology to help solve real problems and create simpler, smarter and more connected customer experiences. Depending on your team, you could gain experience in: Product and platforms – learning how products and propositions are developed, managed and improved across saving, borrowing, everyday banking and planning for the future. Customer propositions and digital journeys – using customer insight to help shape products, services and experiences, and supporting teams to turn opportunities into action. The work you could be doing Exploring customer behaviour and using insight to improve products, propositions and experiences. Helping to design simpler and more connected customer journeys. Analysing data and customer feedback to identify opportunities for improvement. Supporting research, customer journey mapping and project delivery. Contributing to new product or proposition initiatives. Working with colleagues and partners to turn ideas into action. Using digital technology and AI tools to test ideas and support informed decisions. Contributing to more inclusive, sustainable and personalised customer outcomes. The skills you’ll develop By the end of the internship, you’ll have built practical experience, valuable connections and greater confidence in: Customer insight – using data, research and feedback to understand customer needs. Product development – understanding how products and propositions are created, managed and improved. Commercial thinking – beginning to understand how banking decisions create value while supporting positive customer outcomes. Digital and AI capability – using technology and AI tools responsibly to work smarter and explore new possibilities. Relationship building – collaborating with colleagues, stakeholders and partners to achieve a shared goal. Agile problem-solving – adapting to new information, contributing ideas and helping teams deliver at pace Support to succeed You’ll be backed with: Access to learning academies, Viva Learning and internal platforms.
