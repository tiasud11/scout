Begin your career journey in our Asset Management Chief Operating Office (COO): Join M&G’s eight-week Summer Internship Join us on 28 June 2027 for an eight week internship to gain hands-on experience, develop technical and professional skills, and see how our Asset Management Chief Operating Office (COO) function powers a global investment business from trade execution and client serving to technology, data and AI. Your journey will start with a cohort-wide induction, with focused insight sessions to help you learn more about M&G, network with colleagues, and begin developing your professional skills. You will take part in a structured programme, including: Team Placement: Join one team for eight weeks where you will work as a member of the team with real day-to-day responsibilities, contribute to live projects and learn from experienced colleagues to gain a solid understanding of the COO function within the Asset Management business. Group Project: An opportunity to collaborate with your peers on a live strategic challenge, developing teamwork and problem solving skills and presenting your recommendations back to the business at the end of the programme. Professional Development: Take part in interactive learning sessions and networking opportunities designed to grow your expertise and build your professional network Your Support Network: Regular check-ins with the Early Careers Team, your manager, and a buddy will ensure you have all the support you need during the programme This internship is your first step toward a career in Technology. At the end of the programme, you’ll have the opportunity to apply for one of our Graduate Programme starting in September 2028. What will I be doing? The COO function is central to the success of Asset Management, ensuring our business runs efficiently and effectively. It provides the operational backbone that supports investment activity and delivers exceptional client service, while driving strategic change across the organisation. The COO function spans a diverse range of teams and responsibilities, including: Dealing and Implementation – working with our Front Office teams to ensure accurate and timely trade execution. Operations – including Middle Office and Valuations; managing the full trade lifecycle, client servicing, and operational processes. Supplier Management – overseeing relationships with key third-party providers. Business Risk and Controls Management – keeping our operations resilient and well-controlled. Technology – including Data, AI and Analytics; designing and building the platforms and data capabilities that power the business. An internship in the COO function will give you a unique insight into the inner workings of a global investment firm and the pivotal role we play in driving innovation, optimising processes, and ensuring we deliver the highest standards for our clients. As an intern, you could be involved in a range of activities such as: Vendor and Supplier Management – supporting the commercial management of external vendors and service providers. Product Development Support – working as part of a team to design and deliver solutions for new investment capabilities and product challenges. Risk and Control Frameworks – learning how operational controls are designed and implemented, while partnering with the business to ensure risks are effectively managed. Data, AI and automation – exploring how technology can improve client experience, reporting and efficiency. This is an opportunity to see how strategy and operations come together to keep our business running smoothly and evolving for the future. What are we looking for? We’re seeking candidates who are curious, motivated, and eager to learn, with a genuine interest in building a career in operations, technology or risk within asset management. You should bring a proactive mindset, a willingness to take on challenges, and a passion for understanding how a global investment business operates. Successful candidates will bring: Analytical thinking and problem-solving skills. Strong attention to detail. Strong communication and collaboration skills. Comfort with technology and digital tools, and curiosity about how data and AI can improve the way we work. Ability to build and maintain strong professional relationships across teams and stakeholders. We welcome applicants from all degree disciplines and value diverse perspectives and experiences.
