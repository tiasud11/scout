You'll apply the skills you're developing at university, learn from experienced finance professionals and contribute to a business investing in the UK's energy future. What is the Role? Finance is at the heart of how SSE makes decisions. As we invest in the infrastructure, technology and people needed to support the transition to net zero, our Finance teams provide the insight and expertise that help us understand performance, manage risk and make informed investment decisions. During your summer placement, you'll join one of our finance teams and gain practical experience in a professional working environment. Depending on where you're placed, you could gain exposure to areas such as: Financial Accounting Management Accounting Internal Audit Treasury Corporate Finance Commercial Finance & Business Partnering You'll work alongside experienced colleagues, contribute to meaningful work and develop skills that will support both your remaining university studies and your future career. Most importantly, the placement is an opportunity to explore whether a career in finance is right for you and experience what it's like to work at SSE. What We're Looking For You'll be in your third year of university and returning to university after your summer placement. Your degree doesn't need to be in Finance or Accounting. We welcome students from any degree discipline who can demonstrate an interest in developing their commercial and financial understanding. You'll likely be someone who: Enjoys working with data and information to solve problems. Is curious about how organisations make financial and commercial decisions. Communicates clearly and builds positive working relationships. Is organised, adaptable and keen to learn. Takes ownership and looks for opportunities to contribute. About SSE We’re helping shape the future of electricity. A role with SSE is an opportunity to build a rewarding career and help deliver the clean power and electricity infrastructure needed across the UK and Ireland. We're investing £33bn in critical electricity infrastructure to 2030, including major renewable power projects and the networks needed to connect them. From powering homes and businesses to supporting the electrification of transport, our projects are helping shape the future electricity system We’re accredited by the Living Wage Foundation and hold the Fair Tax Mark.
