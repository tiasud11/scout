Job Type: Internship (1 Month+) Job Roles: Banking Commercial Banking Finance Locations: London Job Description The team: Global Corporate Banking is focused on relationship management of global, large cap global corporate and financial institution clients, managing the day-to-day relationship as well as origination of new business with specific responsibility for delivering financing, treasury and risk management advice and execution to our clients globally. Programme Overview Our Summer Analyst programme is designed to offer you a broad and exciting experience of what it is like to work as a full-time Corporate Banking analyst. During the summer you will be pooled across the three London Corporate Banking teams (UK & Ireland Corporate Coverage, Financial Institutions Coverage and Global Subsidiary Coverage), working across a diverse range of industries and products. Training and Development Your training and development is our top priority with extensive formal training offered at the start of the programme in addition to on-the-job support, educational speaker events and mentorship throughout. Responsibilities: As a summer analyst, your key tasks and responsibilities may include but are not limited to: Assist Global Corporate Banking professionals in relationship management, origination, and execution across a diverse set of product areas (including Loan & Bond financing, Foreign Exchange (FX) and Interest Rate Derivatives, and Cash Management) Gain an in-depth understanding of our clients, the industries in which they operate and how the bank helps support both their growth aspirations and day-to-day banking requirements Provide market-informed judgments on a broad array of financing strategies to address clients' specific needs Help prepare detailed financial models and analytical presentations to provide advice across the capital structure Become proficient at credit analysis and the various metrics used to price and structure various debt instruments Gain familiarity with the legal and documentation aspects of various debt instruments Work on live transactions and interact with clients as you build your network and experience Help to generate ideas and cross selling opportunities for new and existing clients Assist in the development of client specific analysis (e.g., capital structure, acquisition finance) Work closely with Investment Banking, Capital Markets and Global Transaction Services teams on client coverage and origination Eligibility Candidates are required to be pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree from an accredited college or university with a completion time frame between June 2027 and July 2028. Completion means that you have completed all final exams, coursework, and have no outstanding requirements for graduation. Please note that we reserve the right to request evidence of this completion date before extending an offer. On track for a minimum 2:1 degree classification (or equivalent) Applications are accepted from a diverse range of degree disciplines such as the arts and humanities, as well as numerically focused subjects such as economics and mathematics Fluency in English is essential, proficiency in any European language would be highly desirable. Candidates who perform well during the internship may be offered full time employment to commence in July 2028; some lines of business may also offer the opportunity of a January or April start date. You must be available to join on one of these start dates if offered, unfortunately we are unable to offer deferrals. What we are looking for Essential: You will need to demonstrate drive, innovation, and a genuine interest in the financial markets Teamwork, leadership, and problem-solving skills, plus creativity are key qualities, as is the ability to work in a fast-paced environment, multi-task and interact with a variety of people Analytical and problem-solving skills. Influences decisions and outcomes through effective communication and interpersonal skills Integrity and ethics, earning trust and respect through consistent honesty and professionalism in all interactions Salary and Benefits We offer a competitive Salary and Benefits package. Diversity and Inclusion At Bank of America, we are guided by a common purpose to help make financial lives better through the power of every connection. We do this by driving Responsible Growth and delivering for our clients, teammates, communities and shareholders every day. Being a Great Place to Work is core to how we drive Responsible Growth. This includes our commitment to being an inclusive workplace, attracting and developing exceptional talent, supporting our teammates’ physical, emotional, and financial wellness, recognizing and rewarding performance, and how we make an impact in the communities we serve. Bank of America is committed to an in-office culture with specific requirements for office-based attendance and which allows for an appropriate level of flexibility for our teammates and businesses based on role-specific considerations. At Bank of America, you can build a successful career with opportunities to learn, grow, and make an impact. Join us! Please note the maximum number of applications that you can submit per recruitment season is 8. You are only allowed to submit: 3 applications in total to U.S. and Canada programs 2 applications in total to APAC programs 2 applications in total to LATAM programs EMEA applicants are limited to 1 application per recruitment season. Once your application has been submitted, you will be unable to apply to any additional EMEA programs. Our recruitment process Analyst internship and full-time recruiting takes place on a rolling basis once our applications are open.
