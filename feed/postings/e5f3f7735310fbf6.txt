From day one, you'll gain hands-on experience, work alongside experienced professionals, and develop your skills in a supportive and dynamic learning environment. At AAB, we focus on who you are, not just the skills you bring. We're always looking for ambitious, motivated individuals who share our values and want to contribute to our continued growth and success. What can we offer you? We provide excellent career development opportunities, including exposure to specialist service lines and unique career pathways. Our open and collaborative culture encourages you to share ideas, seek and provide feedback, and take an active role in shaping both your career and our business. We're proud of our reputation as a team that works hard and enjoys celebrating success together. Throughout the year, we organise a wide range of social, sporting, and fundraising events, and we encourage everyone to get involved. Our People Our people are at the heart of everything we do. We're committed to nurturing talent and helping our colleagues reach their full potential. Many of our team members begin their careers with us and go on to build long-term, rewarding careers within AAB, helping to shape the future of both our business and the profession.InternshipLooking for a fantastic start to your career? Our paid internship programme provides an excellent opportunity to gain first-hand experience of life at AAB. From day one, you'll be welcomed as a valued member of the team, working alongside experienced professionals and contributing to real client projects. During your internship, you'll gain practical, hands-on experience, develop your commercial awareness, and build valuable professional skills that will support your future career. You'll have the opportunity to work on chargeable client-facing assignments, develop relationships across the business, and gain insight into the wide range of services we provide. Applications for our Internship Programme Opens on 21 September 2026 Opportunities are available across a range of exciting service lines, including: Audit (Aberdeen, Edinburgh, Glasgow, Leeds) Global Mobility Tax (Aberdeen, Edinburgh, Glasgow and London) Tax (Aberdeen and Glasgow) An internship with AAB is also a great way to experience our culture, understand what a career in professional services involves, and explore potential graduate opportunities. Many of our interns go on to apply for graduate roles, giving them a strong foundation and a deeper understanding of our business before starting their careers. Application Deadline November 02, 2026 Department Central Employment Type Internship Location UK Workplace type Onsite Entry Requirements We welcome applications from graduates of all degree disciplines and actively encourage individuals from diverse educational backgrounds to apply. When reviewing applications, we take a holistic approach, focusing on your potential, mindset, and aspirations rather than a specific degree subject. We are looking for individuals who demonstrate: A growth mindset and willingness to learn Strong motivation to develop personally and professionally Excellent communication and interpersonal skills A passion for delivering exceptional client service A commitment to going Above And Beyond for clients and colleagues Application Process Our recruitment process is designed to be straightforward and give us the opportunity to get to know you and your potential. The process consists of: Online application form Job-focused assessment, evaluating behavioural and cognitive strengths Video exercise (your elevator pitch) Interview Job offer We understand that AI is increasingly used and may support candidates during the application process. We encourage you to use it responsibly, ensuring that any information submitted is authentic and accurately represents you and your abilities. Diversity, Inclusion and Accessibility As an equal opportunities employer, we are committed to creating an inclusive environment where everyone can thrive. We welcome applications from candidates of all backgrounds and protected characteristics. We are committed to providing reasonable adjustments throughout the recruitment, assessment, and onboarding process. If you would like to discuss any support or adjustments you may require, please contact HR@aab.uk. Right to Work Please note that, at the time your employment commences, you must have the legal right to work in the UK. Ready to take the next step?
