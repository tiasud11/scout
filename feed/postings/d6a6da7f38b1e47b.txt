That’s why we’ve grouped our graduate opportunities into five broad categories that allow you to explore your options and make an informed choice. Take a look at all of our opportunities. Delve deeper into the different types of experiences and development they offer. Then decide which one you want to apply for. There are no bad choices. Every graduate opportunity at PwC offers the chance for you to build a broad range of business skills, expand your knowledge and develop your career. And every career offers you a rich diversity of possibilities and experiences. That said, finding the right place to start, or the right professional qualifications to study for, can make a big difference to your first few years with us. So take a look and apply to whichever graduate opportunity feels most suited to your interests and skills. Graduates can apply to one of our graduate programmes, in one of five job categories: In Audit, Accounting, Tax and Finance, you could qualify as an Actuary, Accountant, Tax or Finance professional. In Advisory and Consulting , you could help organisations to transform as a consultant or deals advisor. In Legal Services , you could build a broad perspective on the business world and develop your legal knowledge. In Strategy, you could advise and collaborate with clients to set their long-term vision and direction. In Technology and AI, you could design, develop and implement the latest tech. Do I need a specific degree to apply for PwC graduate programmes? We welcome applications from students of all degree disciplines. Some programmes require specific academic backgrounds, but many focus on your skills, strengths and interests rather than your subject. Do I need relevant work experience to apply for a PwC graduate programme? You don’t need prior work experience to apply for a PwC graduate role. The recruitment process is designed to assess your potential, strengths and motivation, rather than what you’ve done before. What is the final degree grade required to join? Most of our graduate programmes require applicants to have achieved a pass in their degree. Some roles may have additional academic requirements, so please check the individual job description carefully before applying. Can I apply to more than one graduate programme? You can only apply to one graduate programme per recruitment year. We encourage you to explore all options carefully and apply to the programme that best matches your interests and strengths. Do PwC graduate programmes offer professional qualifications? Yes. Some graduate programmes include funded professional qualifications alongside on‑the‑job learning, helping you build technical expertise while gaining practical experience. What support is available once I start on a graduate programme? As a graduate, you will have a structured learning pathway, professional qualification support (where applicable), and access Talent Coaches and buddies and communities to help you develop and progress throughout your career at PwC. Do you accept applications from graduates who require work permits? Yes. We welcome applications from candidates who are eligible to switch from a Student visa. However, restrictions may apply for some business areas and office locations, so please visit our immigration authorisation page for more information and to check where restrictions apply. I graduated a few years ago; can I still apply for a graduate position? Yes, we welcome all students from all backgrounds. What’s important to us is your potential and that's why we evaluate your intellect, behaviours, skills and motivations.
